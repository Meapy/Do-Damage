When does the gold arrive?

As soon as PayPal says the payment is complete, usually a second after you pay. If you're in game you'll see a gold +N Gold over your character and a message in chat. A payment PayPal holds back (a bank transfer, a review) is paid in by itself when it clears.

I closed the page before it said thank you

Your gold still arrives: PayPal tells the server about every payment by itself. Sign in here again to see it under Your purchases.

What can I buy with gold?

Character slots for 500 gold each and vault chests for 250 each, always the same price (with fame they double after every 5). Every Elder dungeon's key, sold only for gold, 10 to 100 each, at the Nexus Bazaar's Elder key stalls or /shop elderkeys. Items players list for gold on the Marketplace. The other gold shops are /shop donor, /shop donorpets and /shop america. Everything else sells for fame, which you earn by playing.

Something went wrong

Ask on Discord with the order id from your PayPal receipt or from Your purchases. A refunded or charged-back payment takes its gold back.
