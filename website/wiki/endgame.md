When a realm closes, Oryx runs. On this server you can follow him a long way: from his Castle
through his Chamber, his Wine Cellar, the new Oryx's Sanctuary and the Hash Cellar to the Doomed
Cellar, the end of the line. Every stage is harder than the last and drops better loot, and every
Oryx's death is announced to the whole server.

> **The Doomed Cellar is sealed for now.** Oryx the Mad God 5's loot is being reworked, so a Doomed
> Cellar Incantation won't open his portal until he's back (it isn't used up if you try), and the
> shops don't sell it meanwhile. Keep any you have.

## The stages

{{gen:oryx-chain}}

Health is for one player. Each extra player nearby adds a share of it (25% to 35% of the base, per
player, depending on the stage).

## How the chain works

1. **A realm closes** once enough of its events are beaten: {{v:realm_close}}. Oryx shouts, the realm's
   monsters vanish, and about 30 seconds later everyone still in the realm is sent to a new
   **Oryx's Castle**. You can also open the Castle with an Oryx's Castle Key.
2. **In the Castle**, kill both Stone Guardians. Their death opens the way to **Janus the
   Doorwarden**, who drops the open portal to **Oryx's Chamber**.
3. From then on, **each Oryx leaves two portals** when he dies, two tiles either side of his loot:
   a **Realm Portal** back out (open for two minutes) and a **locked portal** to the next stage
   (open for ten). The locked portal's name says where it leads.
4. **To open a locked portal**, someone uses the right **incantation** within 25 tiles of it, in
   that Oryx's dungeon. It turns into the open portal for five minutes, and the dungeon is told who
   opened it. Entering the locked portal without one tells you which incantation it needs. An
   incantation used anywhere else isn't spent.

Inside a dungeon, the quest arrow always points at Oryx.

## The Chicken Chamber

A side door at stage two. A giant chicken has taken over Oryx's Chamber: fight through a coop full
of chickens and eggs (break the eggs before they hatch, about 7 seconds after you come close), then
the **Giant Oryx Chicken** itself. It molts at two thirds of its health, throws eggs and confusing
star rings, and chases you with its chicks at a third. Like Oryx 1 it leaves a locked Wine Cellar
portal, so a Wine Cellar Incantation takes you on. Its key, the Oryx Chicken Chamber Key, drops from
Janus and is sold in the Nexus Bazaar.

## Tips for each boss

- **Stone Guardians and Janus:** the Guardians fire rings; keep moving around them, not through
  them.
- **Oryx 1** (Chamber) cycles minions, an invulnerable dance with rings and a spiral, his artifacts
  and his gaze. Below 20% he grows and chases you.
- **Oryx 2** (Wine Cellar) calls his guards at 55% health, with silencing and weakening spinners,
  then fights faster.
- **Oryx 3** (Sanctuary): first fight through his nobles and his court (kill the Deacon first: he
  heals the others) until the Sanctum's gate opens. He waits in the middle of the Sanctum, chases
  you while he fires missiles, charges about leaving spinning wheels behind him, and backs away
  while he throws bombs. Before some attacks he turns invulnerable and dashes back to the middle.
  When he shouts **"KNEEL!"**, get more than eight tiles from the middle, or you're stunned. At
  half health his three Protectors circle him and must die before he can be hurt again; after
  that his attacks get stronger, and below 20% he hunts players down.
- **Oryx 4** (Hash Cellar): a burning copy of the Sanctuary. The Burning Path winds through four
  chambers held by his Infernal Court, Oryx 3's court with Oryx 2's minions' tricks and a little
  stronger: the Infernal Officer (confusing shuriken), the Brimstone Major (charges, and a cone of
  fire up close), the Cinder Cleric (heals the others and throws blasters: kill it first) and the
  Doom Cardinal (paralysing spears, and a Vintner at its side). Oryx 4 waits in the middle of the
  Pyre at its end: Oryx 1's fight made harder, with Knights of Doom, six orbiting White Moons and
  a weakening ring he warns you about. Mind the lava pools on the floor.
- **Oryx 5** (Doomed Cellar) can't be hurt until the twenty **Trees of Doom** around him are cut
  down, and the castle's Stone Guardians stand with him. Then he cycles fire, a blade charge, star
  spirals and new Knights and Trees (each living tree heals him), turns faster below 60% and makes
  a last stand below 20%.

## The loot

Loot rises with each stage: more stat potions, more Potions of Life and Mana, and higher-tier gear,
from tier 10-12 at the Castle to tier 14-15 at Oryx 5. Potions of Oryx and the white-bag items are
the real prizes:

- **Every Oryx** always drops a Potion of Attack and of Defense (Oryx 5 Speed and Dexterity too),
  with more stat potions on top: about 4 from Oryx 1 up to about 8 from Oryx 5. Oryx 2 always drops
  a Potion of Life, Oryx 3 and later Life and Mana, and Oryx 5 two of each.
- **Oryx 2** can drop the Doom Bow.
- **Oryx 3, 4 and 5** drop the **Oryx set**: Ring of Oryx, Oryx Hide Armor, Oryx Hide Robe, Oryx
  Scale Armor, Oryx Tooth Sword, Doom Dirk, Flying Doom Daggers and Tome of Noble Assault, in white
  bags announced to everyone, and Potions of Oryx. Oryx 3 can also drop Oryx's Escutcheon.
- **Oryx 4 and 5** drop Doom Poison; **Oryx 5** also the Sword of Dark Enchantment and the Crown.
- **Tier 7 abilities and rings** come from Oryx 3, 4 and 5; Oryx 5 is the only place for tier 8.

Everyone who does a fair share of the damage gets the full loot: 5% of the damage is enough for the
gear and the Oryx set, and 3% for the extra stat potions.
