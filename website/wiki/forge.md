The **Divine Forge** turns items you don't need into ones you do. It knows {{v:forge_recipes}}
recipes: {{v:forge_chains}} of them make any tiered weapon, ability, armor or ring from two of the
tier below, and the rest are special recipes, most of them from Doomed Realms.

## Using the forge

1. Type `/forge`, or cross the east bridge from the spawn into the village: the smithy is north
   of the shopping street. The forge is its great furnace; the Enchanter, which puts imprints on
   gear, is the crystal altar beside it (see [Imprints](imprints.html)).
2. Stand by it: the bottom of your HUD says "The Divine Workbench: forge, sell or imprint". Press
   **Open**. The **Divine Workbench** opens at its **Forge** tab; its **Shards**, **Sell for fame**
   and **Imprints** tabs are the same window's (the Enchanter opens it at Imprints).
3. Browse the recipes by what they make (weapons, abilities, armor, rings, the rest, then by kind),
   narrow them to **UT** (untiered gear, the Ascended items first) or **Highest tier** (the top tier
   the forge makes of each kind), search for one, or turn on **Forge Ready Items** to see only what
   you can make with what you carry and what's in your vault.
4. Pick a recipe. **Insert all** puts its ingredients in from your inventory (forge shards from
   your forge storage), and anything you don't carry from your vault, or click each empty
   ingredient slot to put one in. An ingredient from your vault shows a little vault chest on its
   slot, with how many your vault holds (its tooltip says so too). An item several recipes make is listed once: the forge picks a recipe
   you can make, and says how many there are.
5. Press **Forge**. It takes the fame shown, and the new item takes the first ingredient's place in
   your inventory. If every ingredient came from your vault or forge storage, it goes into a free
   inventory slot, or into your vault when your inventory is full.

Only your own vault chests count.

`/forge` and `/forgelist` only point you to the forge now.

## Two for one: the tier upgrades

Every tiered item can be forged from **two of the tier below**, of the same kind: two tier 10
swords make a tier 11 sword, two Rings of Attack a Ring of Greater Attack (rings go by their stat).
The forge never makes something nothing drops, so the top tiers of some kinds aren't there. The
**tier 16 armours** are the exception: each comes from the tier 15 armour of its kind and an
Ascended Armor Shard, for 1,000 fame (they're under Armor in [Other recipes](#other-recipes)).

The low tiers cost next to nothing (two tier 8 staffs make a tier 9 for 60 fame), so forge what you
find. The **starter gear `/gear` hands out can't go in**: two of a class's starter weapon, ability,
armor or ring don't make the next tier, so a fresh character can't forge its way up from free gear.
Because they cost so little, forging tiered gear under 125 fame (below tier 10 on the weapons'
scale) doesn't count towards quests, the Master Smith achievement or the forge's community goal.

## Forge shards and the forge storage

Oryx the Infernal Mad God (Oryx 4) drops **Infernal shards** and Oryx the Ascended God (Oryx 5)
**Ascended shards**, one of each kind of gear: a Weapon, Ability, Armor and Accessory Shard. Each
Infernal shard drops as often as Oryx 4's Potion of Oryx, each Ascended shard half as often as Oryx
5's.

Use a shard (double-click it, as you would a potion) and it goes into your **forge storage**, which
your whole account shares, wherever you are. The forge's **Shards** tab shows what it holds; click a
shard there to take one back out into your inventory, to trade it. When a recipe needs a shard, the
forge takes it from your storage: you don't need to carry it.

The Infernal shards make the **Infernal potions**: a shard and 100 of a stat's potions from your
potion storage (taken from it as you forge; the recipe shows how many it holds) make an Infernal
potion of that stat. The Weapon shard makes Attack and Dexterity, the Ability shard Mana and
Wisdom, the Armor shard Defense and Vitality, the Accessory shard Life and Speed. What they do:
[Progression](progression.html#stat-potions-and-maxing). The old forge
materials (the Shattered Rune of Oryx, Broken Oryx Armor and Purified Doom Saliva) and the Forge
Amulet are gone: nothing drops them, and the forge neither takes nor buys them.

## The Ascended items

Oryx the Ascended God's own gear, which he never drops: untiered items with a red slot edge, which
the forge makes from an item of the highest tier he drops and an Ascended shard of its kind. Each
weapon and armour comes from its own kind's top item (a tier 15 dagger makes the Enthroned Dagger),
and each has a look of its own (the Enthroned Fists still look like theirs, with purple sparkles). Each has the numbers of the item it's forged from: its
stats, its ability and its shots' damage and what they do (an Enthroned Katana's shots still
pierce, an Enthroned Dual Blades' still ignore defense). All are named **Enthroned**: the Enthroned
Sword, Dagger, Bow, Wand, Katana, Dual Blades, Fists, Hide and Plate, and the **Enthroned Staff**,
from a tier 15 staff (it was the Enthroned Spear, made from his
Elysian Staff). His own pieces: the **Enthroned Robe** from his **Robe of Absolution**, the
**Enthroned Boots** (+50 speed) from any ring and two Ascended Accessory Shards, and the
**Enthroned Ring of Grace** from both of his
gems, the **Ascended Gem of Adoration** and the **Ascended Gem of Tenderness**, and three Ascended
Accessory Shards. The Ring of Grace gives +200 health and magic and +10 to every other stat. Every
Enthroned item gives a **10% fame bonus**.

The forge doesn't make the Enthroned Helm (for now) or the Enthroned Ring of Wrath any more; ones
already made keep working.

Every Enthroned weapon fires in the same pattern as the weapon it's forged from, and its volleys
take turns: first the **weapon's own shot**, with the weapon's own damage and what its shots do,
then Oryx's violet **Rift**, the same but piercing and ignoring defense. The Enthroned Fists fly
his gold **Radiance** in place of a shot of their own.

Dropped from your inventory, an Enthroned item goes into a **purple bag only you can see**, and it
only goes into loot bags that are yours alone.

too (see [Imprints](imprints.html)).

## Other recipes

Recipes that don't follow the two-for-one rule: the Doomed Realms forge's untiered (UT) items,
other ways to the same tiered items, a few potions, and items no monster drops, which only the forge
makes: the **Reforged Forgotten Crown** (from two Forgotten Crowns), the **Reforged Dirk of Cronus**
(two Dirks of Cronus), the **Reforged Doom Bow** (two Doom Bows) and the **Dagger of the Terrible
Talon** (two Obsidian Daggers). Some items share their numbers and differ only in looks, and the
forge turns one into the other for 100 fame, either way: the **Dirk of Cronus** and the **Phantasm
Dirk**; the **Doom Bow**, **Sun's Judgement** and **Bow of the Morning Star**; the **Thousand Shot**
and the **Sunny Side Bow**; the **Staff of the Cosmic Whole** and **Frosty's Walking Stick**; the
**Staff of Extreme Prejudice** and the **Staff of Iceblast**; the **Wand of the Bulwark** and the
**Eternal Snowflake Wand**; the **Wand of Evocation** and **Winter's Breath Wand**; the **Demon Blade**
and the **Sword of Illumination**; the **Helm of the Juggernaut** and the **Helm of the
Jack-o'-naut**. The **Prismatic Crystal Wand** is forged from two Crystal Wands, and the
**Penetrating Blast Spell** from two Elemental Detonation Spells.
No recipe for an untiered item costs more than 3,000 fame. Click an item for what it does.

## Selling for fame

The forge's **Sell for fame** tab buys items: it shows your inventory with what the forge pays for
each one under it. Pick the items and sell them. `/sellfame` points you there. To sell to other
players instead, use the [Marketplace](marketplace.html).
