The **Divine Forge** turns items you don't need into ones you do. It knows {{v:forge_recipes}}
recipes: {{v:forge_chains}} of them make any tiered weapon, ability, armor or ring from two of the
tier below, and the rest are special recipes, most of them from Doomed Realms.

## Using the forge

1. Go to the Nexus's south yard, west of the arena portals. The forge stands in the middle of the
   class memorial, on a floor of gold.
2. Stand by it: the bottom of your HUD says "Forge items, or sell them for fame". Press **Open**.
3. Browse the recipes by what they make (weapons, abilities, armor, rings, the rest, then by kind),
   narrow them to **UT** (untiered gear, the Ascended items first) or **Highest tier** (the top tier
   the forge makes of each kind), search for one, or turn on **Forge Ready Items** to see only what
   you can make with what you carry.
4. Pick a recipe. **Insert all** puts its ingredients in from your inventory (and forge shards from
   your forge storage), or click each empty ingredient slot to put one in. An item several recipes
   make is listed once: the forge picks a recipe you can make, and says how many there are.
5. Press **Forge**. It takes the fame shown, and the new item takes the first ingredient's place in
   your inventory.

`/forge` and `/forgelist` only point you to the forge now.

## Two for one: the tier upgrades

Every tiered item can be forged from **two of the tier below**, of the same kind: two tier 10
swords make a tier 11 sword, two Rings of Attack a Ring of Greater Attack (rings go by their stat).
The forge never makes something nothing drops, so the top tiers of some kinds aren't there.

{{gen:forge_example}}

What an upgrade costs, by the tier it makes (a dash: nothing to forge at that tier). No tier upgrade
costs more than {{v:forge_cap}} fame.

{{gen:forge_tiers}}

> Weapons and armor start at tier 0 and 1, so the first upgrade makes a tier 1 weapon or a tier 2
> armor. Every ability and ring costs the same at each tier. A few weapon and armor tiers keep the
> Doomed Realms forge's own prices, which is why daggers, wands and the armors differ a little.

## Forge shards and the forge storage

Oryx the Infernal Mad God (Oryx 4) drops **Infernal shards** and Oryx the Ascended God (Oryx 5)
**Ascended shards**, one of each kind of gear: a Weapon, Ability, Armor and Accessory Shard. Each
drops half as often as that Oryx's Potion of Oryx.

Use a shard (double-click it, as you would a potion) and it goes into your **forge storage**, which
your whole account shares, wherever you are. The forge's **Shards** tab shows what it holds; click a
shard there to take one back out into your inventory, to trade it. When a recipe needs a shard, the
forge takes it from your storage: you don't need to carry it.

The Infernal shards don't make anything yet: their recipes come in a later update. The old forge
materials (the Shattered Rune of Oryx, Broken Oryx Armor and Purified Doom Saliva) and the Forge
Amulet are gone: nothing drops them, and the forge neither takes nor buys them.

## The Ascended items

Oryx the Ascended God's own gear, which he never drops: untiered items with a red slot edge, which
the forge makes from an item of the highest tier he drops and an Ascended shard of its kind. Each
weapon and armour comes from its own kind's top item (a tier 15 dagger makes the Enthroned Dagger)
and looks like it, with purple sparkles. All are named **Enthroned**: the Enthroned Sword, Dagger,
Bow, Wand, Katana, Dual Blades, Fists, Hide and Plate. His own pieces: the **Enthroned Spear**, a
staff drawn after the one he holds, from his **Elysian Staff**; the **Enthroned Robe** from any tier
16 armour, the **Enthroned Helm** from any tier 8 ability, and the **Enthroned Boots** and the
**Enthroned Rings of Wrath and Grace** from any tier 8 ring.

> These are **placeholders** with no numbers yet: their weapons hit for 0, and they give no stats or
> ability effects. They'll get their own numbers later.

The **Enchant** tab beside Forge is locked: enchanting comes in a later update.

## Other recipes

Recipes that don't follow the two-for-one rule: the Doomed Realms forge's untiered (UT) items,
other ways to the same tiered items, a few potions, and items no monster drops, which only the forge
makes: the **Reforged Forgotten Crown** (from two Forgotten Crowns), the **Reforged Dirk of Cronus**
(two Dirks of Cronus), the **Reforged Doom Bow** (two Doom Bows) and the **Dagger of the Terrible
Talon** (two Obsidian Daggers). Some items share their numbers and differ only in looks, and the
forge turns one into the other for 100 fame, either way: the **Dirk of Cronus** and the **Phantasm
Dirk**; the **Doom Bow**, **Sun's Judgement** and **Bow of the Morning Star**; the **Thousand Shot**
and the **Sunny Side Bow**; the **Staff of the Cosmic Whole** and **Frosty's Walking Stick**; the
**Staff of Extreme Prejudice** and the **Staff of Iceblast**; the **Wand of the Bulwark** and the
**Eternal Snowflake Wand**; the **Wand of Evocation** and **Winter's Breath Wand**; the **Demon Blade**
and the **Sword of Illumination**; the **Helm of the Juggernaut** and the **Helm of the
Jack-o'-naut**. The **Prismatic Crystal Wand** is forged from two Crystal Wands, and the
**Penetrating Blast Spell** from two Elemental Detonation Spells.
No recipe for an untiered item costs more than 3,000 fame. Click an item for what it does.

## Selling for fame

The forge's **Sell for fame** tab buys items: it shows your inventory with what the forge pays for
each one under it. Pick the items and sell them. `/sellfame` points you there. To sell to other
players instead, use the marketplace (see [Items](items.html#trading-and-the-marketplace)).
