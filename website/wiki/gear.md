Every piece of gear in the game has a place here, read from the game's own data, so the lists stay
true as items are added. Each kind of item has its own page, with tabs for the other kinds; each
class has one page with a tab each for the weapons, abilities, armor and rings it can use.

On every list, items are grouped by what they are, as the edge of their slot in your inventory
shows it ([Items](items.html#tiers-and-their-colours)): **tiered** items by tier, then **untiered
(UT)** items, the **future additions** (untiered items nothing drops, sells or forges yet), **set
pieces (ST)**, which can't be found for now either, the rare **Legendary**, **Ascended** and
**Ethereal** items, the staff's **rewards**, and last the **admin** and test items, which are for
staff. The rings have no Legendary, Ascended or Ethereal items for now: those are to be reworked.
Each item says where it comes from: the bosses that drop it, which tier drops it comes in, the
shops that sell it and the Divine Forge's recipe for it.
