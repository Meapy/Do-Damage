Every piece of gear in the game has a place here, read from the game's own data, so the lists stay
true as items are added. Each kind of item has its own page, with tabs for the other kinds; each
class has one page with a tab each for the weapons, abilities, armor and rings it can use.

On every list, items are grouped by what they are, as the edge of their slot in your inventory
shows it ([Items](items.html#tiers-and-their-colours)): **tiered** items by tier, then **untiered
(UT)** items, **set pieces (ST)**, the rare **Legendary**, **Ascended** and **Ethereal** items, and
last the **admin** and test items, which are for staff. Each item says where it comes from: the
bosses that drop it, which tier drops it comes in, and the shops that sell it.
