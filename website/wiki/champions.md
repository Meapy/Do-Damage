The **Legends** remember the dead. The **Hall of Champions** honours the living: nine boards, each
ranking every account (or guild) by one thing, and each giving its **top 10** a reward of its own while they
hold the place. Lose the place and the reward goes; win it back and it returns.

## Finding it

The Hall stands in the west of the Nexus: cross the wooden bridge west from the spawn and follow
the road to a round court of stone ringed with dark wood. In its middle is the
**Champions Monument**. Stand by it and press **View** (or <kbd>0</kbd>) to open the boards. Around
it, each board's **#1 stands on a plinth** as their character, under a plaque with the board, their
name and their score. Standing by a statue and pressing View opens the Hall at that statue's board.

`/champions` lists every board's top three and your own places in the chat, from anywhere.

## The boards and their rewards

The rewards come in three steps: **#1**, **#2-3** and **#4-10**.

| Board | Ranks | #1 | #2-3 | #4-10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fame** | the most fame on a living character (each account's best) | +3% loot | +2% loot | +1% loot |
| **Stars** | class stars: up to five per class, from its best character's fame | gold glow | silver glow | bronze glow |
| **Achievements** | achievement points, from the tiers claimed in the [quest log](quests.html) | gold name in chat | silver name in chat | bronze name in chat |
| **Arena Solo** | your best [Arena](progression.html#the-arena) run on your own: its wave, then the quicker run | +15% fame from Arena waves | +10% | +5% |
| **Arena Group** | the best Arena runs with others: each place is a **whole group** (the same players, its best run), and everyone in it holds the place | +15% fame from Arena waves | +10% | +5% |
| **Weekly Fame** | fame earned this week, on all your characters together | +10% experience and fame | +6% | +3% |
| **Weekly Dungeons** | dungeons cleared this week (be near a boss when the last one falls) | +3% loot in dungeons | +2% | +1% |
| **Guild Living** | a [guild](guilds.html)'s living fame: its members' living characters, counting members who played in the last two weeks | +2% loot for every member | +1% | +0.5% |
| **Guild Dead** | a guild's dead fame: every character that died in it this year (afresh each 1 January, UTC) | +2% loot for every member | +1% | +0.5% |

- **Loot:** the boards' loot boosts add up to **at most +4%** (Fame's, the guild boards' and, in a
  dungeon, Weekly Dungeons' together). The Legends' +1% and a guild hall's own (1% to 3%) come on top of that.
  "In dungeons" means anywhere but the realms.
- **Guilds:** each place on the guild boards is a whole guild, shown as its best member's character,
  and every member (staff aside) holds the reward.
- **Groups:** a group only counts while all its players are ranked (not guests or staff). You can
  be in more than one group on the board; your best place is the one that counts. Its statue shows
  the whole group.
- **Arena fame:** the two Arena boards' rewards add up, so #1 on both gives +30% fame from Arena
  waves.
- **Experience** at level 20 turns into fame, so Weekly Fame's reward is more fame too.
- **Glow and name colour:** a glow you chose yourself (`/glow`) shows instead of the Stars glow, and
  the Legends' red glow gives way to it. The Achievements name colour shows over the rank colour you
  picked in the quest log, which comes back when the place is lost.
- `/boosts` lists every reward you hold.

## Weekly boards

**Weekly Fame** and **Weekly Dungeons** start afresh every **Monday at midnight UTC**, as the weekly
quests do; the board shows how long is left. So a new player can top them in their first week.
**Guild Dead** starts afresh every **1 January** at midnight UTC.

## When it changes

The boards are ranked again every **5 minutes**. When a ranking moves you, the game tells you in the
chat ("You're #2 on the Weekly Fame board…", or that a reward has ended) and the reward starts or
stops within a minute or so, wherever you are. Ties go to the higher tiebreak (for Stars, your
classes' best fame together; for the Arena, the quicker run), then to the older account. Guests
and staff aren't ranked.

## The board

The monument's window has a tab for each board (your own place shows on its tab while you hold a
reward). Each tab says what it ranks and what each step gives, shows its top three on a podium with
their characters, lists #4 to #10, and ends with **your own place**, even far below the top 10, and
how much you'd need to reach #10.
