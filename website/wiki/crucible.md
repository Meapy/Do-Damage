> **In short:**
> - Five floors, five boss fights, no waves of monsters: up to four of you climb together.
> - Open to anyone who reached **round 50 of the Arena**, or has a **Gilded Realm Pass**.
> - **One climb a day** (two with the Gilded Pass). Type `/crucible`, or take the portal in the Nexus's shrine.
> - Bring friends: each extra player adds only about a sixth to a champion's health.

The **Crucible** is a tower of nothing but bosses. You climb it floor by floor, up to four of you
together, and on each floor a champion arrives to stop you. There are no waves of monsters. Each
champion fights in phases, and the floor fights with them: blades sweep the ground, the tide rises,
lava runs through the cracks, the clock strikes. Beat all five and the Crucible is conquered.

## Getting in

- **Who:** anyone who has reached **round 50 of the Arena** (in any season, solo or in a group), or
  anyone holding a **Gilded Realm Pass**. The Arena rounds you reached before the Crucible came
  out count too.
- **How often:** **one climb a day**, or **two** with a Gilded Realm Pass. A new day starts at
  00:00 UTC. A climb counts once it begins, so walking into the lobby and leaving costs nothing.
- **Where:** its portal is sunk into the floor of the Nexus's shrine, across Realm 2's vortex
  from the Arena's pit. You can also type `/crucible` from
  anywhere. `/crucible status` tells you whether it's open to you, how many climbs you have left
  today and the three best climbs.

Before the portal lets you in, it reminds you of the above and asks whether to climb.

The climb begins a minute after the first climber arrives in the lobby (the Threshold). Anyone in
the lobby can say **GO** to start it sooner. Once it begins, nobody else can join: not through
the portal, `/crucible`, `/visit`, `/summon`, `/help` or the Scrying Glass. (An admin can still
summon someone back in, for instance after they lost their connection mid-fight.) A lobby holds four;
the next to come in starts a lobby of their own.

**Bring friends.** The champions are made for a group of up to four. Each player after the first adds
only about a sixth to a champion's health, so a full group has far more damage for each point of
health than someone climbing alone.

## How a climb goes

1. **The way up opens.** You climb the Crucible on foot. A path builds itself out of the sky
   ahead of whoever is furthest along it, winding up to the next floor, made of that floor's
   stone with its pillars along the sides. As you get near, the next floor comes together round
   where the path meets it. Behind the last of you, the path breaks away and falls, and once
   everyone is on it, so does the floor you left. Nothing can hurt you on the way up.
2. Once everyone is on the new floor, its title comes up and the champion arrives in the middle.
   Anyone who lags behind for 20 seconds after the first arrives is brought up.
3. It fights in **phases**, each called out on screen as it begins. The floor changes with them.
4. When it falls, you are healed and the **Ascension Sigil** turns in the middle. A few seconds
   later the next way up opens. Take your time with the loot: the floor stays until everyone has
   left it.

The floors float in open air, so your shots (and the champions') fly on over the gaps in a floor
and the sky beside it instead of stopping at the edge.

Your pet climbs with you, and so do allies you've summoned (a turret, a pet from an item): they
come up to each floor beside you.

Falling in the Crucible doesn't kill you: you're sent back to the Nexus, and the others climb on
without you. There is no fame for a climb. The reward is the champions' loot.

## The champions

Every floor has a shape of its own, and each champion's aura grows as you wear it down: more of
its floor joins in once a third of its health is gone, and more again at two thirds.

The floors are big, with room to move round the champions. As each phase begins a floor reshapes
itself: all the ground that will go cracks at once, then falls into the sky in a wave out from
the middle, new ground rises out of it, and pillars rise and fall with it. Most shapes then keep
turning until the next phase (the gear, the star fort, the wheel of isles, the coral arms, the
grate, the anvil, the crucible, the hourglass, the coil, the Apex's rings, shards and halo), a step
every few seconds and still in between: what is about to fall cracks first, then the floor shifts
at once. Lockdown's
towers, the petals, the Apex's fortress and its cross hold still. Anyone left standing where the ground falls, or cut off from the rest
of the floor, is thrown back onto it (with a light hit), so watch for the cracks.

The real danger is the champions' shots: each phase has patterns of its own to read and slip
through. The floor adds at most one hazard at a time (two only at Chronarch's zero hour), quick
but always shown before it strikes.

Every champion makes a **last stand** at the end of its health (12%, Vaelith at 8%): a moment
untouchable while its floor changes one last time, then a phase of its own, harder than any before
it.

The shots aimed at you hit hard, about as hard as Oryx's last form, and carry each champion's
afflictions; dodge them rather than stand in them. The big rings and spirals are lighter and carry
nothing: they're there to weave through. Each champion also has a signature shot, slower and
heavier, a **wall**: a line of shots side by side aimed at you, flying as one, that you get
out of the way of rather than stand still between. Each champion's last phase throws shots where
you're going, so keep moving. Each champion's shots look like its own (iron, the sea, the melt,
clockwork, gold and flame), the ones aimed at you leave a trail of sparks in its colour, and some
don't fly straight: **thrown hammers** and **rewinding blades** come back the way they went, the
**tide's crests** wash back too, **meteor fragments** weave as they burn and **halo shards** loop
round where they were thrown. Whenever a champion rises into a new phase it throws out two rings,
the second in the first's gaps.

| Champion | Its shots | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| Ferrogrim | Bleeding shard fans; rings of hot rivets; hammer waves that break armour and daze; thrown hammers that spin out and back, breaking armour and stunning | **Rending Volley:** a quick tight spray of red-hot splinters, bleeding for 3 s |
| Nerissa | Barbed tridents (bleeding), the quick violet riptide tridents confusing; slowing orbs and bubbles; crests of the tide that roll out and back (slowing) | **Undertow:** three great slow whirlpools that quiet you and pull you under (paralyzed) |
| Pyrrhax | Magma that leaves you weak; cinders that make you sick (no healing); rings of embers, spirals of molten drops; meteor fragments that weave as they burn (sickening) | **Slag Ball:** three heavy slag boulders that paralyze and break armour |
| Chronarch | Hand-blades (bleeding); chrono orbs that slow and daze; rings of brass gears and steel cogs; blades that rewind back to him (slowing) | **Stolen Moment:** two quick pocket watches that silence you and freeze you in place |
| Vaelith | Lances that leave you exposed; void embers that blind, break armour and curse; gilded quills that quiet; halo shards looping round where he stood | **Final Decree:** three huge slow winged lances through armour that stun and break it |

Their helpers carry them too: the Sentinels' shards cut, the Banners' break armour, the eels'
bubbles quiet, the golems' cinders sicken, the sentries' gears unsettle, and the echoes bring
their champions' with them.

Every champion also sends **rushers** out round its floor now and then, a few at a time: small,
quick and frail, they run straight at you and bite up close with their champion's affliction, so
shoot them on the way in. Ferrogrim's Iron Hounds (bleeding), Nerissa's Brine Darters (slowing),
Pyrrhax's Cinder Imps (sickening), Chronarch's Clockwork Mites (dazing) and Vaelith's Gilded Wisps
(quieting).

### The tower's caches

The Crucible fights on your side too. While a champion can be hurt, the tower drops a **cache**
out of the sky beside one of you every 18 seconds or so (the first 10 seconds into the fight, two
at a time with three or more climbing, three on the floor at most). A shaft of light marks where
it lands, always within a few steps of a climber and well away from the champion, so melee classes
can reach it as easily as anyone. **Break it** (shots, blades, abilities, pets and turrets all
count) and its charge launches at the champion, arcing over the floor and homing on it wherever it
goes. Where it lands, the champion loses a share of its health, past its defense and credited to
whoever broke the cache, and takes the cache's affliction, which breaks through the champions'
immunities:

| Cache | Toughness | Its blow | Its affliction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Powder Keg** | 4,500 | 3.5% of the champion's health, a fireball | none: the biggest plain blow |
| **Stormglass Jar** | 1,800 | 1%, lightning out of the sky | **Stunned** 2.5 s: it can't shoot |
| **Frost Cask** | 2,800 | 1.2%, a burst of frost | **Paralyzed** 3 s, then **Slowed** 6 s |
| **Rending Crate** | 5,500 | 1.5%, iron shards | **Armor Broken** 6 s: everyone hits it harder |
| **Hex Urn** | 2,400 | 1%, a hex | **Cursed** 6 s (takes a quarter more damage) and **Dazed** 5 s (fires half its shots) |
| **Gilded Reliquary** (rare) | 8,000 | 5%, a column of gold | **Stunned** 1.5 s, **Armor Broken** and **Cursed** 8 s |

The jars and urns break in a few blows even from a slow weapon; the kegs and crates take a
group's fire for a moment. A cache has no defense, never moves and never fights back. While its
champion is untouchable (a phase turning, Lockdown, the moment before a last stand) a shield holds
over its caches too, so no charge is wasted; it lifts with the champion's. A cache crumbles if
it's left for 20 seconds (it blinks faster in its last few), if the floor falls from under it, or
if its champion falls first.

When a champion falls over the sky (beside an edge, or over a gap one of its phases opened), a
landing of the floor's own ground rises under its loot, with a bridge back to the floor.

### I. The Iron Bastion: Ferrogrim, the Iron Warden

A keep: square, with a round bastion at each corner and a gate in each side.

A horned juggernaut in riveted iron, a furnace burning in his chest, a tower shield and a molten
hammer.

- **Shield Charge:** he braces behind his shield with a flash, then rams at you; where he stops,
  a double ring of shards bursts out, rivets flying off him as he runs. Get out of his line while
  he braces.
- **Hammerfall:** he slams his hammer and two rings of fire race out across the floor, each
  with three wide gaps. He throws a cross of hammer waves, turned with each blow, and rings of
  shards twice, the second in the first's gaps.
- **Lockdown** (70%): the yard falls in, leaving a tower at each corner and the walls between
  them, and his dais in the middle with a gate of iron grates across each drawbridge. An Iron
  Sentinel holds each tower; he can't be hurt until they're broken (or 30 seconds pass). A
  four-armed spiral of shards turns out from him over the yard, his anvil's cross of hammer
  waves cuts through it, and he throws his hammer over the walls at you, spinning back to him.
- **Grinding Blades:** the keep becomes a great iron gear, ten teeth and five holes, and two
  blades of fire sweep round it from its middle. He leaps on you often, and every so often slams
  the floor again: the double ring, the cross and two hammers thrown wide of you, coming back.
- **Iron Tempest** (35%): a star fort, his keep in a dry moat crossed at the gates and a bastion
  pointing out from each corner. A banner on each bastion guards him and his hammer whirls,
  dragging two blades of fire round the fort the other way, while two spirals of shards turning
  opposite ways weave a lattice round him and he throws three hammers at a time at you.
- **Last Bastion** (12%): the fort falls back to its inner keep, square, a tower at each corner,
  and he holds its middle. Its walls close in on him one after another, molten, each with two
  gates on opposite sides that keep their place as the wall closes: slip out through a gate, or
  be caught inside with him, his anvil's cross and his double rings.

### II. The Drowned Sanctum: Nerissa, the Tidecaller

An atoll with a ragged coast and tide pools, five coral isles across it.

A sea-queen with a serpent's tail, a coral crown and a golden trident.

- **Tidal Lanes:** a band of surging tide rolls across the floor, shown first at its edge.
  Step through one of its two wide gaps. She throws rings of swaying orbs, each turned half a gap,
  and crests of the tide that roll out and wash back.
- **Rising Tide** (66%): the atoll sinks to a wheel: five dry isles, one in the middle and four on
  the rim, a ring of deep sea round it and channels out to them, Riptide Eels hunting in the deep.
  Get to an isle and hold out for 20 seconds against her flower of orbs, arms of bubbles, crests
  of the tide and now and then an undertow.
- **Whirlpool:** the floor becomes three arms of coral round her, two curved arms of tide wheel
  across them, her bubbles spiral out the same way and she throws three crests at you.
- **Maelstrom** (33%): a ring of coral round a vortex, an islet in its eye reached by three
  shallows. The tide's arms turn the other way, and two spirals of bubbles cross round her.
- **Tsunami** (12%): a lagoon, her islet in the middle of deep water crossed by four shallows, a
  ring of reef round it. Great waves roll over the whole floor one after another, each from a new
  side, with a single dry lane through it, shown first at its edge: find the lane. Two eels hunt
  the lagoon, and she rings out tide orbs from her islet and a ring of crests that rolls out over
  the lagoon and back.

### III. The Ember Foundry: Pyrrhax, the Forge Tyrant

A forge hall in a cross, four arms out from a square crucible, slag channels down the arms.

A horned demon of rock and fire with wings of ash and a hammer of lava.

- **Lava Veins:** two veins of lava race open across the floor at a time, glowing for a moment
  first, then burning, then cooling to slag. Now and then he throws a fan of meteor fragments.
- **Meteor Storm** (70%): the floor becomes an iron grate over the sky, furnaces floating off it,
  meteors fall round him, each marked first, while Molten Golems join the fight and he erupts in
  rings of slow magma, a turning star of meteor fragments and slag boulders at you.
- **Hammer:** an anvil, its horn out to the west and the hardy hole through it. His hammer falls
  where each of you stands, marked first; he throws walls of cinders in a cross that turns with
  each throw, and leaps on you.
- **The Crucible Pours** (35%): the forge falls away but for its crucible and spout, plates of it
  drifting round, lava pouring in from the edge and embers raining over what's left, while two
  arms of molten drops pour off him and fragments come at you.
- **Molten Core:** his body goes molten, a meteor falls on each of you now and then, and a
  spiral of magma turns out from him.
- **Molten Sea** (12%): the crucible fills. Everything but an island of floor, rimmed with slag,
  glows, then floods with molten metal for six seconds while the island drifts across it: get
  onto it and stay on it. It drains, then fills again with the island somewhere else. He stands
  in the melt, throwing magma and slag at you.

### IV. The Clockwork Orrery: Chronarch, the Hour Thief

A clock face: three rings over open sky, joined by spokes, a mark at each hour round the dial and
two hands at the hub. Watch where you step when the clock strikes.

A hooded thief of time with a clock for a face and an hourglass for a heart. Four arms carry
blades made of a clock's hands, and an orrery's ring wheels round him.

- **Hour Hands:** the clock's two hands sweep round the floor and burn whoever they cross: the
  broad hour hand, and the narrower minute hand a little over twice as fast. He throws blades
  that run out and rewind back to him.
- **Midnight Chime** (70%): the dial splits into twelve petals, one for each hour, and the hours
  light up in turn, half the floor at a time, with a rest between. Stand on a petal that isn't lit
  when it strikes, while a clock hand of blades sweeps round him and rewinding blades come at you.
- **Time Fracture:** an hourglass. Six Hour Echoes of him keep the broken clock's hours: before
  each strike, each blinks onto an hour, throws a bolt down it and lights it, the warning spreading
  out from where it stands. Half the hours burn at a time, nearly three seconds after they're lit,
  so get off them. The echoes can't be hurt and never shoot; Chronarch blinks round it (a cyan
  ring closing in on the floor shows where, a moment before he lands), shooting
  less than in his other phases (but a ring of rewinding blades now and then), and sends no
  rushers.
- **Reversal** (35%): a coil winding out from the hub to the rim; the clock turns magenta and runs
  backwards, both hands with it and faster, two hands of gears sweep backwards round him and a
  ring of blades rewinds back to him.
- **Zero Hour** (12%): the dial splits into its twelve petals again and counts down: every third
  hour burns at once, the next three a step round each time, while both hands sweep slowly round
  over them. He blinks about (each time marked first, as in Time Fracture), ringing out the hours
  in gears and throwing rewinding blades.

### V. The Apex: Vaelith, the Crucible Sovereign

The biggest floor: a star of marble with eight points, the tower's sigil inlaid in gold. It
doesn't stay a star. With each phase it reshapes itself in a wave out from its middle: ground that
goes cracks first and falls into the sky, new ground rises out of it, and anyone left standing where
it falls is thrown onto what's left (and hurt). Vaelith too: he never stays over the sky.

The tower's maker: a crowned champion in white and gold with wings of fire and a ring of gold
set with the four floors' gems.

- **Sovereign's Decree:** stars fall on each of you, and halo shards loop round where he stands:
  don't follow him in close.
- **The Echoes** (80%, 65%, 50%, 35%): one by one he raises the ghosts of the four champions
  below. Each remakes the Apex in the shape of its floor and brings back one of its hazards (two
  blades, a sweep of tide, lava veins, the clock's two hands), while he turns out rings of stars:
  - **Iron:** a fortress, a square keep with a round bastion at each corner, a gate in each side,
    four pits in its yard and iron pillars at the gates and bastions.
  - **Tide:** an archipelago, a middle isle and six round it in a deep sea that slows you and wears
    you down, shallows running out to the isles.
  - **Forge:** a forge hall in a cross, four long arms over open sky, slag down them, furnaces
    floating off its corners.
  - **Hours:** an orrery, a hub and two rings over the sky, joined by spokes.
- **The Crucible Breaks** (20%): the Apex shatters. Only a disc of its middle is left, with its
  shards floating round it, all four echoes return at once and flame feathers bloom out of him,
  four arms of stars turning through them, quills at you and halo shards looping round him.
- **Unbound:** the Apex re-forms as a halo of void marble round the void itself, rays of gold
  across it, Vaelith coming after you round the halo, his wings turned to the void. Two slow arms of
  flame turn across it, and he fires double rings of void embers.
- **Final Aria** (8%): he sings the tower down. Three arms of flame turn the other way round the
  halo, gold falls on each of you, marked first, and he throws a crown of stars, four arms
  spiralling out, with his lances and his halo shards through it.

## The white bags

Each champion has a **1% chance** to drop a white bag with its own ability item, each for a
different class. Each one does what its class's ability does, then adds something of its
champion's:

| Champion | Item | Class | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ferrogrim | Ferrogrim's Bastion Turret | Mechanic | A turret of Ferrogrim's iron, thrown where you aim like the Mechanic's other turrets (up to three out, faster with you near, wearing down unless allies heal it, a blast when its health runs out). It fires three shards at a time, and every 3 seconds it slams the ground: 380 damage round it, enemies' armor broken, allies beside it Armored. |
| Nerissa | Nerissa's Tidepearl | Druid | A Druid's pearl, above the T8: 420 where it lands, a tide pool reaching 3.5 tiles round it for 5 seconds (130 a pulse past defense, mending allies 22), 650 to every enemy on its way home and a 260 heal (more for each enemy struck), three out at once. Where it lands, three waves of the tide roll out: 220 damage to enemies, and 60 health to allies, each. |
| Pyrrhax | Pyrrhax's Meteor Mortar | Pirate | A shell that arcs to where you aim and bursts (750, paralyzed 1.5 s). Then four meteors fall round the crater, and its slag burns on for 3 seconds. |
| Chronarch | Chronarch's Coat of Stolen Hours | Blademaster | Armored for your allies and Damaging for you, as a jacket gives. Then time stops round you: enemies slowed and you Speedy, while a clock hand sweeps twice round you (320 each sweep). |
| Vaelith | Vaelith's Final Aria | Bard | Inspired for your allies, then four verses ring out from you, each wider and harder (250, 320, 400). The last is a crescendo (700) that breaks armor and sends your allies berserk. |

Each item's page under [Gear](gear.html) has its numbers.

## The champions' pets

Each champion also has a **1% chance** to drop a little copy of itself as a pet, in a white bag of
its own: Lil' Ferrogrim, Lil' Nerissa, Lil' Pyrrhax, Lil' Chronarch and Lil' Vaelith. Each fights
as its champion does, with its shots in its patterns, and every 10 seconds it takes on its
champion's last form (Iron Tempest, Maelstrom, Molten Core, Reversal, Unbound) and throws out its
spiral. They hit as hard as the Lil' Oryxes (about 1,100 for Ferrogrim's up to 2,000 for
Vaelith's a second, at their last level), and each helps you in its champion's way. [Pets](pets.html) has their numbers.

## The leaderboard

The **Crucible Leaderboard** trophy stands in the Arena court, west of the samurai statue. Its
sign shows the best solo climb and the best group climb; press **View** for the top ten of each.
Climbs rank by the **floors cleared**, then the **quicker time** to the last of them. Solo and
group climbs are kept apart (a climb begun by a group stays a group's, however many are left).

- **Who is on it:** only those there when the climb's last floor fell who never fell themselves.
  Fall on the way and that climb isn't yours, even if the others go on.
- **What counts:** a climb that cleared at least one floor. A climb with an admin in it doesn't count.
- The board began again with the 2.6.5 update: climbs from before aren't on it.
- `/crucible status` shows the top three, and the realm is told when a climb takes first place,
  solo or group.

The [Hall of Champions](champions.html) ranks the same climbs on its **Crucible Solo** and
**Crucible Group** boards, whose top ten get more loot in the Crucible.
