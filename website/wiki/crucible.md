The **Crucible** is a tower of nothing but bosses. You climb it floor by floor, up to six of you
together, and on each floor a champion arrives to stop you. There are no waves of monsters. Each
champion fights in phases, and the floor fights with them: blades sweep the ground, the tide rises,
lava runs through the cracks, the clock strikes. Beat all five and the Crucible is conquered.

## Getting in

- **Who:** anyone who has reached **round 50 of the Arena** (in any season, solo or in a group), or
  anyone holding a **Gilded Realm Pass**. The Arena rounds you reached before the Crucible came
  out count too.
- **How often:** **one climb a day**, or **two** with a Gilded Realm Pass. A new day starts at
  00:00 UTC. A climb counts once it begins, so walking into the lobby and leaving costs nothing.
- **Where:** its portal stands in the Nexus beside the Arena's. You can also type `/crucible` from
  anywhere. `/crucible status` tells you whether it's open to you, how many climbs you have left
  today and the three best climbs.

The climb begins a minute after the first climber arrives in the lobby (the Threshold). Anyone in
the lobby can say **GO** to start it sooner. Once it begins, nobody else can join.

## How a climb goes

1. The floor rises: the screen rushes up through the sky and you land on the next floor.
2. The champion's title comes up, and it arrives in the middle of the floor.
3. It fights in **phases**, each called out on screen as it begins. The floor changes with them.
4. When it falls, you are healed and safe for **20 seconds**. The **Ascension Sigil** turns in the
   middle (say GO to go on sooner). Then the floor's edge breaks away into the sky and up you go.

Falling in the Crucible doesn't kill you: you're sent back to the Nexus, and the others climb on
without you. There is no fame for a climb. The reward is the champions' loot.

## The champions

### I. The Iron Bastion: Ferrogrim, the Iron Warden

A horned juggernaut in riveted iron, a furnace burning in his chest, a tower shield and a molten
hammer.

- **Hammerfall:** he slams his hammer and rings of fire run out across the floor.
- **Lockdown** (70%): iron grates rise across the floor in a cross and four Iron Sentinels hold
  the gates. He can't be hurt until the sentinels are broken (or 30 seconds pass).
- **Grinding Blades:** three blades of fire sweep round the floor from its middle.
- **Iron Tempest** (35%): banners rise to guard him, his hammer whirls round him, waves of molten
  grates run out and the blades turn the other way.

### II. The Drowned Sanctum: Nerissa, the Tidecaller

A sea-queen with a serpent's tail, a coral crown and a golden trident.

- **Tidal Lanes:** bands of surging tide sweep across the floor. Step through the gaps.
- **Rising Tide** (66%): the sea floods the whole floor except the coral isles, and Riptide Eels
  hunt in it. Get to an isle and hold out for 20 seconds.
- **Whirlpool:** curved arms of tide wheel round the floor.
- **Maelstrom** (33%): everything at once, with the sea round her whipped into a storm.

### III. The Ember Foundry: Pyrrhax, the Forge Tyrant

A horned demon of rock and fire with wings of ash and a hammer of lava.

- **Lava Veins:** veins of lava crack open across the floor, first glowing, then burning, then
  cooling to slag.
- **Meteor Storm** (70%): meteors fall round him while Molten Golems join the fight.
- **The Crucible Pours** (35%): the floor's rim crumbles into the sky and lava pours in from the
  edge.
- **Molten Core:** his body goes molten and everything burns at once.

### IV. The Clockwork Orrery: Chronarch, the Hour Thief

A hooded thief of time with a clock for a face and an hourglass for a heart. Four arms carry
blades made of a clock's hands, and an orrery's ring wheels round him.

- **Hour Hands:** an hour hand and a minute hand sweep the floor, the minute hand faster.
- **Midnight Chime** (70%): the clock's hours light up in turn, half the floor at a time. Stand
  where it isn't lit when it strikes.
- **Time Fracture:** Hour Echoes of him appear and he blinks round the floor.
- **Reversal** (35%): the clock runs backwards and turns magenta, and the hands and chimes come
  together.

### V. The Apex: Vaelith, the Crucible Sovereign

The tower's maker: a crowned champion in white and gold with wings of fire and a ring of gold
set with the four floors' gems.

- **Sovereign's Decree:** stars fall on each of you.
- **The Echoes** (80%, 65%, 50%, 35%): one by one he raises the ghosts of the four champions
  below. Each turns the Apex into its floor (iron, tide, forge, then clock) and fights with that
  floor's hazards.
- **The Crucible Breaks** (20%): all four echoes return at once and the Apex's rim crumbles away.
- **Unbound:** his wings turn to the void for the end of the fight.

## The white bags

Each champion has a **1% chance** to drop a white bag with its own ability item, each for a
different class. Each one does what its class's ability does, then adds something of its
champion's:

| Champion | Item | Class | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ferrogrim | Ferrogrim's Bastion Turret | Mechanic | A turret of Ferrogrim's iron. It fires three shards at a time, and every 3 seconds it slams the ground: 380 damage round it, enemies' armor broken, allies beside it Armored. |
| Nerissa | Nerissa's Tidepearl | Druid | A substitute that hurls five tridents and heals you 200. Then three waves of the tide roll out: 220 damage to enemies, and 60 health to allies, each. |
| Pyrrhax | Pyrrhax's Meteor Mortar | Pirate | A shell that arcs to where you aim and bursts (750, paralyzed 1.5 s). Then four meteors fall round the crater, and its slag burns on for 3 seconds. |
| Chronarch | Chronarch's Coat of Stolen Hours | Blademaster | Armored for your allies and Damaging for you, as a jacket gives. Then time stops round you: enemies slowed and you Speedy, while a clock hand sweeps twice round you (320 each sweep). |
| Vaelith | Vaelith's Final Aria | Bard | Inspired for your allies, then four verses ring out from you, each wider and harder (250, 320, 400). The last is a crescendo (700) that breaks armor and sends your allies berserk. |

Each item's page under [Gear](gear.html) has its numbers.

## The leaderboard

Every climb that clears at least one floor goes on the Crucible's board: the most floors first,
then the quickest. `/crucible status` shows the top three, and the realm is told when a climb
takes first place.
