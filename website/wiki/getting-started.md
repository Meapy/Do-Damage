## The main menu

The game opens on a main menu with **Play**, **Sign in**, **Register**, **Options**, **Changelog**
and (on the desktop) **Quit**. You can change the options before you play, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> or
"Main menu" brings you back to it later.

You can play in your browser at [play.dodmg.com](https://play.dodmg.com/play/) or with the desktop client from the
[downloads](../#get). Both use the same accounts, characters and vault. See the
[FAQ](faq.html#browser-or-desktop) for the difference.

## Guest or account

**Play** without signing in starts you as a guest, with a name of your own ("Guest" and four
letters). A guest plays like anyone else, with one exception: guests can't buy from merchants, so
the Nexus Bazaar, the shops and the vault's chests are closed to you until you register.

A guest's login is kept on that computer or in that browser. If it's forgotten (you clear the
browser's data, or play on another device), the guest account is lost. **Register** ("Save
progress") at any time turns the guest into a full account, keeping its characters, items, vault
and fame.

To register, choose a name, an email and a password of at least 6 characters. After 10 wrong
passwords for one account, signing in to it is blocked for 15 minutes.

Once registered, **Link Discord** on the main menu links the account to your Discord account: a
free Potion of Maxy the first time, and a new password from the Discord bot if you ever lose
yours ([how](faq.html#why-link-my-discord)). It's optional.

A new account has {{v:char_slots}} character slots and {{v:vault_chests}} vault chests. More slots
cost {{v:slot_cost}} on the character screen; more vault chests are bought in the vault (see
[Items](items.html#your-vault)).

## Choosing a class

Every class is open from the start, the classic ones and the Doomed Realms ones:
{{v:class_count}} in all. Each class uses one kind of weapon, one kind of ability and one
kind of armor, and has its own highest stats. The [Classes](classes.html) page lists them all.

Some easy starts:

- **Wizard** or **Priest** for long-range damage (the Priest also heals).
- **Knight** or **Warrior** if you'd rather take hits up close.
- **Archer** or **Huntress** for safe, steady damage from range.

Each class has {{v:star_count}} class quests: reaching {{v:stars}} fame with a character of that
class. They earn your rank stars ([Getting stronger](progression.html#fame-rank-stars-and-class-quests)).

## Controls

Every key can be rebound in Options (<kbd>O</kbd>), to another key or to the mouse's side
buttons (Mouse 4 and Mouse 5).

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd> | Move |
| Mouse | Aim and shoot. <kbd>I</kbd> turns autofire on and off |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Use your ability at the mouse |
| Middle mouse button | Ping: mark a spot for your party, guild and the players near you ([Pings](#pings)) |
| <kbd>Q</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> | Turn the view; <kbd>Z</kbd> resets it |
| <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>8</kbd> | Use or equip an inventory slot |
| <kbd>F</kbd> <kbd>V</kbd> | Drink a health or magic potion |
| <kbd>B</kbd> | Switch between your inventory and backpack |
| <kbd>0</kbd> | Enter a portal, or talk to the NPC next to you |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | Back to the Nexus |
| <kbd>J</kbd> | The quest log: quests, the daily reward and achievements |
| <kbd>K</kbd> | In your vault: the vault window, every vault chest at once ([Items](items.html#your-vault)) |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Chat; <kbd>Tab</kbd> whispers, <kbd>G</kbd> guild chat, <kbd>P</kbd> party chat. The buttons above the chat box (All, Local, Guild, Party) pick where the lines you type go |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | Show the frame rate and your ping |
| <kbd>X</kbd> | Center the view on you |
| <kbd>F11</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Borderless fullscreen, and back to a window |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Menu: characters, options, log out |

Text boxes (chat, sign-in) copy, cut and paste with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, <kbd>X</kbd> and
<kbd>V</kbd>, select all with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> and undo with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>
(<kbd>Cmd</kbd> on a Mac). In the browser, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>, <kbd>F5</kbd>,
<kbd>F12</kbd> and the browser's tab and zoom keys still work, unless a control uses that key.

**Options** (<kbd>O</kbd>, or from the <kbd>Esc</kbd> menu) has a **Gameplay** tab with
"Drink stat potions from loot bags": while it's on, the stat potions in a bag you stand on are drunk
by themselves, Potions of Oryx too (a maxed stat's go to your potion storage), leaving Potions of
Max for you to keep; only loot bags, never your vault's gift chest. It's off unless you turn it on.
The **Visuals** tab is in three parts. **World**: "Pets' shots" (every pet's, yours too: Normal,
Subtle, the default, with smaller see-through shots, or Hidden), particles, shadows, health bars,
damage numbers and the crosshair. **Other players**: how much you see of other players, of their
**shots and spells** (a slider from 100% down to Hidden; it fades their projectiles, their pets'
shots, their spells and their Necromantic items' powers alike, while your own stay as they are),
of their pets, and whose **pings** show; then their pets' effects, and their **damage numbers**
(off unless you turn it on: the numbers of their hits too, on a boss say, so it's clear which are
yours). **Chat**: its **size** (50% to 150%: its text, width and height together), its
**background** (how dark the box behind it is, from none to the usual) and **Speech balloons** (a
list: tick whose show, such as only guild and party chat). The **Crosshair** tab picks the crosshair's
style (Classic, Cross, Dot, Circle, Circle and dot, Plus, Diagonal, Chevron or your own), its
colour and its size (50% to 200%), with a preview over dark and light ground. To make your own,
pick one of the ten colours under its 15 by 15 grid and click or drag on the grid: each square
takes the colour picked, so your crosshair can have as many colours as you like, and clicking a
square already in that colour empties it. The colours under the grid only paint new squares; the
**Colour** row beside them is for the other styles, and your own keeps the colours you painted.
**Clear** starts again from nothing,
and **Defaults** puts back the Classic crosshair but keeps your drawing. To share a crosshair,
**Copy code** puts its code on the clipboard (the code also shows under the button: `DD0064` is
the usual crosshair, and your own drawing makes it longer); a friend copies it and presses
**Paste code**, or presses <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> on the tab in the browser. The **Graphics** tab has the world zoom, a frame
rate limit and, on the desktop, **Borderless fullscreen**: the window covers the screen it's on,
with no border, and alt-tabbing out is instant. The game remembers it for the next time. It also
has the world's look: **Glow on bright light** (a soft glow round lava, fire, lanterns and
spells), **Vivid colours** (livelier colours and slightly darker corners) and, on a Windows PC,
**HDR**. HDR is on when Windows has HDR turned on for your screen, and then the glow shines
brighter than white. Turn any of them off if the game runs slowly on your computer. The
**Sound** tab has a Master volume, and **Ping sounds** to hear pings or not. The **Language** tab switches the game between its 11
languages (English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Português (Brasil),
Русский, Türkçe and Українська) at once, menus, windows, tooltips and messages alike; the first
time, it starts in your computer's or browser's language. Item and monster names and what the
server writes in the chat stay in English.

## Pings

Click the **middle mouse button** to ping: a marker everyone in your party and your guild in the
same world sees, wherever they are, and so do the other players within about 20 tiles of you or the
ping. What it says depends on what's under the mouse:

| Under the mouse | Ping |
|---|---|
| An enemy | **Danger**, which follows the enemy as it moves |
| A loot bag | **Loot** |
| A portal | **Go here** |
| You | **Help** |
| Anything else, or the ground | **Look here** |
| The minimap | **Go here**, at that spot on the map |

**Hold** the middle button (or drag a little) for the ping wheel: move toward Look here, Danger, Go
here, Loot or Help and let go to ping it there; let go in the middle to cancel.

A ping shows for six seconds: a ring on the ground with a pin over it, saying what it is and who
sent it (green for your party, blue for your guild), a marker at the edge of the screen pointing to
it when it's out of view, and a dot on the minimap. Danger has a sound of its own. You can ping four
times in five seconds. In Options, **Visuals**, Other players, "Pings" shows everyone's, only your party's
and guild's, or nobody's (yours always show), and **Sound** has Ping sounds.

## The screen

The world fills the window, with one bar down the right:

- **At the top:** the minimap, your experience, HP and MP. Your rank star, gold and fame sit
  beside the minimap. On it, your quest is a red box, realm events orange, and players yellow
  over everything; a realm you go back to keeps the map you'd uncovered. In the Nexus it also shows
  its places with their pictures (the Divine Forge, the Warcaller, the Pen Keeper, the portals...),
  ringed gold for those you talk to and violet for portals, even before you've been there, at the
  map's edge when they're out of view: hover one for its name.
- **Your character card:** your name, class and level, with two tabs: your stats, and your records
  (kills, god and Oryx kills, quests, dungeons, accuracy, potions drunk and time played).
  Out of combat you walk faster: **+20 Speed** (shown in green beside it) whenever no enemy has
  hit you and you haven't hit one for 10 seconds. It goes the moment either happens.
- **At the foot:** the Nexus button, your equipment, your inventory (two small buttons switch to
  your backpack), your potions, and a space for whatever is beside you: a bag you stand on, a
  portal, a shop or an invitation. Drag a potion from the potion bar onto an empty inventory
  slot to take it back out.
- **At the top left:** the boss you're after, with its health and the five players doing the most
  damage to it, with their class and share. Healing and buffing count too: a player's assist
  credit shows in green beside their damage ([Progression](progression.html#loot)). With no boss
  around, the menu button there opens your
  last 3 bosses: click one to see everyone who hit it.

Hover over any item to see what it does; the numbers that beat what you're wearing show in green.
Hold **Left Shift** to see what's in the loot bags around you, over each bag.
Hover over a nearby player for their rank and gear. Right-click one in the Nearby players list (or
on the minimap) to keep their card open, then hover the items they wear for each one's tooltip;
right-click again or click anywhere else to close it.

## The Nexus

The Nexus is the safe hub between runs: a cluster of floating islands in a sunset sky, at two
heights and joined by bridges, in the look of a Japanese garden village: cherry trees in bloom,
lanterns, temples and a koi pond, with clouds drifting by below and the odd shooting star. Rock
hangs under every island, and railings run round their edges. You arrive on a deck of
pink-stained wood in the middle of the spawn's island, which heals you while you stand on it, as
the stone water basins at its corners do. From there:

- **On the spawn's island**: the **Support Bot** in the middle of the deck, the Name Changer
  (your first name is free; changing it costs {{v:name_change}} fame) strolling about its west
  side and the Character Changer about its east, the Armoire (skins) and the **Vault** portal.
  The koi pond round the deck is shallow: wade across it, west, east or south, to the paths to
  the big bridges. Every ten minutes the Support Bot lets a flock of **XP ducks** onto the pond:
  shoot them for experience, and now and then a Health or Magic Potion. Frogs sit among the lily pads in the pond's north-east, and cherry
  trees bloom on its banks. Wade north across the pond to the **vault yard**: four **shared vault
  chests** in front of the storehouse, which everyone can use: put something in for another
  player to take, or take what someone left.
- **West**, over the arched wooden bridge: the **market island**. Its shopping street along the
  north is the Nexus Bazaar: each shop is a row of stalls in front of a house (stat potions, dungeon keys,
  Elder and Doomed keys, consumables, specials, pet generators, clothing and accessories). The
  stalls take turns through each shop's items, and everything there costs fame. Over the bridge
  you come to the **market square**: two rows of stalls showing what players sell, where the
  **Market Broker** (the [Marketplace](marketplace.html)), the **Tinkerer** and the **Chronicler**
  (your [quests, daily reward and achievements](quests.html)) stroll about. Up the cross street,
  the guild's teahouse, the **Guild Hall** (GUILD HALL over its door): walk up its steps to go
  through to your guild's hall, and use the **Guild Register**, the notice board at its west side,
  to make a guild or leave yours (see [Guilds](guilds.html): every member gets +1% loot). Below it
  stands the quest house, and in the island's south-west the forge court and its **smithy**: its
  great furnace is the [Divine Forge](forge.html) and its crystal altar the
  [Enchanter](imprints.html), and **Amatsumara**, the smith god, works at his anvil before it.
- **South**, between stone lanterns and down the walled stone bridge to the island a floor below
  and under the torii: the **shrine of the realm portals**. The portals are sunk into its floor:
  **Realm 2**'s great vortex in the middle, and a dark cavern for each realm round it, each with
  its name and how many players are inside, and west of the vortex the **Arena**'s sand pit ([The
  Arena](progression.html#the-arena)). Walk onto one to enter. A pagoda stands behind them, and
  round the shrine a forest of cherry, pine and maple where blossom falls around you: a dry garden
  of raked gravel round the elder cherry in its west, and in its east a quiet clearing with a
  wayside shrine, where the twins Hana and Sora run about.
- **East**, over the other arched bridge: the **Arena island**. Its court of raked sand holds a
  samurai's statue, banners, straw dummies, the Arena Leaderboard's golden trophy, the **Herald**
  and the **Warcaller** (the [community goals](community-goals.html)); east of them, the pet pen,
  where pets of every kind come and go, and the **Pen Keeper** at its gate, who
  [raises and swaps pets](pets.html#the-pen-keeper-raising-a-pet). Beside it rises a round
  stone tower: go in at its door and climb the spiral stair inside to its top, where the
  [Hall of Champions](champions.html) stands round the cap room, with a telescope to look out
  over the sky, and on an overhang to the east the **Sky Bar**: every 20 seconds Barman Kenji
  throws a drink at his guests (15 seconds of Speedy, Hallucinating or Stasis, and a "PARTY!").

Villagers walk between the islands and stop to chat, and bees drift over the flowers. On the
islands you only talk to, and see the signs of, the people and things on your own floor, but the
Hall of Champions' champions show from anywhere, high on the tower.

The **Herald**, on the Arena island's court by the Arena Leaderboard, keeps
everyone's week together: dungeons cleared, Oryx chains completed and failed, heroes fallen,
monsters slain, realm events and realms, potions, forging, and the monsters slain most.

`/nexus` or <kbd>R</kbd> brings you back from anywhere.

**Don't idle in the Nexus.** Stand still there for {{v:nexus_afk}} minutes and your character is
saved and you're sent back to the character screen, so idlers don't take up a place or count as
online. Only walking resets the clock: chatting, trading or browsing the Marketplace while standing
still doesn't. Everywhere else (realms, dungeons, your vault) you can stand still as long as you
like.

## Free starter gear: /gear

Type `/gear` in the Nexus for a set of starter gear for your class: a weapon, an ability, armor and
a ring from about half way up the regular tiers (never above tier {{v:gear_max_tier}}). For most
classes that's a tier 7 weapon, tier 8 armor and a tier 4 ability and ring. The
[Classes](classes.html) page shows exactly what each class gets. It also uses
{{v:gear_boosters}} for you there and then: you level faster and find more loot from the start.

Its limits:

- **Once per character.** A new character can use it again.
- **In the Nexus only**, and **below level {{v:gear_level}}**.
- **It only upgrades.** Untiered items and anything as good or better stay where they are.
- **The boosters and the items it replaces go to your inventory**, so keep a few slots free; it
  says how many it needs if you don't have room. If your gear is already better, you still get
  the boosters.

## The Support Bot

The Support Bot stands by the spawn, next to the Armoire. It greets you when you come near and
gives a tip every {{v:bot_talk}} seconds to players nearby. The tips:

{{gen:bot-tips}}

It also hands out two things:

- **XP gifts.** Every {{v:bot_minutes}} minutes, once every gift of the last batch is gone, it puts
  out {{v:bot_gifts}} XP gifts on the plaza around the spawn. Shoot them for experience: each is
  worth up to {{v:gift_xp}} experience to everyone close by. At the lowest levels a single kill
  gives at most a tenth of what your next level needs, so they help most from about level 7. Between
  tips it says how many gifts are left and when the next batch comes.
- **A dungeon portal.** Every {{v:bot_minutes}} minutes ({{v:bot_portal_offset}} minutes after the
  gifts), it opens a portal to a random dungeon just south of the spawn. The portal closes once
  that dungeon has been cleared (its boss died and no other boss is left for {{v:bot_cleared}}
  seconds), or when the next one replaces it. Anyone already inside stays.

## Your first hour

1. Make a character, then type `/gear`.
2. Kill a few batches of XP gifts by the spawn, or go straight to a realm with `/realm`.
3. In the realm, start near the beaches (where you arrive) and move inland as you level. The
   further from the shore, the harder the monsters.
4. Take the dungeon portals that monsters drop. The [Dungeons](dungeons.html) page says which are
   easy.
5. Once you're level 20, try `/godlands` with friends. The Godlands' gods drop the best realm
   loot.
6. Bring stat potions and anything you want to keep to your vault (`/vault`) before trying
   something risky.
