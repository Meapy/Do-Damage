Do Damage is a Realm of the Mad God private server. Most of the game works like the original: you
pick a class, fight your way up through the realm, collect gear, and one death is final for that
character. But a lot here is different: free starter gear, a Support Bot in the Nexus, dozens of
dungeons fixed or rebuilt, Doomed Realms classes and items, and an Oryx chain six stages deep.

This wiki is written for this server. The dungeon list, the classes, the commands, the shop prices
and many of the numbers on these pages are read straight from the game's code and data every time
the site updates, so they match what you'll see in game.

## The game in one minute

1. Make a character and type `/gear` in the Nexus for free starter gear.
2. Go to a realm (a portal in the Nexus, or `/realm`) and kill monsters to level up. Level 20 is the
   top.
3. Head for the realm's quests, its toughest monsters (the arrow at the edge of the screen points
   at yours), and the dungeon portals they drop.
4. Drink stat potions to raise your stats to their highest. That's what "maxing" means.
5. When enough of a realm's events are beaten ({{v:realm_close}}), it closes and everyone in it is sent to
   Oryx's Castle. Follow Oryx down.
6. Put what you want to keep in your vault: when a character dies, it and everything it carries
   are gone, but its fame is yours to keep.

Use the search box to find anything on these pages: a dungeon, a boss, an item or a command.
