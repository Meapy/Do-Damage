## The game in one minute

![The Nexus: where every character starts, with portals to the realm, the shops, the Divine Forge and the dungeons](nexus)

1. **Make a character** and type `/gear` in the Nexus for free starter gear.
2. **Go to the realm** (a portal in the Nexus, or `/realm`) and kill monsters to level up. Level
   20 is the top.
3. **Follow the arrow** at the edge of the screen to the realm's toughest monsters, and take the
   dungeon portals they drop.
4. **Drink stat potions** to raise your stats to their highest. That's what "maxing" means.
5. **Close the realm:** when enough of its events are beaten ({{v:realm_close}}), everyone in it
   is sent to Oryx's Castle. Follow Oryx down.
6. **Keep what matters in your vault:** when a character dies, it and everything it carries are
   gone, but its fame is yours to keep.

> dungeon, a boss, an item or a command.

## About this wiki

Do Damage is a Realm of the Mad God private server. Most of the game works like the original, but
a lot is different: free starter gear, a Support Bot in the Nexus, dozens of dungeons fixed or
rebuilt, {{v:class_count}} classes and an Oryx chain six stages deep. The dungeon list, the classes,
the commands, the shop prices and many numbers here are read straight from the game's data every
time the site updates, so they match what you see in game. Found a mistake? See
[FAQ and help](faq.html).
