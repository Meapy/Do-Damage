There are {{v:dungeon_count}} dungeons you can reach on this server. Each is its own little world
with a boss at the end; beating it drops loot and a Realm Portal back out (every dungeon boss
leaves one, and portals bosses drop inside a dungeon stay open).

**How to get in:** most dungeon portals drop from monsters in the realm, open for a while, and
show the dungeon's name. A **key** opens its dungeon's portal wherever you use it. Keys drop from
bosses and are sold in the **Nexus Bazaar**. The Support Bot also opens a random dungeon by the
Nexus spawn every {{v:bot_minutes}} minutes.

A **Portal of Cowardice** takes you back the way you came: to the realm from a dungeon opened in
a realm, and to the Nexus from one opened in the Nexus or in another dungeon.

Some dungeons are laid out anew for every run, from the rooms of their map: small, medium or large.

> **About these numbers.** The list is built from the server's data each time the site updates.
> Drop chances are the listed ones; your real chance is higher (about double when you're alone),
> since every roll grows with your share of the damage. Difficulty is a rough guide from the
> boss's health, alone: groups make bosses tougher, and some easy bosses have hard dungeons
> before them.

## Candyland Hunting Grounds

Each room starts with six Candy Creatures. Clear them and leave the room for a boss to appear;
the next wave waits until that boss is defeated and you return.

## The Lair of Draconis

The Rock Dragon in the realm drops its portal, and a Draconis Key opens it. You arrive by the
Altar of Draconis, with four dragon souls round it, one in front of each sealed corridor. Step onto
a soul to be taken into its dragon's lair: Nikao the Azure Dragon (north), Pyyr the Crimson Dragon
(west), Limoz the Veridian Dragon (east) or Feargus the Obsidian Dragon (south). A dragon wakes
when you come near. Pyyr hides behind his hatchlings and throws lava bombs, Limoz behind six
shields that must fall first. At the end each dragon turns into a loot balloon: pop it and the
corridor back to the altar opens. Beat all four in any order and the altar opens the Ivory
Wyvern's portal, and half the time the Elder Lair's.

Each dragon drops its own stat potions and, rarely, a relic of itself to keep. An Elder Draconis
Key, sold for 400 fame at the Bazaar or dropped by the Elder Lair's dragons, opens the Elder Lair
too. The **Elder Lair** is the same lair with Elder dragons: twice their health, harder hits and
faster attacks, and better loot, including a potion that maxes the dragon's stat and its Elder
armour or bow. Its altar opens the Elder Ivory Wyvern instead of the Ivory Wyvern.

The **Ivory Wyvern** fights with four mirror images, then with the four dragons' soul flames,
then alone. Half the time it leaves the portal to the **Elder Ivory Wyvern**: the same fight with
twice the health, harder hits and faster attacks. The Elder Wyvern can drop a Potion of Max Life
or Max Mana, better gear and its own Elder Ivory Wyvern Key, and, rarely, any of the four Elder
dragons' armours and bow. The Elder Draconis Key drops from the Elder Lair's dragons; each key
drops in its matching Elder dungeon. Both keys can also be community-goal rewards.

## The Shatters and the Elder Shatters

The Avatar of the Forgotten King in the realm drops the Shatters' portal, and a Shatters Key
opens it. Three bosses hold it: the Bridge Sentinel (kill its obelisks first), the Twilight
Archmage and his birds Inferno and Blizzard, and the Forgotten King, who hides between his
attacks. Each leaves a loot balloon.

Half the time the Forgotten King also leaves the portal to the **Elder Shatters**: the same
ruin by brazier light, every monster in its Elder version (twice the health, harder hits, faster
attacks), and a phase of its own for each boss:

- **The Bridge Sentinel's Judgement,** below 45% health: armoured, it stops sweeping the bridge
  and spins two spirals round itself, slowing blades one way and heavy bolts the other, and
  throws Blobombs at you between its swarms.
- **The Twilight Archmage's Eclipse:** he throws fire portals as well as ice ones while he casts,
  and where the regular Archmage stands spent after his last blast, the Elder one calls an
  Eclipse (piercing bolts and confusing blades turning against each other, rings of heavy orbs,
  a rain of firebombs) and then goes round his generators again.
- **The Forgotten King remembered:** at a tenth of his health he doesn't die. He comes out of
  hiding for good, fire and ice turning round him, his guardians back at his side, until he is
  truly beaten.

Each Elder boss drops a Potion of Max (Defense, Mana and Life), better gear, the Shatters' own
rings at twice the chance, and a ring found nowhere else: the **Bracer of the Elder Guardian**
(braces you with defense when your health falls), **The Eclipsed Gemstone** (every kill restores
mana) and **The Remembered Crown** (your ability lends you attack). The Elder Shatters Key drops
from all three. Like every Elder boss they can be raised again, below, for a trap, a spell and
a cloak of their own.

## Raising an Elder boss

Every Elder dungeon's boss can be fought twice. When it falls it leaves its remains for a little
while, and a **Necromantic Relic** used beside them raises it again in its **Empowered** form: a
harder fight with better loot and an untiered item of its own. See
[Raising an Elder boss: the Necromantic Relic](necromantic-relic.html) for how it works, and each
boss's health, item and drops.

## The Lost Halls

The Lost Sentry in Realm 2's Lost Lands opens the Lost Halls when it dies, Oryx 2 now and then
leaves a portal too, and a Lost Halls Key opens one anywhere. The halls are a maze of marble
rooms, laid out anew for every run. Each room has its own guards: the Crusade round its
Commander, Oryx's army round the Champion of Oryx, the Grotto's bats, rats and slimes round the
Grotto Blob, three golems round the Tormented Golem (who can't be hurt until they fall), ghostly
sentries floating over a bridge across a pit (they can't be hurt: run past), the Agonized Titan
and halls of traps: pressure plates that burst a moment after you step
by and arrow slits along the walls. The pot room holds seven treasure pots full of potions, but
one in three hides an Evil Spirit.

At the end of the maze is the Colossus room. Four Marble Defenders rise when you come in; once
they fall, the **Marble Colossus** wakes. He fights in many phases: rocks and pillars that circle
him, lasers, marble cores you must break before he can be hurt again, and a last stand where he
draws back part of his strength. One run in four the Colossus doesn't stir and a hidden passage
to the **Cultist Hideout** opens instead. Where the Colossus falls, a tear into the Void stays
open for five minutes (after four it warns that it's closing): that's where a Vial of Pure
Darkness opens the Void. His Realm Portal opens three tiles west of it (and the Elder Lost Halls
portal, when he leaves one, three tiles east), so the Void's portal has its own spot.

The Colossus drops like Oryx 3: stat potions, tier 13 to 15 gear, and his own Sword of the
Colossus, Breastplate of New Life, Marble Seal and Magical Lodestone. The rooms' leaders drop gear
too, and the Tormented Golem now and then a piece of the Lost Golem set.

## The Elder Lost Halls

Half the time the Marble Colossus, Malus and the Void Entity each leave a portal to the **Elder
Lost Halls**, and an Elder Lost Halls Key opens one anywhere. It is the Lost Halls by brazier
light, laid out anew for every run, every monster in its Elder version (twice the health, harder
hits, faster attacks), and it leads on as the regular halls do: one run in four its Colossus room
opens the **Elder Cultist Hideout**, and a Vial of Pure Darkness used where the Elder Colossus
fell opens the **Elder Void**. Each of the three bosses has a phase of its own:

- **The Marble Colossus' Reckoning,** late in his second life: he goes back to the heart of his
  hall, wheels of marble turning one way round him and spikes the other, slow bullets rolling out
  between them. Walk with the wheels, between their arms.
- **Malus' Pentagram:** once he has taken his cultists' powers, he returns to the centre of the
  star after his own fire each time round. Molek's flames land on its five points and the five
  between them, and two five-armed spirals turn through them.
- **The Void Entity's Singularity,** at a tenth of its health: back at the centre, it throws its
  projections out a third time and turns two spirals against each other until it falls.

Each Elder boss drops a Potion of Max (Defense from the Colossus, Attack from Malus, Life from
the Void Entity), tier 14 weapons, tier 15 armour, tier 7 abilities and rings, its own items at
twice the chance, the Elder Lost Halls Key and a ring found nowhere else: the **Lodestone of the
Halls** (stand still for a moment and you turn to marble, armored until you move), the **Ring of
the Blood Pact** (below half your health your shots hit harder and ignore defense) and **The
Hollow Sourcestone** (every few seconds it breaks the armor of the enemy nearest you). Raised
again, below, they drop a seal that raises marble pillars round you, a skull that draws a
pentagram on your target and a bow whose hits open a singularity.

## The Cultist Hideout and the Void

A portal in the Lost Halls sometimes leads to the **Cultist Hideout**, and a Cult Key opens it
too. Malus' followers, one colour for each of his cultists, and his fire,
ice and lightning demons hold its winding halls. In the ritual hall at the end, Malus raises his
four cultists on the points of a pentagram and wakes them one at a time: Argus (unstable bolts),
Gaius (paralysing lightning), Basaran (slowing ice) and Dirge (silencing light). Then he summons
the fire demon Molek, then Balaam, who splits into his three heads when slain, and at last takes
his cultists' powers for himself and fights you with each in turn.

Malus drops the cult's gear and, half the time, a **Vial of Pure Darkness** for one player in the
group. Use it in the Lost Halls, where the Marble Colossus fell, to open **the Void**: a platform
in a sea of Pure Evil (stay on the platform: the evil hurts). The Void Entity rises at its centre
and, at three quarters and at half its health, throws its projections onto the four marks round
the platform, from where they fire inwards for half a minute. It drops the Void's gear.
