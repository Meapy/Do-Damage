## How pets work

A **pet generator** is an item. Use it and that pet joins your character: it follows you
everywhere, fights for you and, depending on the pet, heals your HP or MP. A character has one pet
at a time; using another generator swaps it. `/removepet` sends your pet away.

- **Pets that shoot** fire real projectiles, sent as your own shots: their hits count as yours for
  loot, experience and quests.
- **Boss pets**, pets that look like a boss (Bes, Nut, Geb, the Cube God, Limon, Malphas,
  Septavius, Stheno, Thessal, the Grand Sphinx and others), shoot that boss's projectiles in its
  patterns, without its stuns and other effects.
- Some pets strike in melee or throw things, and some heal.

The generator's tooltip shows the pet's damage a second on one enemy and how much it heals and how
often.

## Where to get one

- The **Nexus Bazaar** sells every pet generator for {{v:pet_price}} fame.
- `/shop` has a pet shop.
- Some monsters drop generators: the table below says which.

Other players' pets, and their shots, can be faded or hidden in Options, Visuals.
