Every Elder dungeon's boss can be fought twice. When it falls, its souls cling to the body, and
with a **Necromantic Relic** you can drag it back to life in its **Empowered** form: a much harder
fight with much better loot, and an item of its own found nowhere else. That's
{{v:raise_bosses}} bosses: one for every Elder dungeon, three in the Elder Tomb, three in the
Elder Shatters and four in the Elder Lair of Draconis.

## Getting a relic

Every Elder boss drops a Necromantic Relic to each player who did at least
{{v:relic_threshold}} of the damage, {{v:relic_chance}} of the time. It's an Ethereal item, and
it can be traded: a group can pass one to whoever should use it, and a relic kept from one run
raises a boss in the next.

## The remains

A fallen Elder boss leaves its **remains** where it died: the body lying on the ground, grey and
still, with green souls swirling up round it. They stay for {{v:raise_seconds}} seconds. If no one
raises the boss in time the souls scatter and the body goes. An Empowered boss leaves no remains:
each boss can be raised only once a run.

## The souls' call

The souls want their master back, and they know who can do it. While anyone within 25 tiles
carries a relic, the souls call them by name in the chat, one carrier after another, every few
seconds ("We hear your power, *name*... Use the Necromantic Relic! Bring our master back!"), and
the first time they call a player it shows over their head too, so the whole group sees who holds
one. In the last 15 seconds they beg you to hurry. With no relic near they only mourn.

## The ritual

Stand within 3 tiles of the remains and use the relic (double-click it, or its number key). Used
anywhere else it does nothing, says why and stays in your inventory. The relic is spent, and the
ritual begins: a skull of soul-fire rises over the body, an ominous chant starts, the souls rush in
from all round and soul-fire strikes down into the corpse three times. After three seconds it
bursts in a ring of dark light and the boss stands up again. Everyone in the dungeon is told who
raised it.

## The Empowered form

An Empowered boss is the same boss, and it fights as it always did, but stronger in every way:

- a fifth bigger, wreathed in souls and glowing violet;
- more health than the Elder boss: {{v:raise_health_small}} times for the smaller bosses, less the
  bigger the boss ({{v:raise_health_200k}} times at 200,000, down to {{v:raise_health_big}} times), and up to
  {{v:raise_defense}} more defense (less for a boss already heavily armoured). In a group each player
  adds the same share as on the Elder boss;
- its own shots hit 30% harder, and everything it does is a quarter faster;
- for its first moments it is rising and can't be hurt.

The souls fight for it too:

- **Soul Nova:** a ring of eighteen soul bolts, then a second ring a moment later, every 7 seconds.
- **Wailing Souls:** two ghosts that hunt the players and fire bolts at them, every 11 seconds, up to
  {{v:raise_souls}} at once. They go when the boss falls.
- **Soul Storm,** below 60% health: four arms of fast, slowing bolts sweeping round the boss, every
  10 seconds.
- **Enraged,** below 30% health: faster still (nearly half as fast again as the Elder boss), shots
  45% harder, a Soul Nova every 5 seconds and three Wailing Souls every 8, glowing pink.

## What it drops

An Empowered boss drops everything its Elder form drops, with every chance doubled for everyone
who fought it (except what's in the loot balloon the dragons and the Wyvern turn into; the
Elder Shatters' three drop what their balloons held themselves). Then it rolls the Elder boss's own items a second time, at up to twice their chance
(ten points more at most), with its Potion of Max at four times its chance (a tenth at least, a
quarter at most).

On top of that, each Empowered boss has an untiered item of its own: {{v:raise_item_chance}} for
each player who did at least 1% of the damage, before loot boosts. There are {{v:raise_bosses}},
a different kind of gear for each, so every class has some to hunt. Each is built on the best item
of its kind, at {{v:raise_item_min}} to {{v:raise_item_max}} times its strength (the strongest give something up: speed, defense or
health), has stats of its own on top, and has a **power** no other item has. They're soulbound.
The gear pages list them with their powers.
