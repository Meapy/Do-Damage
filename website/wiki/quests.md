Do Damage gives you something to aim for every time you log in: **quests** that change every day,
week and month, a **daily reward** for showing up, and **achievements** that remember everything
you've done, account-wide. All of it lives in the **quest log**: press <kbd>J</kbd>, click
**Quests** beside the Nexus button, or talk to the two keepers by the Nexus spawn.

> **Rewards go straight to you.** Fame goes to your account, and items into your inventory (or, when
> it's full, into the gift chest in your vault). Everything is account-wide: progress on one
> character counts for all of them, and dying doesn't lose any of it.

## The quest keepers

Two NPCs stand either side of the spawn rug in the Nexus:

- **The Tinkerer**, a gnome tinker with brass goggles, keeps your daily, weekly and monthly quests.
  Walk up to him and he tells you how many are done, or when new ones come.
- **The Chronicler**, a hooded scholar with a great glowing book, keeps the daily reward and your
  achievements. She tells you when today's reward is waiting and how many deeds you can claim.

Stand by either and press **Quests** (or **Open**) in the bottom of your HUD to open the quest log
at the right page. You can claim from anywhere, though: the keepers are there to remind you.

The **Quests** button turns ember and shows a gold count while rewards wait, and a popup at the top
of the screen tells you when you finish a quest, earn an achievement or reach a new rank. Type
`/quests` to see it all in the chat.

## Quests

You get **{{v:quests_daily}} daily**, **{{v:quests_weekly}} weekly** and **{{v:quests_monthly}}
monthly** quests, picked for you from the lists below; no two of a period are of the same kind.
They reset at **midnight UTC**: daily quests every day, weekly ones on Monday, monthly ones on the
1st. The quest log counts down to each reset.

1. Play: progress counts as it happens, wherever you are (but see what counts, below).
2. When a quest's bar fills, its card glows gold: press **Claim**.
3. Claim all of a period's quests and its **bonus** unlocks: claim that too.

Don't like a quest? Each period gives you **{{v:quests_swaps}} swap**: press **Swap** on a quest you
haven't finished to trade it for another. A finished quest you don't claim before the reset is
lost, so claim as you go.

### What counts

- **Monsters** count when they die near you, as experience does: you needn't land the killing
  blow. Monsters in the Nexus, vaults and the minigames don't count (the Arena has its own wave
  quests), nor do monsters that give no experience or that staff spawned.
- A **dungeon** counts as cleared once its last boss is dead, for everyone still in it who was near
  a boss when it died. Each run counts once. Its difficulty is the one on the
  [Dungeons](dungeons.html) page, from its strongest boss.
- **Realm events** are the realm's event bosses (the ones that count towards closing it).
  **Closing a realm** counts for everyone in the realm when its last event falls.
- **Stat potions** count when they raise a stat: one that goes to potion storage doesn't (you can
  take it back out).
- **Fame** is the fame your characters earn as they play (what the death screen calls base fame).
- **Arena waves** count when everyone in the Arena beats a wave.

## The daily reward

Log in once a day and collect a reward from the Chronicler (or the quest log's **Daily Rewards**
page). The track has {{v:quests_login_days}} days, with bigger rewards (&#9733;) at the end of each
week; after the last day it starts again. The day moves on only when you collect, so **missing a
day never loses your place**: you just pick up where you left off. A new reward comes at midnight
UTC, and a popup tells you when one is waiting. A full track is worth {{v:quests_login_fame}} fame
as well as its items.

## Achievements

There are {{v:quests_achievements}} achievements, each with {{v:quests_tiers}} tiers: Bronze,
Silver, Gold and Ember. They count your whole account's play, for good, and they don't reset. When
you reach a tier, the Chronicler writes it down: press **Claim** on the quest log's
**Achievements** page to collect its reward and its points. If you've reached several tiers at
once, one claim collects them all.

Your kills, god kills, Oryx kills, levels and fame from before achievements came were counted from
your characters, alive and dead, so veterans start with tiers to claim.

### Ranks and name colours

Achievement points raise your **rank**, shown at the top of the Achievements page (all
{{v:quests_max_points}} points make you Mythic). Each rank from Adventurer up unlocks a **name
colour** for the chat: pick it under "Chat name colour" (the **A** puts your usual colour back).

{{gen:quests_ranks}}

## Good to know

- Guests can't collect rewards: register your account first.
- Quests, the daily reward and achievements are kept on your account, so they follow you to every
  character and every server, and survive server restarts.
- The rewards are meant to add to your play, not replace it: a quest gives a few minutes' worth of
  fame, and the items are the kind that drop anyway.
