The **Enchanter** is the crystal altar beside the [Divine Forge](forge.html)'s furnace, in the smithy of the
Nexus's village, north of its shopping street. It puts **imprints** on **Enthroned gear** (Oryx the Ascended God's own items, which the
forge makes; see [The Divine Forge](forge.html#the-ascended-items)): small upgrades that stay on the
item, through your vault, trades and the [Marketplace](marketplace.html). No other item takes imprints.

> Imprints are being tested: for now only admins can imprint gear. Anyone can open the Enchanter
> and look.

## Slots

Every piece of Enthroned gear has three imprint slots, one of each colour:

- **Offensive** (red): more damage;
- **Defensive** (green): more health and defense;
- **Misc** (purple): an effect of its own, most of them for one kind of weapon.

A fourth colour is coming in a later update. An item shows a small gold-edged square for each
imprint it carries, side by side at the top left of its slot (red first, then green, then
purple). Its tooltip shows its numbers with the imprints (in green where they change them), lists
the imprints under **Imprints** and sums up what they change under **With imprints**.

Not every imprint fits every item. The damage imprints only fit weapons, as they make the item's own
shots stronger; the health and defense imprints fit any gear; **Last Breath** fits rings; and each
weapon's Misc imprint fits only that kind of weapon. Some items have more imprints to choose from
than others: more are coming.

Imprints work only on the item they're on, and only while it's **equipped**: +5% damage makes a
weapon that hits for 100 hit for 105. They never change an ability's own effects.

## Imprinting an item

1. Stand by the Enchanter: the bottom of your HUD says "The Divine Workbench: imprint your gear".
   Press **Open**. The **Divine Workbench** opens at its **Imprints** tab (the forge opens the same
   window; its Imprints tab is here too).
2. Pick a piece of Enthroned gear: equipped, in your inventory (or backpack), or in your vault
   (the **Vault** section lists the Enthroned gear in your own vault chests). Only the imprints
   that fit it show. Click it again, or **Show all imprints**, to look over every imprint of each
   colour and what it fits; the list scrolls with the mouse wheel.
3. Pick a colour, then press **Imprint** on the imprint you want. A slot that already holds one
   says **Replace**: the new imprint takes the old one's place.

Imprints show wherever the item does: in your inventory and bags, in the trade window, on other
players' gear in their card (the players near you, bottom right), and on the character list.

Each imprint costs **one Ascended shard**, of the kind for its colour, taken from your forge storage
(or your inventory when your storage has none):

| Colour | Costs |
|---|---|
| Offensive | an Ascended Weapon Shard or an Ascended Ability Shard |
| Defensive | an Ascended Armor Shard |
| Misc | an Ascended Accessory Shard |

Oryx the Ascended God (Oryx 5) drops the Ascended shards (see
[The Divine Forge](forge.html#forge-shards-and-the-forge-storage)).
