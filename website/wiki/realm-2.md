Realm 2 is a second kind of realm, opened with a **Realm 2 Key** rather than from the Nexus. Every
Realm 2 is built new when it opens, so no two are the same.

## How it's laid out

You arrive on **the shore and lowlands**, at one end of a starry void: beaches round the edge,
then sand, plains and forest, with the realm's lowland monsters and quests. Next to them are
**the midlands and highlands**, with the realm's mid and high monsters and quests. Everything
beyond them is void, until the events come.

## Events and their lands

Events come one at a time, as in the realm (more at once when it's busy). First the two easy ones,
the **Lucky Ent God** and the **Lucky Djinn**, in the midlands. Then come the eleven big events in
a random order, and each one brings **a land of its own** when it appears: its own ground,
scenery, ruined walls and monsters, with its boss in an arena at its heart. Each new land grows out
of one already there, further from the shore, in any direction, and runs into it by a broad neck of
land (now and then a bridge or a pool instead). So Realm 2 spreads out as it's played, into one land
you can roam from end to end like the realm's Godlands. A land stays once its event is beaten.

- **Monsters** roam in small groups, as in the Godlands: each land's own, and the Godlands' gods
  (White Demons, Medusas, Beholders and the rest) with them. They're about as strong as the gods
  and drop as they do (tiered gear up to T10 armour, and a stat potion now and then), the gods
  their dungeon portals too, and they count as gods for your quests. The small ones (Cubelets,
  Scarabs, Worker Bees...) only come with the quest monsters that bring them.
- **Quest monsters** have far more health and drop better: T10-T12 gear, T4-T5 abilities and
  rings, two kinds of stat potion and sometimes Life or Mana, and now and then a portal to a dungeon
  that fits their land (Mad Lab, Cursed Library, Haunted Cemetery, Ocean Trench, Davy Jones'
  Locker, Tomb of the Ancients, Undead Lair, Magic Woods, Lair of Draconis, The Shatters, The Hive).
  Each land always has its two; one that dies comes back within a minute or so.
- Some lands have ground of their own to watch for: the Ashen Caldera's **lava** burns, and the Honey
  Meadow's **honey** and the Tidepools' and Wreck Coast's water slow you.

## Closing Realm 2

Realm 2 closes once all thirteen of its events are defeated: the two Lucky gods and the eleven
lands' bosses. Oryx then sends everyone in it to his Castle, as a realm does. A busy Realm 2 needs
more: with more than eight players it needs one more event for each extra player (as a realm does,
up to 25), and those bring new lands of their own. Event bosses grow stronger with the players
near them, as in the realm. Each event that falls is announced to Realm 2 with how many are down
and how many it needs.
