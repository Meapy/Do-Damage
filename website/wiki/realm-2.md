Realm 2 is a second kind of realm, opened with a **Realm 2 Key** rather than from the Nexus. Every
Realm 2 is built new when it opens, so no two are the same: the same lands in a different order,
different shapes, different links between them.

## How it's laid out

Realm 2 is a chain of thirteen lands across a starry void, winding a different way each time.

1. **The shore and lowlands**, where you arrive: beaches round the edge, sand, plains and forest
   inside, with the realm's lowland monsters and quests.
2. **The midlands and highlands**, joined to the shore by land: the realm's mid and high monsters
   and quests, and the two easy events, the **Lucky Ent God** and the **Lucky Djinn**, each in a
   clearing of its own.
3. **Eleven event lands**, one for every big realm event, in a random order. Each one has its own
   ground, scenery and walls, four monsters and two quest monsters of its own, as strong as the
   Godlands, and its event boss waiting in an arena at its heart.

Each land joins the next by a **bridge** over the void, a **pool** you wade across, or plain
**land**. Nothing else joins them, so you go through the lands in turn.

- **Monsters** drop as the Godlands' gods do (tiered gear up to T10 armour, and a stat potion of
  their land's kind now and then) and count as gods for your quests.
- **Quest monsters** have far more health and drop better: T10-T12 gear, T4-T5 abilities and
  rings, two kinds of stat potion and sometimes Life or Mana, and now and then a portal to a dungeon
  that fits their land (Mad Lab, Cursed Library, Haunted Cemetery, Ocean Trench, Davy Jones'
  Locker, Tomb of the Ancients, Undead Lair, Magic Woods, Lair of Draconis, The Shatters, The Hive).
  Each land always has its two; one that dies comes back within a minute or so.
- Some lands have ground of their own to watch for: the Ashen Caldera's **lava** burns, the Honey
  Meadow's **honey** and the Tidepools' and Wreck Coast's water slow you.

## Closing Realm 2

Every event is up from the start: the eleven event bosses and the two Lucky gods, thirteen in all.
When they're all defeated, Oryx closes Realm 2 and sends everyone in it to his Castle, as a realm
does. A busy Realm 2 needs more: with more than eight players it needs one more event for each
extra player (as a realm does, up to 25), and the extra events come back one at a time, each to
its own land. Event bosses grow stronger with the players near them, as in the realm.

Each event that falls is announced to Realm 2 with how many are down and how many it needs.
