Every month the realm opens a new **season** of the **Realm Pass**: a road of
**{{v:pass_tiers}} tiers**, each {{v:pass_tier_xp}} **pass XP**, earned by the things you do
already: quests, the daily reward, dungeons, bosses, realms and fame. There's no list of chores and
no tier slower than the one before.

Everyone walks the **free track**. For **{{v:pass_price}} gold** a season you can gild it: the
**Gilded track** pays a reward at every tier, among them the season's hand-drawn skins, gold back,
Elder keys, Potions of Max, a character slot, a name colour and the season's grand aura.

> **Never too late.** Gilding is retroactive: every Gilded reward of a tier you've already reached
> is ready the moment you buy it. And if you fall behind, **Momentum** speeds you up (below).

## Opening the pass

- Press **Y** (change it in Options, Controls), or the **Pass** button beside Quests on the HUD.
  The button shows your tier on a crest with a ring of progress, and a badge when rewards wait.
- Or walk up to **the Wayfarer**, a traveller in a wide-brimmed hat and a frost-blue cloak, a lantern hung from his staff, a few steps east
  of the Warcaller at the north point of the Nexus's spawn island. He greets you with what waits.
- `/pass` in the chat tells your tier, the XP to the next and the time left.

The window has three tabs:

- **Rewards**: the season's road, six tiers at a time (scroll with the wheel or the arrows). The
  free reward is above each tier and the Gilded one below; what you've reached and not claimed
  glows. Hover a reward to see it, press it to claim it, or **Claim all**.
- **Earn XP**: what gives pass XP, today's first-dungeon bonus, Momentum and the Gilded boost to
  the community goals.
- **Looks**: the auras and name colours you own, each playing live, to wear or take off.

## Earning pass XP

Pass XP comes on its own as you play; a little "+XP" rises by the Pass button each time.

{{gen:pass_xp}}

A dungeon gives more the harder it is: from Beginner to Endgame. A player of about an hour a day,
most days, reaches tier {{v:pass_tiers}} well before the month is out.

### Momentum

The pass keeps a **pace**: every tier by {{v:pass_pace}}% of the season. While you're a whole tier
or more behind it (you joined late, or missed a week), **all pass XP comes
{{v:pass_momentum}}% faster** until you've caught up. The window shows a flame on the XP bar while
it's on.

## The Gilded pass

Buy it from the Rewards tab for **{{v:pass_price}} gold**, once a season. It lasts until the season
ends and gives, besides its track:

- **Two trips and an Arena save**: `/visit` and `/summon` take you into any one dungeon or instance once; with the Gilded pass, twice. In the Arena, the first defeat of each game is undone: you stay on your feet at full health, invulnerable for 3 seconds.
- **The community goals' loot, boosted**: while your pass is Gilded, the loot of every
  [community goal](community-goals.html) tier you claim is rolled {{v:pass_luck}} Overdrive level
  luckier, and rolled {{v:pass_rolls}} more time.
- A gold crown of light when you buy it, which everyone around you sees.

Gold comes from the [Gold Store](../gold.html), and the Gilded track pays some
of it back.

## Skins, auras and name colours

Frostfall's three skins are drawn for one class each, with their own walk and attack, and stand
bigger than an ordinary character: the **Frostcrown Archmage** (Wizard, at tier 1), the
**Winterguard** (Warrior, tier 10) and the **Aurora Ranger** (Archer, tier 22). Each carries its own
effects: frost motes spiralling round the Archmage and ice in his footsteps, frost mist and
steaming breath round the Winterguard, and the northern lights over the Aurora Ranger, streaming
behind her as she runs. A skin you win is yours on the account: wear it on that class at the
Armoire in the Nexus, or pick it when you make a new character.

**Auras** are new: a living effect round your character that everyone sees, such as falling snow
and a ring of ice, or a turning constellation. Your steps leave a trail of it. **Name colours**
colour your name in the chat. Wear both from the Looks tab, one of each at a time. A look you own
already, coming round again, pays {{v:pass_repeat_fame}} fame instead.

{{gen:pass_looks}}

Auras are hidden while you're invisible, and Options, Visuals turns particles (auras among them)
off.

## When a season ends

A season ends at midnight UTC on its last day, and the next begins at once with a new road and a
new XP bar. Anything you reached and never claimed is paid to you the next time you play (items to
the gift chest in your vault, fame and gold to your account), so nothing is lost. The skins, auras
and name colours stay yours for good.
