Quests are yours alone. **Community goals** belong to everyone: each week the realm gets
**{{v:community_slots}} goals** to reach together, such as defeating Oryx the Mad God a number of
times or clearing a pile of dungeons, and every kill, clear and level anyone makes counts towards
them. Each goal has four **tiers** along the way, and when the realm reaches one, **everyone who
helped** claims its reward. And the realm doesn't have to stop at the goal: every
{{v:community_bonus_step}}% past it is an **{{v:community_bonus_name}}** level, a bonus more for
everyone, up to {{v:community_bonus_max}}%.

> **Help a little, share it all.** You don't need to be the best: once you've added the goal's
> share (a small one, such as two dungeons or twenty gods), you can claim every tier the realm
> reaches, even the ones reached while you were offline.

## The Warcaller

The **Warcaller**, a knight in a plumed helm holding up the realm's violet war banner, stands on
the sand of the Arena island's court in the Nexus, by the Herald and the Arena Leaderboard (east
across the bridge from the spawn). Walk up to him and he tells you
what waits for you, or which goal still needs you; every so often he calls out how one of the goals
stands to everyone nearby. Type `/warcaller` to go to him from anywhere. Stand by him and press **Rally** in the bottom of your HUD to open the
**Community Goals** window.

The window lists the week's goals on the left, with a bar for each, and the goals that ended last
week below them while their rewards can still be claimed. Pick one to see:

- its **bar**, filling as the realm plays, with a gem for each tier: lit once it's reached; and
  beside it the gold **{{v:community_bonus_name}}** track past the goal, a gem for each level;
- the four **tiers** under it, each with its rewards and a **Claim** button once it's yours, and
  the **{{v:community_bonus_name}}** card, which claims every level reached at once;
- **your part**: how much you've added, your place among everyone who helped, and what you still
  need to add to share the rewards;
- the goal's **loot rolls**: everything its rolls can give, each edged in its rarity's colour with
  its chance (hover one for its odds in {{v:community_bonus_name}});
- the **top contributors**, and the prize the top {{v:community_top}} claim once the goal ends.

A tag by the goal's name says when this week's goal was made **harder** or **easier** than the
one written below (see "Goals that fit the realm").

**Claim all** collects everything waiting on every goal at once. The window keeps itself up to date
while it's open, so you can watch the bars move. Type `/goals` to see it all in the chat.

When the realm reaches a tier, a banner at the top of everyone's screen (and a line in the chat)
says so, and another tells you when a reward of yours is waiting.

## How it works

1. **New goals every Monday** at midnight UTC: {{v:community_slots}} of the goals below, each of a
   different kind. A kind comes back only after the others have had their week.
2. **Everything counts as it happens**, in realms and dungeons alike (not the Nexus, your vault or
   what staff spawn). A kill, a dungeon cleared, a realm closed or an Arena wave counts **once** for
   the realm however many of you shared it, and once for each of you that did.
3. **Tiers** are reached at a quarter, half, three quarters and all of the goal. Once you've added
   the goal's share, claim each tier the realm has reached, at any time that week or the next.
4. **{{v:community_bonus_name}}:** past the goal, each {{v:community_bonus_step}}% more is a level,
   {{v:community_bonus_name}} I to {{v:community_bonus_last}} ({{v:community_bonus_max}}% of the
   goal), and each pays the goal's bonus again to everyone who helped.
5. **The top {{v:community_top}}** who added the most claim the top's prize once the goal ends.
   A goal finished early (below) ends there, so its top claim their prize straight away.
6. **Rewards** go straight to you: fame to your account, items to the gift chest in your vault.
   The window and the chat name what each claim gave you. Ended goals' rewards wait {{v:community_claim_weeks}} more week, then go.

## Finished early: a bonus goal

When the realm takes a goal all the way, through its last {{v:community_bonus_name}} level
({{v:community_bonus_max}}% of the goal), before the week is out, that goal is **finished**: it
ends there, its rewards wait to be claimed as an ended goal's do, and a **bonus goal** of another
kind takes its place at once, with its own tiers, levels and loot. A banner tells everyone.

- The bonus goal runs until the usual **Monday reset**, when the new week's goals come as always.
- **The faster a goal is finished, the harder things get.** The bonus goal is bigger than usual by
  how quickly the last one went (finish one in a day and a half and the next asks over twice as
  much), and each quick finish in a row builds on the last, up to {{v:community_early_heat}} times
  the usual size. It all cools off at the Monday reset.
- The finished goal comes back harder too, by the same pace, the next time it's drawn.
- It's never one of the week's other goals, and where it can be, none of last week's or next
  week's either. Finish the bonus goal too and another follows.
- None starts in the last {{v:community_early_hours}} hours of the week.

## Loot rolls

Every goal gives its **own** rewards, and many of them are **loot rolls**: an item drawn from the
goal's own loot table below, from common to divine. Oryx's goal rolls incantations, shards and Lil'
Oryx pets, the dungeon goals keys, the gods' goal stat potions, the forge's goal forging shards,
and so on.

- Each **{{v:community_bonus_name}}** level rolls luckier: the rarer an item, the likelier it gets
  with each level, and the top's prize rolls as lucky as {{v:community_bonus_name}}
  {{v:community_bonus_last}}.
- When you claim, the Warcaller **rolls each one on a reel** over the window, as the death spin
  does: it scrolls past the goal's loot and slows onto what you won, then the next roll starts,
  and what you've won lines up below it. **Stop** (or a click, Space or Enter) lands the roll
  turning, **Skip all** (or Escape) lands them all. The chat and the window say what you got only
  once the last roll has landed. The items are already in your gift chest: the reel only shows
  them.
- A **{{v:community_announce}}** roll or rarer is told to the whole server.

## Goals that fit the realm

The goals size themselves to how the realm plays. When a week's goal ends, the next time it comes
up it's made **harder** if the realm went well past it and **easier** if the realm fell short,
aiming for the realm to end each goal at about {{v:community_adapt_target}}% (a little
{{v:community_bonus_name}}). It moves at most {{v:community_adapt_up}}% harder or
{{v:community_adapt_down}}% easier at a time, and the tiers and levels move with it. The goals
below are as written; the window shows each week's.

## Good to know

- Guests can't collect rewards: register your account first.
- What you add counts for your account, so every character of yours adds to the same share.
- Claims are kept by the server before it pays, so a reward is never lost or paid twice, even if
  you're disconnected right then.
- The goals are sized for the realm as it is, and resize themselves week by week: with everyone
  pitching in, all of them are reachable in a week, and the last tier takes a real push.
- A goal's size is set when its week begins, so it never moves while you're working on it.
