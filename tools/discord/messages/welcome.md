# Welcome to Do Damage
Do Damage is a free Realm of the Mad God private server, run for a small group of players. Most of it plays like the game you know: pick a class, fight your way up through the realm, collect gear, and one death is final for that character. On top of that: 29 classes, free starter gear, the Divine Forge, the Oryx chain all the way to Oryx's Elysium, the Arena, and daily, weekly and monthly quests.

**Play now:** https://dodmg.com/play/ in your browser, or download the desktop client from https://dodmg.com
**New?** Read {#getting-started}, then ask in {#help}.
**Updates:** every update is posted in {#updates}. Type `/updates` to get pinged.

**The rules**
1. Be decent. No harassment, slurs or hate, here or in game.
2. No cheating: no hacked clients, bots or exploits. Found an exploit? Report it in {#bug-reports} instead of using it.
3. No scamming in trades, and no selling items or accounts for real money.
4. Keep channels on topic, and don't spam or advertise.
5. Staff have the last word. If something's wrong, tell a Staff member.

Do Damage is fan-made, not affiliated with DECA Games or Kabam.
