# Getting started
**1. Pick how to play.** Both are the same game, with the same account, characters and vault.
• **Browser:** https://dodmg.com/play/ (nothing to install, always the newest version; it needs WebGL 2, so if it won't start, update your browser and turn on hardware acceleration)
• **Desktop** (Windows, Linux, macOS): download it from https://dodmg.com, unzip it and run it. It updates itself. If Windows warns about it, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

**2. Make an account.** **Play** starts you as a guest straight away, but guests can't buy from merchants, and a guest is lost if you clear your browser or change computer. **Register** ("Save progress") any time to keep everything: a name, an email and a password of at least 6 characters.

**3. Make a character and type `/gear`.** Every class is open from the start. In the Nexus, below level 20, `/gear` gives you free starter gear for your class, plus an XP Booster and a Loot Drop Potion. It works once per character, so keep a few inventory slots free.

**4. Into the realm.** `/realm` takes you to the busiest realm. Start by the beaches and move inland as you level, and take the dungeon portals monsters drop. At level 20, try `/godlands` with friends. Put anything you want to keep in your vault (`/vault`) first: a death takes everything your character carries.

**Good to know**
• `/commands` lists every command, and **J** opens your quests and daily reward.
• Everything in game costs fame, which you earn by playing. There's nothing to buy.
• The wiki has every class, item, dungeon and drop: https://dodmg.com/wiki/ (or `/wiki <term>` in {#bot-commands}).
• The full guide: https://dodmg.com/wiki/getting-started.html
