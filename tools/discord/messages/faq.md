# FAQ
**Is it free?** Yes. Every shop in the game takes fame, which you earn by playing.

**Browser or desktop?** The same game and the same account. The desktop client is faster and can unlock its frame rate; the browser needs nothing installed. Phones and tablets can't really play: the game needs a keyboard and mouse.

**How do updates work?** The desktop client updates itself in the background and switches over at your next portal. The browser is always the newest. Server updates don't kick anyone. Every update is posted in {#updates} and on https://dodmg.com/changelog.html

**I can't sign in.** After 10 wrong passwords, signing in to that account is blocked for 15 minutes. "Account in use" usually means the game still has you signed in after a crash: wait a minute and try again.

**I lost my items.** When a character dies, everything it carries is gone; only the vault, potion storage and gift chests are safe. If something vanished another way, post in {#bug-reports}.

**How do I report a bug?** In {#bug-reports}, or as an issue at https://github.com/Meapy/Do-Damage/issues. Say what happened, where and when, your character's name, and whether you play in the browser or on the desktop, with a screenshot if you can. `/checkid` in game shows ids that help find you.

**Where do I read about something?** `/wiki <term>` in {#bot-commands}, or https://dodmg.com/wiki/. Still stuck? Ask in {#help}.
