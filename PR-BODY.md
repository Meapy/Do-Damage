Release 2.6.4: the Crucible, a boss-only tower over the Arena (five champions, floors that change as they fight, a white bag each), a Gilded Realm Pass can rally a community goal (everyone's help counts double until it ends, and the top prize is theirs too) and shows the way to a dungeon's boss,, the chat's emoji are redrawn and brought up to date (salute included), every Black Between and Crucible boss drops itself as a pet (1%), pets level up as the Pen Keeper feeds them (fame to feed, a pen grown with fame or gold for bigger pets) and each heals its own way with less MP, and the Mechanic is reworked (turrets built where you aim, up to three, overclocked near you, a blast at the end; every tier rebalanced and redrawn) with five new skins at the Armoire, and the Empowered Marble Colossus breaks his hall open as he rises, and a Gold Store button in the game (the gold + and the menus); deploys client and world servers (players are not disconnected; the Crucible and the turrets need both)

## For players

- **The Crucible:** a tower of nothing but bosses, over the Arena. Up to six climb its five floors together, each floor a shape of its own (a keep with round bastions, a ragged atoll, a forge hall in a cross, the rings of an orrery over open sky, a star of marble), and on each a champion arrives: **Ferrogrim, the Iron Warden** (iron grates rise and sentinels hold them, blades of fire sweep the floor, his hammer whirls), **Nerissa, the Tidecaller** (bands of tide, then the sea floods everything but the coral isles, a whirlpool, a maelstrom), **Pyrrhax, the Forge Tyrant** (lava veins, a meteor storm, the floor's rim crumbling as lava pours in, a molten core), **Chronarch, the Hour Thief** (hour and minute hands sweeping, the clock's hours chiming half the floor at a time, echoes of him, the clock running backwards) and **Vaelith, the Crucible Sovereign**, on the biggest floor, who raises the ghosts of the four below one by one, the Apex reshaping itself for each (a fortress with pits in its yard, isles in a deep sea, a cross with furnaces floating off it, rings joined by spokes), then shatters it and fights Unbound on a halo of void round himself. Every champion's floor reshapes itself with each phase the same way (Ferrogrim: towers, a gear, a star fort; Nerissa: a wheel of isles, coral arms, a ring round a vortex; Pyrrhax: a grate over the sky, an anvil, the crucible alone; Chronarch: twelve petals, an hourglass, a coil) and then holds still, anyone cut off is thrown back onto the floor, and the ground shakes gently and at most every few seconds.
  - Every phase is called out on screen and each champion has its own aura (forge sparks, circling spray, embers and smoke, turning gears, falling feathers of flame) that builds as it's worn down (molten iron dripping and the forge beating through the floor, ripples and columns of sea, scorched floor and lava bursting up, the clock's face and time tearing both ways, the tower's sigil and pillars of light, at a third and at two thirds of its health gone) and a burst for each phase. Your pet and summoned allies climb with you. Between floors you're healed and the Ascension Sigil turns; then you climb on foot: a way up builds itself out of the sky ahead of you, winding up to the next floor in that floor's stone with its pillars along it, the floor comes together round you as you reach it, and everything behind breaks away and falls. Shots fly on over the gaps in its floors and the sky beside them instead of stopping at the edge. It has its own sky, which changes floor by floor, its own music and a leaderboard (`/crucible status`).
  - **Afflictions and signatures:** the shots aimed at you carry each champion's afflictions (Ferrogrim: Bleeding shards, hammer waves that break armour and daze, Iron Tempest's stun; Nerissa: bleeding tridents, confusing quick ones, slowing orbs; Pyrrhax: magma that leaves you Weak, cinders that make you Sick; Chronarch: bleeding hand-blades, orbs that slow and daze; Vaelith: lances that leave you Exposed, void embers that blind, break armour and Curse, feathers that Quiet), and each champion has a heavy signature shot: Rending Volley (7 shards, Bleeding 3 s), Undertow (3 great orbs, Quiet and Paralyzed 0.8 s), Slag Ball (3 globs, Paralyzed 1 s and Armor Broken), Stolen Moment (2 quick orbs, Silenced and Paralyzed 0.6 s), Final Decree (3 huge lances through armour, Stunned 1.5 s and Armor Broken). The big rings and spirals carry nothing. Their kits fire about a tenth faster. Minions and echoes carry their champion's afflictions. A champion that falls over the sky leaves a landing of the floor's ground under its loot, with a bridge back to the floor.
  - **Rushers and livelier phases:** every champion sends rushers out round its floor now and then, at most three about at once: small, quick, frail things that run at the nearest player and bite up close with their champion's affliction (Iron Hounds bleed, Brine Darters slow, Cinder Imps sicken, Clockwork Mites daze, Gilded Wisps quiet). Hour Hands has both its hands again, the broad hour hand and the quick minute hand, burning whoever they cross (backwards and faster in Reversal, and in Vaelith's Echo of the Hours). The damaging floors move faster (rings, blades, tides, whirlpools, veins, chimes), and the phases that had gone quiet have something of their own: two blades turning against Iron Tempest's spirals, Lockdown's anvil cross, Pyrrhax's hammer falling where each player stands in Hammer, embers raining as the forge pours, the broken clock striking half its hours at random in Time Fracture.
  - **Harder, dodge or pay:** the aimed shots and signatures hit about as hard as Oryx 3's (Ferrogrim's shard fans 210 and hammer waves 270, Nerissa's tridents 230, Pyrrhax's magma 290, Chronarch's hand-blades 240, Vaelith's lances 280, the signatures 130 a shard up to Final Decree's 380 through armour; were 95-260), the rings and spirals a little more (140-175), minions and echoes about a fifth more. The champions have about 15% more health (Ferrogrim 175,000, Nerissa 210,000, Pyrrhax 245,000, Chronarch 280,000, Vaelith 400,000), and their last phases lead you (Iron Tempest's hammer waves, quick tridents in the Maelstrom, cinders in Molten Core, hand-blades in Reversal, Unbound's feathers). Chronarch's floor is drawn at full size as a clock face again: smooth rings and spokes, a mark at each hour (the quarters longer), a ring round the hub and two hands at ten past ten.
  - **Room to fight, bullets over floors:** the floors are 1.4 times as big as first drawn and their shapes hold still once formed; ground about to fall cracks 1.6 seconds ahead and throwing you back onto the floor hits for 50 (was 120). Each phase has at most one floor hazard, slower and shown first (Ferrogrim one slow molten ring at a time and two blades instead of three, Nerissa one wide-gapped wave every 6.5 s and two arms of tide, Pyrrhax two lava veins with a full second's glow and no veins under the meteors, Chronarch only the hour hand, Vaelith one hazard per echo), and the champions' shots carry the fight instead: two spirals turning opposite ways (Iron Tempest, Maelstrom), double rings with the second in the first's gaps, Ferrogrim's turning cross of hammer waves, Nerissa's flower of swaying orbs, Pyrrhax's rings of slow magma and turning walls of cinders, Chronarch's clock hand of blades sweeping round him and his gears running backwards, Vaelith's flower of feathers and double rings of void embers.
  - **Getting in:** the portal tells you how often you can climb and to bring friends before it lets you in. Reach round 50 in the Arena (rounds reached before count) or hold a Gilded Realm Pass. **One climb a day**, two with the Pass (a new day at 00:00 UTC); leaving the lobby before the climb begins costs nothing. Its portal is sunk into the Nexus's shrine across Realm 2's vortex from the Arena's (a vortex of fire in a lip of iron and gold, the floors' gems lighting in turn, embers rising off it), or type `/crucible`. Falling sends you to the Nexus instead of killing you. There's no fame for a climb: the champions' loot is the reward.
  - **White bags**, 1% each, an ability for a class each, with a power of its own: **Ferrogrim's Bastion Turret** (Mechanic: his sentinel, firing shards and slamming the ground, breaking armor and Armoring allies beside it), **Nerissa's Tidepearl** (Druid: a substitute of five tridents and a heal, then three waves of tide that hurt enemies and heal allies), **Pyrrhax's Meteor Mortar** (Pirate: a molten shell arcing to where you aim, then four meteors and a burning crater), **Chronarch's Coat of Stolen Hours** (Blademaster: Armored and Damaging, then time stops round you while the clock's hand sweeps twice) and **Vaelith's Final Aria** (Bard: Inspired, then four verses of rings of light, the last a crescendo that breaks armor and sends allies berserk).
  - The wiki has a page for it (The Crucible), and the items are on their gear pages.
- **Boss pets:** every boss of **the Black Between** and every **Crucible** champion has a **1% chance** to drop a little copy of itself as a pet, in a white bag: Lil' Vesper, Lil' Aurel and the Lil' Mysterious One, Lil' Ferrogrim, Lil' Nerissa, Lil' Pyrrhax, Lil' Chronarch and Lil' Vaelith. Each fights as its boss does, with the boss's own shots in its patterns: Vesper's weaving dusk volleys, turning rings and bursting dusk mines; Aurel's crescents, thrown sword, divine lightning and the Sunwheel; the Mysterious One's two eyes at once and his black hole; and the champions take on their last form every 10 seconds to throw out its spiral (Iron Tempest, Maelstrom, Molten Core, Reversal, Unbound). They're as strong as the Lil' Oryxes, about 1,200 (Ferrogrim's) to 3,600 (the Mysterious One's) damage a second at their last level, and the champions' help you too (Armored, healing, Berserk, Speedy or Damaging now and then). The wiki's Pets and Crucible pages list them.
- **Pets grow:** every pet starts at level 1 and levels up as the **Pen Keeper** feeds it, to its size's last level (Tiny 10, Small 15, Medium 20, Large 25, the Lil' Oryxes and boss pets 30): its damage goes from 60% of its kit's to 120%, its healing up to 160%, and it grows a quarter bigger. It eats gear (tiered by tier; untiered by how rare its bag is, from 150 for a brown bag's to 2,000 for a white bag's and 3,000 for Ascended gear), potions, Superburgers (1,500), Ambrosia (5,000), Shards of the Mad God (200 each, also the ones he keeps for the account) and other pets' generators (by size, plus half the food a fed one holds). **Feeding costs fame**: 3 for each 100 food at level 1, 15% more each level after, so a whole climb is about 185 fame for a Tiny pet up to 6,820 for an Oryx-sized one. **The pen** feeds Tiny pets at first; each bigger size is bought once per account, for fame or gold (Small 2,000 or 200, Medium 5,000 or 400, Large 10,000 or 700, Oryx 20,000 or 1,000). Any pet can still be put in and fights at its level. His window is new: the pet's level and food bar, damage and healing now and at its last level, the inventory's food with what each is worth, what it costs and what level it reaches, the pen's sizes and the prices to grow it. Generators keep their pet's level (shown in the corner and the tooltip); pets from before levels came back about as strong as they were, raised ones at their last level.
- **Every pet heals its own way:** by its size, how hard it hits for its size (the hardest hitters heal a little less), what it is (healers half again as much) and a roll of its own, every 1.5 to 3 seconds; and every pet restores about half as much MP a second as it heals HP (40% to 60%, mages and such 50% to 65%), where before every pet of a size healed the same, as much MP as HP.
- **Realm Pass:** a **Gilded** pass has two new perks.
  - **Rally a community goal.** Open the Warcaller's window, pick a goal and press **Rally x2** (once on each goal). From then until the goal ends everyone's help with that goal counts **double**, in the goal's total and in each helper's own share, so the whole realm climbs its tiers twice as fast. The realm is told ("Name rallied the realm!") in a banner and in chat, the goal's card burns gold with "x2 · rallied by Name" (and "+N" when others rallied it too), and the goal shows an **x2** in the list. Rallying a goal that is already burning changes nothing for the others, but still shares you its top prize. The one who rallied also shares the goal's **top prize** once it ends, whatever their place in its top 3 (they still need the goal's minimum share of their own). Without a Gilded pass the button is there, dimmed with a lock, and says what it takes.
  - **The way to the boss.** In a dungeon, a trail of small gold arrows runs along the **shortest walk** to its boss, flowing toward it and fading out ahead, with a chip at the foot of the view: the boss's picture, an arrow turned the way to walk and how many tiles are left. It finds its way round walls, goes through doors only when there's no other way, follows the boss as it moves and goes away once you're within 30 tiles of it or anyone has started fighting it. Options, Gameplay has a switch to turn it off.
  - Both are on the pass's **Gilded perks** tab and the wiki's Realm Pass, Community Goals and Dungeons pages.
- **Mechanic:** the class is reworked to be worth playing.
  - **Building:** a turret blueprint throws a crate to where you aim (up to 8 tiles away; "You can't build there." on a wall or water), which lands and unfolds into a turret on a gear plate. Its head turns to the nearest enemy in reach (about 9 tiles), leads it as it moves and keeps to it, and its shots leave the barrel's tip; it sweeps slowly while idle. Up to **three** stand at once: building a fourth blows up the oldest. A blueprint can be used once a second.
  - **Overclock:** while you're within 7 tiles of a turret, a cable of light with pulses runs from it to you and it fires half again as often.
  - **Blast:** when its time is up (12 seconds at T0 up to 20 at T8) it blows up, hitting every enemy within 2.5 to 3.5 tiles for 150 (T0) up to 550 (T8); a ring shows the blast's reach for its last 3 seconds and a timer arc runs round its plate.
  - **Rebalanced:** every tier fires every 0.6 seconds (0.4 overclocked): T0 42, T1 51, T2 60, T3 two piercing thorns of 36, T4 a piercing bubble of 84, T5 two hellfire bolts of 50, T6 a piercing lance of 114, T7 two jade darts of 66, T8 two piercing bolts of lightning of 75. The War Turret fires 30 every 0.2 seconds. Mana 40 (T0) to 110 (T8). Before, a turret stood at the Mechanic's feet and fired one shot every 0.5 seconds (50 at T0 up to 180 at T8) for 10 to 30 seconds, with no limit to how many stood at once; each turret now does a little less alone but more with the Mechanic beside it and its blast, and the cap keeps a stack of them from melting everything.
  - **Art:** every blueprint, turret base and head, and shot is redrawn per tier (scrap, iron, steel, a living stump, coral, black iron and hellfire, white gold and a prism, sandstone and jade, a storm crown; the War Turret a brass gatling). The tooltip shows the shots, the overclock, the blast, the range and the cap of three.
  - **Ferrogrim's Bastion Turret** (the Crucible's white bag for the Mechanic) is a turret like the others now: thrown where you aim, one of your three, overclocked beside you and blowing up at the end (600 within 4 tiles), its head turning to fire three iron shards of 100 every 0.7 seconds, and still slamming the ground every 3 seconds (380 round it, armor broken, allies Armored). It's redrawn as a black iron keep with a forge in its gate under a head of three glowing barrels, and it can be used once a second. Before, it stood at your feet, fired 3 shards of 160 and, with no cooldown or cap, could be stacked without end.
  - **Skins:** the Armoire sells four 8 by 8 Mechanic skins for 200 gold (the **Steamwright** in a tall top hat with goggles on its band, a curled moustache, an oxblood frock coat and a wrench; the **Scrapper** in a welding helmet with a white-hot slit, hazard stripes, an oxygen tank and a blade of rusty scrap; the **Arcwright** in storm blue with a lamp on the hat, cyan goggles, a lightning bolt, a copper coil on the back and a crackling blade; the **Clockwork Mechanic**, a brass automaton with one red eye, steel arms, a gear on its chest and a winding key in its back) and the 16 by 16 **Titan Artificer** for 800: a Mechanic in a walking exo-frame, a brass diving helm with a porthole burning cyan, great brass shoulder plates, copper arms, a reactor in the chest and a boiler glowing on the back, a red scarf and a great wrench it swings overhead and slams down. The ring of walls cracks apart from the north round to the south with lightning from the Colossus, the marble floor builds itself outwards half as wide again (about 48 by 41 tiles instead of 32 across), dark at its edge with the light flooding out behind it, new walls rise along the rim as the floor reaches them with sparks racing along them, and he hurls the braziers' fire out to light them on the new rim; it ends in a stamp and a shockwave out to the new walls ("The halls widen!"). - **Testing was out of reach for browser and https players** (everyone on the Rust client): the gateway took any target on port 2051 for its own world proxy (`Router::is_proxy` compared only the port), so `wss://play.dodmg.com/ws/169.58.244.146:2051` went straight to one of Europe 2's world servers, and Testing never saw the sign-in. Now only a loopback host on that port is this machine's proxy (`is_local_proxy`, with a test); other machines' world servers are connected to as they are. Found when PR #81, on Testing, still sent its admin to Europe 2.
- **Rank 6 lets you into Testing too.** Testing's door (`HelloHandler`) only took the account's `admin` flag, and the owner's account is rank 6 without it, so it was sent on to Europe 2 even with the gateway fixed. `DbAccount.IsAdmin` (the flag or rank 6, as the sprite review, `/autoaim` and the Champions board already count it) now decides there and in `MessageHandlers.IsEnabledOrAdminOnly`, whose Testing check also refused a whitelisted player unless they had the flag as well.
- **Files:** `.github/workflows/testing.yml` (new), `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (a comment), `deploy/deploy.py`, `deploy/remote/install-site.sh`, `client-rs/gateway/src/world.rs`, `source/Shared/database/account/DbAccount.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/net/handlers/HelloHandler.cs` and `MessageHandlers.cs`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md` ("Pull requests on Testing", "Changes from upstream").
- **Redeploys:** the world servers (rolling), the gateway (a new slot while the old one hands over) and the website machine (the new `install-site.sh` and `testing.txt`); nobody is disconnected. Redis: none. Until the gateway is deployed, "Testing (admin)" goes to Europe 2 for browser and https clients.
- **For the owner:** it's off until set up (SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Pull requests on Testing"): create the `testing` environment with `VPS_SSH_KEY`, `VPS_KNOWN_HOSTS` and `VPS_CONFIG` (best a key of its own that only the website machine takes, since a pull request's workflow can read that environment's secrets), then set `TESTING_DEPLOY_ENABLED` to `true`.
- **A test client per pull request:** the workflow's `client` job (Windows, no secrets) builds the same commit's desktop client and keeps it 14 days as the run's artifact, `Do-Damage-testing-pr<number>`, so client and server changes are tried together on Testing. Unsigned, so it never updates itself and no player's client takes it. It signs in at the repository variable `TESTING_CLIENT_ACCOUNT`, by default `http://play.dodmg.com:8080` (plain TCP, straight to Testing, which works before this release's gateway is live; the password goes unencrypted); `https://play.dodmg.com/api` once it is.
- **Not done:** nothing is published from a pull request. Testing plays on the live Redis, so pull requests that change what's saved stay drafts until tried on an isolated stack.
