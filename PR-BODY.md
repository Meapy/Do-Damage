Release 2.5.8: the Marble Colossus stays in its room, a stronger Superior staff and monsters that fight as they did in the original game (deploys the client and the world server; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Dungeons:** the Marble Colossus (and its Elder version) in the Lost Halls stays inside its boss room: it no longer follows players out through the doors.
- **Dungeons:** some monsters fight as they did in the original game again (Elder versions too): the Snake Pit's hidden dart throwers fire bleeding darts east and west across the room every 3 s or so while you fight the Snakepit Guard; answer the wounded Thessal wrongly or not at all within 12 s and she rages for 6 s (rings of Yellow Walls and Thunder Swirls, Super Trident crosses, Coral Bombs) before leaving; the Ghost of Roger (Davy Jones' Locker) follows you, and below 40% health charges with Skull Bolt spreads and calls two Violent Spirits; the Undead Lair's Vampires and Vampire Kings bite with their heavy Green Bolt (100/120) within 3 tiles.
- **Oryx's Castle, Oryx 1 and Oryx 2** fight as they did in the original game. The two Stone Guardians wake together, wander or charge on their own and every few seconds join in one of four formations round the middle of their room; when one falls the other shelters 5 s, healing, then fights alone and throws its sword, staying invulnerable until the sword is knocked to half and flies back. The castle's living floors throw Quiet Bombs from a distance and fire silencing rings up close, Suits of Armor wake when you come within 4 tiles and heal once at 45%, and Insect Commanders send swarms that bite up close. Oryx 1 picks his attacks at random from nine (minions, Circle Combat, Artifact Dance, Hadouken, Minion Toss, Bullet Dance, Orbitals, Chase Course, White Planet), never one of his last three, with 5 invulnerable seconds between them and his shield dropping for a second or so at a time; below 55% only the harder ones, and below 6% he heals a little, grows and hunts you with his Assassins. Oryx 2 throws each of his six stars at its own reach and rings of fire bolts; his Henchmen bring three of his four minions each, back 7 s after they die (the Bile's puddles sicken and slow), and at 30% he sends them away, floods the cellar with silencing and weakening spinners, then circles close with Scarabs all round. **Oryx 4** in his Inferno now fights Oryx 1's new fight, burning: the nine attacks with 4 s of warning (Oryx 1's 5 s), hellfire bolts throughout, an Infernal Officer and a Brimstone Major back with every wave of minions, red Infernal Elements in his Artifact Dance (four close that aim at you, three fast ones further out), and his last stand from 10% with his Assassins and court; in an arena he fights as before.
- **Realm:** the Weretiger fires three claws (was one), the enraged Desert Werewolf a stronger fireball every 0.6 s, the Werelion's ring its Black Missiles (35, were the 22 Grey ones), a Drake Baby below 65% shoots back while calling its mother, the Giant Crab's beams aim where you are going, and the Warrior Wasp leads its shots one time in five (was every shot).
- **Items:** Superior (Oryx the Celestial Mad God's staff) does 60-95 damage a shot (was 45-75), each hit strips 1% of the enemy's defense for 5 seconds (up to 10 stacks, 10%; it falls off if you stop hitting), and it drops 1% of the time (was 0.4%). The Chaotic Scripture's Chaos Beam has an effect of its own: a column of violet light slams down onto the cursor, a shockwave runs out to the blast's 2.5-tile edge, and a rift with the eight arrows of chaos stays torn open about 1.4 s, crackling (damage unchanged).

## Details

### Marble Colossus stays in its room

- **What changed:** new behavior `StayInRoom(radius)` pulls its host back to within `radius` tiles of its spawn point after the movement behaviours have run. The Colossus uses 12 (the round boss room is 31 tiles across, centred on where it rises).
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/behaviors/StayInRoom.cs`, `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.LostHalls.cs`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected). Nothing in Redis.
- **Not done or known to be off:** not tried in game yet.
### Superior staff buffed

- **What changed:** `Superior` (0x70a7) does 60-95 and has `<ArmorShred percent="1" max="10" duration="5"/>` (new `Item.ArmorShred`): each of its hits that leaves the enemy alive calls `Enemy.StackShred`, adding a stack of 1% (up to 10) and renewing all of them for 5 s, on top of `ShredArmor` (the mystic orb's shred: the defense stat itself is lowered, so the client's damage numbers follow). With no hit for 5 s every stack goes; a hit after that starts at one. Oryx the Celestial Mad God's drop is `0.01` (was `0.004`). Description says so.
- **Files:** `source/Shared/resources/xml/prod/EmbeddedData_EquipCXML.xml`, `source/Shared/resources/Item.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/objects/Enemy.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/objects/player/Player.Projectiles.cs`, `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxSanctuary.cs`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected). The item's XML is also in the client's data, but it ignores the new element, so the client only needs the new description for its tooltip. Nothing in Redis.
- **Not done or known to be off:** not tried in game; the wiki's measured gear tables aren't re-measured.

### Behaviours checked against the original server's

- **What changed:** our behaviours were compared, Shoot by Shoot, with the official release 7.0 server data (about 2013) in dginovker/Open-RotMG-Server (`data/reference/release-7.0`, branch `docs/mpgh-behavior-reference`), reading its `behavior.cc` for the defaults (unset `cooldown` 1 s, unset `angle` 9°, `type="auto"` spreads starting at `defaultAngle`). Of its 736 monsters with behaviours, 191 shoot as ours do and 271 differ; most of those are bosses we run a later official rework of (Tomb, Oryx 3, Abyss, Mad Lab, Stheno, Ghost Ship and so on) or our own changes, kept as they are: the 2013 versions would be a step back (Oryx 1, 2 and the castle were then taken after all: next part). The 42 with attacks there and no behaviour here are never spawned. Taken: slips where ours fires the wrong projectile, count or chance with the same projectile data (Warrior Wasp, Weretiger, Desert Werewolf, Werelion, Drake Baby, Giant Crab, Lair Vampire, Lair Vampire King), and three that did less than the original (the Snakepit Dart Throwers never fired, and are now ordered half a second after the Guard rises, as ordered in the same tick they saw no Guard and went back to sleep; the wounded Thessal's wrong answer did nothing, the Ghost of Roger stood still with one attack). Details in SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Behaviours checked against the original server's". The test bot's new `--shots` logs every enemy volley.
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Midland.cs`, `BehaviorDb.Lowland.cs`, `BehaviorDb.UndeadLair.cs`, `BehaviorDb.SnakePit.cs`, `BehaviorDb.OceanTrench.cs`, `BehaviorDb.DavyJones.cs`; `client-rs/bot/src/main.rs` (test tool only), `client-rs/AGENTS.md`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected). The client's code is unchanged (the bot isn't shipped); nothing in Redis. The wiki doesn't describe monster attacks, so it's unchanged.
- **Not done or known to be off:** only `Shoot`s were compared mechanically; movement, grenades, tosses and minions only where read by hand for the monsters above. Ghost of Roger keeps our Lost Soul spawning (3, every 5 s) rather than the original's (4, every second), as our Lost Souls shoot and the original's didn't.

### Chaotic Scripture's Chaos Beam effect

- **What changed:** the beam (`Chaotic Scripture Effect`'s `AllyAOE`) now sends `ShowEffect` 41 (Relic) look 29 first, centred on the strike with its radius and the owner as `SourceId` (so another player's beam fades with Options, Visuals), then the old lightning and blast ring as before. `AllyAOE` takes an optional `fx` look number for this. The Rust client's new look `CHAOS` (`particles/relic.rs`): a solid column of violet light with a white core falling from 9 tiles up in 90 ms, sparks spiralling down round it, then a white flash, violet sparkers out to the edge, shards and dark smoke; two shockwave rings; a new `Rift` decal (darkened ground, seven glowing jagged tears, the eight turning arrows of chaos, a void at the heart) for 1.4 s; and an aura crackling arcs along the floor for 1.1 s. After look 29 the client skips the plain lightning (11) and blast (5) on the same object for 0.5 s, so only the new look is drawn. Look 29 is noted in `Player.RelicPowers.cs`'s `Fx` so it isn't reused.
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/behaviors/new/allies/AllyAOE.cs`, `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Allies.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/objects/player/Player.RelicPowers.cs`; `client-rs/app/src/particles.rs`, `client-rs/app/src/particles/relic.rs`; `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the client and the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected). Older clients ignore look 29 and keep drawing the old lightning and ring. Nothing in Redis. The wiki doesn't describe the effect's look, so it's unchanged.
- **Not done or known to be off:** seen with the beam's object `/spawn`ed (no owner, so no damage), not cast by a Priest on enemies.

### Oryx 1, Oryx 2 and Oryx's Castle as the original server ran them

- **What changed:** the three were ported from the release 7.0 server data (`oryxChamber.xml`, `oryxWineCellar.xml`, `oryxCastle.xml`, and `testingObjects.xml` for Oryx 2 and the Henchman), keeping our health (`BossStats`), loot, portals, announcements and Oryx 2's arena version. Ours were simplified versions with parts missing or broken: Oryx 1 cycled five fixed phases and his minion `Reproduce`s never fired with a player near; the Wine Cellar's Bile of Oryx and its puddles were never spawned, Vintners fired every 0.25 s, Abominations only within 1 tile, Blasters and Scarabs never went away; the Stone Guardians had health phases instead of the duo fight the TaskMaster directs. **Oryx 1:** nine attacks drawn at random, never one of his last three, 5 s invulnerable before each (none after the Hadouken), shield cycles, a different pool below 55%, and a last stand below 6% with his Assassins, all with the original's helpers. **Oryx 2:** his six stars at their own reaches, Fire Bolt rings, up to three Henchmen whose minions come back 7 s after dying, and at 30% the Henchmen leave, silencing and weakening rings, then strafing with Scarabs. **Castle:** Stone Guardians that wake together and join the TaskMaster's four formations, the survivor healing then throwing its sword; living floors, Suits of Armor and Insect swarms as in the original. NR-CORE (cp-nilly/NR-CORE) was checked too: its repository has no behaviours (they came in a forum resource pack), and the descendants that carry them (Valor-Server, UTR-Source) and Nilly's older source share our lineage and are simpler, so nothing was taken from them. **Oryx 4:** Oryx 1's fight became a builder (`OryxFight(OryxSpec)`), which Oryx 4 now fights outside an arena with shorter pauses, All In at 10%, hellfire, his court with the minions and his Infernal Elements (the Guardian Elements' dance, aimed); his intro, health, loot and portal are kept, his old cycle stays for arenas only, and Oryx 1's helpers orbit whichever Oryx is near. Details in SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Oryx 1, Oryx 2 and Oryx's Castle as the original server ran them"; the wiki's endgame tips describe the new fights.
- **Sides:** servers only (world server); no client change, nobody disconnected, no Redis data.
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxChamber.cs` (new: Oryx 1 and his helpers), `BehaviorDb.Oryx.cs` (Oryx 2 and the cellar), `BehaviorDb.OryxCastle.cs`, `BehaviorDb.OryxDoomed.cs` (the White Moon serves Oryx 1 and 4; Oryx 4's unused Guardian Elements and the unused Oryx 5 sword went); `logic/transitions/FreshRandomTransition.cs`, `logic/behaviors/RememberPicks.cs` and `KeepMinions.cs` (new); `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/endgame.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the website (the wiki). Nothing in Redis; the client is unchanged.
- **Not done or known to be off:** the castle's Knight, Brute, Eye Warrior and Pet stay as ours (their 7.0 data is half-written). The original's 45% chance of a lifeless Suit of Armor was left out. The guardian's sword wait is capped at 20 s (the original waited for good). The Wine Cellar places 44 Henchmen, each now keeping its trio of minions (as the original), so the cellar is busier than ours was. Our Oryx 2 had an invulnerable call for guards at 55%; the original has none, so he now takes damage steadily until 30%.
