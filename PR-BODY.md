Release 2.6.6: 

## For players

### The Crucible
- **New shots for every champion:** every shot is redrawn at twice the detail, each champion's in its own look (Ferrogrim's hot iron, Nerissa's sea, Pyrrhax's melt, Chronarch's clockwork, Vaelith's gold and flame), and the shots aimed at you leave a trail of sparks in the champion's colour. Each kind of shot now looks like what it does: rings of rivets, embers, cogs and stars to weave through; red-hot splinters, violet riptide tridents, whirlpools, slag boulders, pocket watches and winged lances for the signatures. Some are new and don't fly straight: Ferrogrim's **thrown hammers** and Chronarch's **rewinding blades** spin out and come back, Nerissa's **tide crests** roll out and wash back, Pyrrhax's **meteor fragments** weave as they burn and Vaelith's **halo shards** loop round where he threw them.
- **More to dodge in the quieter phases:** Ferrogrim sheds rivets as he charges, throws his hammer over the walls in Lockdown and three at a time in the Iron Tempest; Nerissa throws tide crests in the Tidal Lanes, Rising Tide, the Whirlpool and the Tsunami (and an undertow in Rising Tide); Pyrrhax adds meteor fragments and slag boulders to the Meteor Storm and pours molten drops in the Crucible Pours; Chronarch throws rewinding blades in every phase; Vaelith adds halo shards to his Decree and Final Aria, rings of stars to the echoes, and stars, quills and halo shards to the Crucible Breaks. Every champion bursts out two rings (the second in the first's gaps) as it rises into a new phase.

### Gold Store
- PayPal payments for gold name the account the gold goes to: the item reads "1,000 Gold for <name>" on the receipt and in PayPal.

### Scrying Glass
- **The boss fight in the big view:** the top left of the picture shows the quest of the player watched, as their own top left shows it once it's in sight: its picture, name and health bar, and under it the five who did the most damage (class, damage, assist credit and share), with the watched player's own row under their name when they're not among them.

### Realm Pass
- **60 tiers, more loot:** the pass goes to tier 60 (from 40), each tier now 2,000 XP (was 1,000), so the whole track takes three times the XP (120,000 instead of 40,000). Nobody drops a tier: XP already earned this season is doubled to match, so everyone stays where they were. Every free tier pays now: pet food, fame, potions, Elder keys, clovers, three pets and three white bags, the season's aura at 40 and a **pet effect** at 50. The Gilded track adds four more pets (three boss pets), a white bag, a second Potion of Maxy, Royal Pet Feasts, two Growth Tonics, a Backpack and, at tier 60, the season's grand pet effect, the **Burning Amulet** and 1,500 fame. The Wayfarer's Cache can hold pet food and, rarely, the Prismatic pet effect.
- **Pet effects:** a new kind of look, for your pet as an aura is for you: Frostpaw (frosty paw prints and drifting snow), Winter's Crown (ice shards circling over its head), Kindled (a soft flame and rising embers), Phoenix Wings (beating wings of fire), Starlit (twinkling stars and a trail of stardust), Moonlit Orbit (three crescent moons circling it) and Prismatic (a rainbow ribbon). The pass's new **Pets** tab shows each one on your own pet, to put on or take off; everyone sees it on whichever pet you have out. The first one you win goes on by itself.

### Pets
- **Pet food without fame:** the Realm Pass's Pet Biscuit (750 food), Hearty Pet Stew (2,500) and Royal Pet Feast (7,500) feed a pet at the Pen Keeper for no fame at all.
- **Growth Tonic:** grows a Tiny, Small or Medium pet at its last level to the next size for free (growing it otherwise costs fame or gold), from the Pen Keeper's window.

### Website
- **dodmg.com redesigned:** the 3D version from 2.6.5 read as basic and tacky, so the site is laid out anew: big pixel headings, sections divided by hairlines instead of glowing glass cards, in the site's own violet night with ember and gold. At the top of the home page Oryx the Ascended God's fight plays in 3D, everyone built cube by cube from their sprites in the game with the game's black outline: his Wardens circling him, then his Radiant Cross, Firmament and Divine Hunt, the Call of the Host, the Transcendence, the halo of the Ascension, the gold Apotheosis and Wrath, the Last Judgement with the lava closing in, the Concession through every Oryx he was, and his fall. His Host walk about and shoot, the Noble and Cardinal rise beneath him, his splats hatch Ichor Sprites, and a party of six classes (Wizard, Archer, Priest, Knight, Warrior, Rogue) strafes, dodges his shots, fights him and his minions with damage numbers and their abilities; his taunts show beside him. Below: the screenshots large, the six Oryx stages as rows each with its boss, the features in two columns, browser and desktop side by side, the keys, the newest changes, the questions and a closing call over the Oryx screenshot. The wiki, gold store and changelog keep their look, without a 3D scene behind them.

### The Crucible: more shots, and new shots for every champion

- **Asked:** more shots for each champion where it's quiet, and every champion's projectiles cooler, more interesting and unique.
- **Art (`tools/crucible/small.py`, `shots()`):** the shots are drawn at 16x16 on `Crucible16x16.png` (were 8x8 on `Crucible8x8.png`; the client draws a 16x16 shot at half scale, so they're the same size on the ground with twice the detail). Redrawn: Iron Shard, Hammer Wave, Trident Bolt, Tide Orb, Bubble, Magma Ball, Cinder, Gear Shot, Chrono Orb, Hand Blade, Sovereign Lance, Sovereign Star, Flame Feather, Void Ember. New: Rending Splinter, Iron Rivet, Forge Hammer, Riptide Trident, Undertow, Wave Crest, Ember, Molten Drop, Slag Boulder, Meteor Fragment, Cog, Rewind Blade, Stolen Moment, Decree Lance, Gilded Quill, Halo Shard (new type codes in `tools/crucible/types.json`, no old one renumbered). `SPIN` gives the hammer, rivet, whirlpool, boulder, gears, cog, watch, star and halo shard a `<Rotation>`.
- **Shots (`tools/build_crucible.py`, `CHAMPIONS`):** `foe()` takes `trail` (`<ParticleTrail>` colour, intensity, lifetime: the Rust client's sparks behind a shot), `boomerang` and `loop` (`<Parametric>` with a `<Magnitude>`). Damage, speed, size and afflictions of the existing projectile ids are unchanged except Ferrogrim's 3 (now the Forge Hammer: a boomerang, 75 speed for 3 s, was 70 for 2.4 s, so it reaches about 5.6 tiles and comes back). Each champion's existing ids keep their roles but some now point at the new objects (Ferrogrim 2 rivets, 3 hammer, 4 splinter; Nerissa 3 riptide trident, 4 undertow; Pyrrhax 2 ember, 3 molten drop, 4 slag boulder; Chronarch 3 cog, 4 stolen moment; Vaelith 4 quill, 5 decree lance). New ids: Nerissa 5 Wave Crest (170, slowed 1 s, boomerang), Pyrrhax 5 Meteor Fragment (180, sick 1 s, swaying), Chronarch 5 Rewind Blade (190, slowed 1 s, boomerang), Vaelith 6 Halo Shard (180, a figure of eight 4.5 tiles across round where it was thrown). Trails ride on the aimed shots and signatures only, not on the rings and spirals (fewer particles, and the shots worth watching stand out). The pets, minions and echoes keep their shot names, so they get the new art too.
- **Behaviour (`BehaviorDb.Crucible.cs`):** `RiseBurst(count, index)`: two rings once each time a champion enters one of its untouchable Rise states (11 of them). Added shots: Ferrogrim's SC Run (rivets), Lockdown (a thrown hammer), Blades Slam (two hammers), Tempest (three hammers instead of one hammer wave); Nerissa's Lanes, Rising, Whirl, Tsunami (crests; an undertow in Rising); Pyrrhax's Smelt, Storm, Pour (fragments, slag, molten drops); Chronarch's Tick, Midnight, Fracture Fight, Reversal, Zero Fight (rewind blades); Vaelith's Decree, Breaks, Aria (halo shards; stars and quills in Breaks) and every Echo (a ring of stars).
- **Regenerated:** `python tools/build_crucible.py`: both copies of `CrucibleObjects.xml`, `Crucible8x8.png` (the shots moved off it, the white bags' icons moved up), `Crucible16x16.png`. The map and grounds are unchanged.
- **Redeploys:** world server and client together (an older client doesn't know the new projectile ids, so it wouldn't show, or be hit by, the new shots, and draws the rest with the old art). Nobody is disconnected. No Redis change.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/crucible.md` (the shots table, each phase's new shots, the rising rings, the shots that come back or loop).
- **Not done:** the difficulty of the added shots is a guess at "a bit more": not playtested through a full climb.

### Scrying Glass: the watched player's boss health and damage

- **Asked:** the glass's big view should show the boss's health and the damage dealt, as the player sees it there, so watchers can see how much of the boss is left and who did how much.
- **Server:** `SpectatorHub.Step` sends each watcher, every 5 of the watched player's ticks, their quest (`QuestObjId`, when it changes) and everyone who hit it (`QuestDamageFull`, when it changes), wrapped in `SpectateFeed` like the rest of the picture; kept per watcher (`Spectator.QuestId`, `QuestSent`) and reset when the picture changes, so someone who starts watching mid-fight gets it at once. `Player.FullQuestDamage` builds the message (the player's own `SendQuestDamage` now uses it too; unchanged for them).
- **Client:** `glass.rs`'s `Picture` keeps the quest and its damage from the feed; the big view draws `hud::draw_watched_boss` at the picture's top left. `hud::draw_boss` takes where to draw and whose row is "theirs" (us, labelled You, or the player watched, by name); `game::full_hitters` is shared. No new text.
- **Redeploys:** world server and client. An older client ignores the new feed messages; a new client on an older server shows no box. Nobody is disconnected. No Redis change.
- **Not done:** the damage rows weren't seen in the glass in a shot (the test bot couldn't hurt its quest); the health bar was, and the rows are the HUD's own drawing from the same message.

A season's `filled` list names those tiers; the first time a state loads in that season, `PassBook` clears the claim marks on them (`PassState.FilledFor` remembers it was done), so players who'd passed them can claim the new rewards once. Only for the running season (`SeasonDef.Filled`).
- **Rules (`source/Shared/pass/`):** `PassData` (`PetFx` reward, `PetEffects`, `PetEffect(id)`, `SeasonDef.Filled`, checks), `PassState` (`pets`: owned ids, `pet`: the one worn, `filled`), `PassBook` (`Grant` of a pet effect: an owned one pays `repeatFame`; `WearPetEffect`, `WornPetEffect`; the first won is worn). Tests in `PassBookTests`.
- **Game (`WorldServer`):** `PassTracker` act 7 (`PETFX`, value 0 takes it off), the player's `PetEffect`, sent on the pet as stat 223 (`StatDataType.PetEffect`, `Pet.Effect`) so everyone nearby sees it; `PASS_DATA` has the pet effects and the one worn after `ShowTitle` (older clients stop before it), `PASS_RESULT` reward kind 8. `/passadmin petfx <id>` gives one (admins, test stacks).
- **Pet food (`source/Shared/pets/PetFood.cs`, `Player.PetPen.cs`):** `Pet Biscuit`, `Hearty Pet Stew`, `Royal Pet Feast` (0x58A9-0x58AB) are treats worth 750 / 2,500 / 7,500 food that the Pen Keeper feeds without fame when every picked slot is one (`PetFood.Free`). `Growth Tonic` (0x58AC): Pen Keeper act 13 grows a Tiny, Small or Medium pet at its last level a size, like a paid grow (`Grown`), using up the tonic. Tests in `PetLevelsTests`.

- **XP per tier changed mid-season:** a season's `xpPerTierWas` (Frostfall: 1,000) is the cost its XP was earned at; the first time a state of that season loads (`PassBook.Refresh`, also before paying an ended season's leftovers), `PassBook.Rescale` scales its XP towards the tiers to the new cost (30,500 becomes 61,000: tier 30, half way into 31), keeping XP past the last tier as it was so no Wayfarer's Caches appear. `PassState.RescaledFor` (`rescaled`) makes it once per state; a season begun afresh is marked at once. `PassBookTests.ChangedXpPerTierKeepsEveryonesTier`.

### /resetcrucible: everyone's Crucible climbs back for the day

- **Asked:** a simple admin command to give everyone their Crucible attempts back for the day.
- **What:** `/resetcrucible` (rank Admin or the `admin` flag). Every account whose `crucibleDay` is today and `crucibleClimbs` above 0 gets `crucibleClimbs` 0, straight in Redis (`Shared/crucible/CrucibleReset.cs`, read in batches of 500 accounts up to `nextAccId`), so offline players have their climbs when they come back. The admin is told how many accounts it reset. A new inter-server message (`Channel.Crucible`, `Shared/isc/data/CrucibleMsg.cs`) has every world server on that Redis (both slots of a hand-over, Testing) reload the field for the accounts it holds, whose copy would otherwise still count the climbs, and tell the players who had climbed "Your Crucible climbs are back for today: you may climb it once/twice/N times more" (as many as the day allows now, events and Gilded included).
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/core/commands/admin/Command.ResetCrucible.cs` (new), `source/WorldServer/core/worlds/impl/crucible/CrucibleAccess.cs` (`ResetAll`, `Listen`, `OnReset`), `source/WorldServer/core/GameServer.cs` (listens), `source/Shared/isc/Channel.cs`, `source/Shared/isc/data/CrucibleMsg.cs`, `source/Tests/CrucibleResetTests.cs`.
- **Redeploys:** world server only; nobody is disconnected. A world server still on the old build during a hand-over ignores the message: its players' climbs are reset in Redis but it keeps counting their old ones until they change server or log in again. No Redis migration. No changelog line (only admins use it; players see the notice when it's used).
