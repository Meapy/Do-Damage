Release 2.5.3: out of combat speed, `/realm` into Realm 2, realm kills and closes told to everyone, the Sprite Editor (draw item sprites, admins review them, approved ones ship with credit in the tooltip) and single-stat max potion sprites (deploys the client, the servers and the website; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Game:** out of combat you walk faster: +20 Speed (shown in green beside your Speed) whenever no enemy has hit you and you haven't hit one for 10 seconds. It goes the moment either happens and comes back after 10 quiet seconds. `/realm` now takes you into the Nexus's Realm 2, as `/realm2` does, instead of the busiest realm (Realm 1 is still its Nexus portal). Everyone in the game sees a Realm line in the chat when a realm event is killed ("Grand Sphinx killed in realm Empyrean."; those in that realm get its usual "[n/13]" line) and when a realm closes ("Realm Verdance closed.").
- **Sprite Editor:** a new button on the main menu. Pick any item and draw it a new look starting from its current sprite, or draw the art for a new item (with its name and kind: weapon, ability, armour, ring, consumable or other), at 8×8 or 16×16, with a pencil, an eraser, a fill, a colour picker, mirrored drawing, undo and redo, a 40-colour palette, any colour by hex code, and a preview outlined in a slot beside the item's sprite now. **Send in for review** sends it to the admins (registered accounts; up to 5 waiting at once). **My sprites** shows each one: waiting for review, approved, turned down with the admins' reason, or in the game; a waiting one can be withdrawn, and any can be opened again as a copy. Admins get a **Review** tab to approve or turn them down. Approved sprites come with the next update, and the item's tooltip (and its card on the wiki's Gear pages) says **Sprite by** and the player's name.
- **Items:** each of the eight single-stat Potions of Max has its own sprite.

## Details

### Out of combat speed

`Player.Combat.cs` keeps when the player was last in combat: hurt by an enemy (`PlayerHit`, or `Player.Damage` from an `Enemy`) or hurting one (`EnemyHit`, or `Enemy.Damage` credited to them, which also covers their bleeds and pet hits credited to them). `Player.Tick` flips the state back after 10 s (`CombatMs`) and recalculates the stats. `BoostStatManager.ApplyOutOfCombat` adds +20 Speed (`Player.OutOfCombatSpeed`) as a stat boost, flat after the Apotheosis scaling. The client needs no change: it reads the Speed stat and its bonus as before, so older clients get it too.

- Redeploy the world server. No Redis change, nobody disconnected.
- A world change gives a new player object, so you arrive in a new world out of combat.
- Ground damage (lava and the like) doesn't count as combat.

### Sprite Editor (client, account server, tools)

- **Client** (`client-rs/app/src/sprite_editor.rs`, new): the main menu's **Sprite Editor** button opens `Screen::Sprites`, drawn and fed by the app over the sky (`main.rs` routes keys, clicks, drags, the wheel and pastes to it while it shows). It grew out of the crosshair painter: a grid to click or drag, Pencil (B), Eraser (E), Fill (G), Pick colour (I), Mirror (M), Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, 8×8 or 16×16 (resizing keeps the drawing), Clear and Start over. Picking an item loads its sprite from the sheets in memory (`Sprites::pixels`; `Ui::src` is now `pub(crate)`). Three tabs: Draw, My sprites, and Review for admins (the server says who is one). The item tooltip ends with "Sprite by {name}" when the item's XML has `<SpriteBy>` (`hud::item_tooltip`). `--screen sprites`, `sprites-mine`, `sprites-review` and `sprites-item:<item id>` open it in tests. 84 new lines in all 10 language tables.
- **Account server** (`source/App/Controllers/SpritesController.cs`, `source/Shared/database/SpriteSubmissions.cs`, new): `/sprites/mine`, `submit`, `withdraw`, admin-only `queue` and `review` (approve, turn down with a reason, or put back to waiting), and the public `/sprites/approved`, which the tool reads. Approving a new look for an item turns down any other approved one for the same item, so only one goes in. Admins are accounts with the `admin` flag or rank 6. The server reads `resources/data/community_sprites.json` (re-read when it changes) and shows the submissions it lists as in the game, dropping them from the approved list.
- **Safeguards:** requests over 16 KB are refused before they're read (and 16 form values at most); sprites are checked before Redis is asked anything (8×8 or 16×16, hex pixels, each solid or empty, something drawn), names cleaned to 32 characters and notes to 200 (no markup characters, one line); guests, banned accounts and banned addresses can't send; one account at most every 20 seconds, 5 waiting and 15 a day; one address 10 an hour; everyone 400 a day and 300 waiting. Rejected, withdrawn and released submissions are forgotten 30 days later, an account's list keeps its last 30, and the public list is cached for 30 seconds.
- **Redis** (new keys, nothing migrated): `sprites.<id>` hashes, `sprites.nextId`, the sorted sets `sprites.pending` and `sprites.approved`, the lists `sprites.by.<account>`, and the expiring counters `sprites.wait.<account>`, `sprites.day.<account>`, `sprites.ip.<address>` and `sprites.all.<yyyymmdd>`.
- **Into the game** (`tools/community_sprites.py`, new): `list` and `apply --server https://dodmg.com/api` fetch what's approved and, for each new one, draw it into the next cell of `client/src/kabam/rotmg/assets/custom/images/CommunitySprites8x8.png` or `CommunitySprites16x16.png` (new, registered in `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py`), add it to the catalog, point the item's `<Texture>` at the cell and add `<SpriteBy>` in every XML copy, and add a changelog line and this file's part; `--commit` commits exactly those files. Run it in a worktree on the release branch: approved sprites ship with that release. Everything fetched is checked again as untrusted (ids, sizes, pixels, the item must exist; names cleaned before they reach Markdown). `rework` re-applies the catalog, and `tools/port_doomed.py` and `tools/make_elder.py` run it after writing their XML, so regenerating keeps the players' sprites.
- **Wiki:** the Items guide has a Sprite Editor section; gear cards show "Sprite by" (`tools/wiki_gear.py`).
- **Redeploy:** client, account server (restarts: sign-ins pause for a few seconds), world servers (XML) and website. Nobody is disconnected. Nothing for the owner to set up. To give someone the Review tab, make them an admin (SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Admin").
- **Not done:** no Discord notice when a sprite is sent in; art for a new item waits in the catalog until someone makes the item; only 8×8 and 16×16 sprites (the editor says so for an item drawn at another size); animated items' frames aren't editable.

### Single-stat max potion sprites

Each of the eight Potions of Max uses its own supplied sprite, Life to Wisdom, appended to `drLofiObj2` at tiles `0x100`–`0x107` by `tools/port_doomed.py` from `tools/doomed/single_stat_max_potions.png`. Both generated XML copies reference their tile. Redeploy the client and the world server; no Redis change.

### /realm into Realm 2, realm news for everyone

`RealmCommand` (`UnrankedCommands.cs`) now calls `Command.Realm2.EnterNexusRealm2`, the part of `/realm2` that sends a player outside a Realm 2 into the Nexus's (still being built or full: an error, as before). `RealmManager.CountingEvents` sends "<event> killed in realm <name>." to every player on the server outside that realm (`ChatManager.Realm`, which now takes a world to leave out), and `RealmWorld.CloseRealm` sends "Realm <name> closed." to everyone, for any close (its events, `/closerealm`, adopting a closed snapshot). Both are Realm lines (English from the server, like the realm's other lines).

- Redeploy the world server. No client change, no Redis change, nobody disconnected.
- Each world server tells its own players: during a rolling hand-over, players on the other slot don't hear the old slot's kills (a close reaches them when their copy adopts it). The admin-only Testing server tells only its own players.
- Wiki (`commands`, `getting-started`, `progression`, `realm-2`) and the Discord getting-started message updated; the Discord message only changes in the server when `tools/discord/setup_server.py` is run again.
- `/godlands` from outside a realm still picks the busiest realm.
