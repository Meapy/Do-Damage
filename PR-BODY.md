### Sharklord

### Monk

### Death replays line up

- **What and why:** reported from a real replay (an Elder Undead Lair death): the shots that dealt damage didn't line up with the character. The recording is the server's view: the player's place comes with their moves, a tick or so late and a ping behind, and their hits when their client reported them, while monsters' shots are recorded as the server fires them. Played as recorded, the player trailed the shots by their ping and a tick, and the client's glide added another tick, so a shot crossed the place they had been a moment before, and the numbers came at the wrong moment. Measured on the sample: the drawn player met the shots that hurt them up to half a second after the hit.
- **Client:** `app/src/replay/sync.rs` (new) works out, once per replay, how much earlier the player's recorded places are shown (the lead, 0 to 400 ms, the one that brings the shots that hurt them closest to them; 100 ms on the sample) and, for each hit from a shot, which of its shooter's shots dealt it and when it met them; the hits are then shown at that moment, in the order the server counted them (each shooter's shots reordered if matched the other way round), and the rest (unmatched shots, the ground) as much earlier as the matched ones were. Past the last recorded place the player carries on as they were moving, since the server never hears of their last moments. `app/src/replay.rs`: the player is placed along their recorded places each step (no glide), facing and walking from their path; monsters' shots fly through them (`World::untouchable`, `crates/world`), and the shot matched to a hit ends on them with sparks thrown its way; a tick from before a hit already shown doesn't put the health back up. The hit list and timeline use the times shown.
- **Redeploy:** client only. Nothing on the server or in the replay format changed, so replays already saved line up too. Nobody is disconnected.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/progression.md` (death replays: a hit lands as its shot strikes).
- **Not done:** the player's own shots are still drawn as the server heard of them (a ping late against the monsters); hits a monster deals by touching them keep the server's time.

- `Shared/database/GoldStore.cs`: `GoldDiscount` and `GoldStore.BlackFriday` (50% off, 24 hours, starts only before 8 December 2026 UTC, so a late deploy or a restored backup doesn't start it); `StartDiscount` writes the end time to `gold.discount.black-friday-2026` with SET NX (first start wins, kept for good so it never runs twice); `Discounted` gives the packs at the cut price (to the cent, never under PayPal's $0.50) with their usual price in `was`.
- `App/CoreService.cs` starts it at startup, only while PayPal is enabled (a closed store doesn't use up the day), and logs "Gold store: Black Friday, 50% off until ...". `App/Controllers/GoldController.cs` sells at the discounted packs (an order is made at, recorded at and checked against the price shown, so an order made during the sale and paid just after still matches); `store` adds each pack's `was` and `discount` {name, percentOff, ends}.
- `website/gold.html`, `gold.js`, `style.css`: a banner counting down to the end, the usual price struck through on each pack and in the total, "Save N%" on the pouch too; at zero the page asks for the prices again.
- Redeploys the **account server** (restart pauses sign-ins only) and the **website**; no client change, nobody disconnected. New Redis key `gold.discount.black-friday-2026`; nothing migrated. Nothing for the owner to do; to end it early, set the key to a past time and restart `bsk-account`; to give it again another year, add a new `GoldDiscount` with a new id.
- Not in the wiki (a one-day price, not part of the game).

### The Gold Store sells in euros

The store charged US dollars; from this release it charges euros, at the same numbers (€1 for 100 gold, the bonus packs as before).

- `Shared/PayPalSettings.cs`: the currency is no longer a setting: `PayPalSettings.StoreCurrency` is `EUR`, and a `currency` left in a config (the live `deploy/paypal.local.json` says `USD`, and the `PAYPAL_CONFIG` secret is its copy) is ignored, so the switch doesn't depend on editing secrets. `deploy/paypal.example.json` drops it.
- Orders keep their own currency (`gold.order.*`), so a dollar order made before the deploy and paid or refunded after still checks against dollars; the purchases list shows each in its own currency; `gold.sales` starts a `cents.EUR` beside `cents.USD`.
- `website/gold.js` (fallback currency, a comment), `website/wiki/items.md` (€1 for 100 gold), `GoldStore.cs` comments.
- **The owner:** the PayPal account receiving the money must accept euros. If it holds only dollars, PayPal may hold euro payments until accepted by hand, or convert them (its payment preferences); check before the deploy, and buy the smallest pack after it.
- Redeploys the account server and the website, as the sale above; nobody disconnected.
