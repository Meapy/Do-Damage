Release 2.6.1: Arena Season 2, Necromantic items drop half as often; deploys client, servers and website (players are not disconnected)

## For players

- **Arena:** Season 2, *Rise of the Fallen*. Every wave is now a round from one of nearly 80 dungeons and corners of the realm, in six tiers: Realm 2's bosses and the realm's gods first, then the Godlands, the Snake Pit, Candyland and the Mad Lab, the Manor, the Docks and the Library, the Tomb, Oryx's Castle and the Elder dungeons, and from wave 30 Oryx, the Shatters, the Cultist Hideout, the Void and the Reef, up to Oryx 4, the Ascended God and the Elder Shatters, Cult and Void. Each tier deals its rounds from a shuffled pile, so nothing repeats until the rest have come. A round can be a Horde (no boss, three times the monsters), a Clash (two dungeons at once), a Boss Rush (three bosses one after another), a Throwback (an old boss back as a Legendary one) or, every tenth wave, a Showdown (the Godlands' four gods, the Tomb's three guardians, the Battle for the Nexus's four bosses, Oryx's court, the Elder Cult). From wave 6 rounds may carry a twist: Elite, Swarm, Ironclad, Frenzy, Bounty (double fame), Blessing (you hit harder for 20 seconds) or Golden (full loot). From wave 12 an Empowered Elder boss may rise in the middle of a fight: rare, announced to the whole server, and worth 200 fame to everyone there.
- **Arena:** a banner shows each round as it begins (its name, shape, twist and bosses), the box under the wave counter keeps its name, and the vote panel shows what the next fight will be. Empowered bosses and a Boss Rush's next boss get banners too.
- **Arena:** the leaderboard keeps Season 1 exactly as it ended, on its own tab, and Season 2 starts afresh; Season 2 runs also show the Empowered bosses slain and the round the run fell at. `/leaderboard 1` lists Season 1. The trophy's sign shows the season's record, a new season record from wave 10 is announced, and the Hall of Champions' Arena boards follow the current season.

- **Dungeons:** the untiered item an Empowered Elder boss drops (the Necromantic weapons, armor, rings and the rest) now drops at half the chance: 3% for each player who did enough damage, down from 6%.
- **Dungeons:** Empowered Malus' blood moon ritual heals him far less: each cultist soul still standing when the chant ends gives back 1% of his health, down from 2.5% (at most 3% a ritual instead of 7.5%). Players on their own could not out-damage it.

## Details

### Necromantic item drop rate

- **What changed:** `UT_CHANCE` in `tools/build_resurrection.py` went from 0.06 to 0.03, and `source/Shared/resources/data/resurrection.json` was regenerated (only each boss's last `loot` chance changed). The XML the generator also writes was left as it is, so the community sprite taken in earlier is kept. The wiki's Necromantic Relic page reads the chance from the data, so it follows on the next site build.
- **Deploys:** world server and website. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes.

### Empowered Malus' ritual heal

- **What changed:** `EmpoweredSignatures.RitualHealShare` (new, 0.01) replaces the 0.025 per surviving soul in the Blood Moon Ritual (`source/WorldServer/core/resurrection/EmpoweredSignatures.cs`). The ritual is his only heal; it runs every 17 seconds below 40% with him shielded for 6.5 of them, so a whole ritual left standing healed about 29,000 of a solo Malus' ~385,000 health, more than one player deals in the time between. `source/Tests/EmpoweredPhaseTests.cs` caps a full ritual's heal at 3%. The Necromantic Relic wiki guide now gives the amount.
- **Deploys:** world server and website. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes.

### Arena Season 2

- **What changed:** the Arena's waves come from `ArenaSeason2` (`source/WorldServer/core/worlds/impl/ArenaSeason2.cs`: 80 encounters in six tiers, six showdowns, the Empowered pool, the planner) instead of Season 1's seven stages, which are gone from `ArenaPools` (its loot, fame, vote and Oryx rules stay). `DoomedBattleArena` (`DoomedWorlds.cs`) plays the rounds: health caps per tier kept up every tick, Boss Rush relays, twists, Empowered bosses through `BossResurrection.RaiseAt` (new `BossResurrection.InfoFor`), sleeping bosses woken (`ArenaSeason2.Wakes`: the Garden Statues, the Cult's lieutenants) and leftovers taken away (`IsLeftover`: the dragons' corpses, the Lost Sentry's spectre). New message ARENA_ROUND (170, `ArenaRoundMessage`) to clients that subscribed to the votes. Seasons: `Shared/database/leaderboard/ArenaSeasons.cs` (new), `DbArenaRuns` (season-aware, a v2 member with Empowered count and the round it fell at, `RankOf`), `ArenaController` (`season` in and the season list out), `ChampionSweep` and `ChampionBoards` (current season), `/leaderboard [season]`, `GameServer.Run` (`ArenaSeasons.Begin`). Admin tools: `/arenatest wave | empowered | plan | probe` (a game changed by them is not recorded). Client: `bsk_proto::ArenaRound`/`ArenaRoundInfo`, `bsk_net::account::parse_arena_board`, `npc.rs` (season chips, season line, Empowered and fell), `signs.rs` (SEASON n RECORD), `hud.rs` (round strip, banner, vote preview), `game.rs`, `text.rs` (faded text), 43 new lines in every `app/lang/*.json`. Docs: CHANGELOG, `website/wiki/progression.md` (the Arena section rewritten), `champions.md`, `tools/build_wiki.py` (/leaderboard), SETUP-LOCAL.
- **Left out after the probe:** the Mountain Temple (Daichi moves to fixed spots of his own map), the Forgotten King (his timer and crystals), the Marble Colossus (his rocks never die, so both Lost Halls are out), and Elder Malus (untouchable while his lieutenants are away). Limoz's Green Dragon God Deux only comes in the Battle for the Nexus Showdown, where Malphas Deux, whose death it waits for, comes too. Their bosses still appear nowhere in the Arena; everything else in the catalog was fought in the ring.
- **Deploys:** client, account server (leaderboard), world server, website. Nobody is disconnected. Older clients keep working: they see the current season's board without tabs and ignore ARENA_ROUND.
- **Redis:** Season 1's `doomed.arenaLeaderboard` is untouched. Season 2 writes `doomed.arenaLeaderboard.s2` and the hash `doomed.arenaSeasons` (its start, set by the first world server that runs it). The Hall of Champions' Arena boards are empty until Season 2 runs come in, so their fame rewards go to the new season's best.
- **Not done:** no new achievements or quests for Season 2 (the Arena wave quests count as before). Not played through by real players: tuned by the probe and the planner's tests.
