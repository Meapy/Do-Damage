Release 2.6.11: the Elder Ice Cave and the Scroll of the Unwilling, the Rime Herald event with the Frozen Barrow, the Frozen Court reworked and the Ice Tomb redrawn, replays of deaths saved with comments, marks where teleporting bosses appear, softer Void Entities, potion pickup from bags and an Ivory Wyvern fix. Deploys the world and account servers, client and website; nobody is disconnected. The new content and replay features need the client and servers together.

## For players

### Realm
- **A new realm event, the Rime Herald**, in both realms: the Frozen Court's herald, who always leaves the Ice Tomb's portal. It walks its ground throwing ice lances, rings of snowflakes and slowing hail; at 65% it raises four Frozen Sarcophagi round it and is Armored until they are broken, each letting out one of its dead, and raises them again a while after; below 30% it calls a whiteout and stalks and charges under falling hail. It drops the Ice Tomb's Frozen Wand, Arctic Bow and Tome of Frigid Protection, Defense and Wisdom potions. In the realm it comes on a patch of snowfield, rime ice and barrow flagstones; in Realm 2 it brings the **Frozen Barrow**, a land of snow, cracked ice, frigid pools (which sting and slow), graves, ice spires and braziers, with Barrow Mummies (with Ice Mites), Rime Wraiths and Frostfang Wolves, and quests the Barrow Colossus and the Frostfang Packmother, who can open the Ice Cave. A Realm 2 now shows eleven of its fifteen lands.

### Dungeons
- **The Elder Ice Cave:** half the time Esben leaves the portal to the Elder Ice Cave (or use an Elder Ice Cave Key, 30 gold): the Ice Cave and the Inner Sanctum in their Elder versions, every monster Elder. The Elder Esben throws icicles down on everyone while his crosses stand (Frostfall), fights back with fans and rings of ice while he can be hurt (Unbound Rend), and at a tenth of his health lets the cornus tear free: he vanishes into mist, returns to the heart of the sanctum armoured and turns two arms of ice against each other with rings of piercing bolts, icicles and two shielded kings for 16 seconds or until he falls. He drops a Potion of Max Mana (2.5%), Elder gear and his key, and leaves remains a Necromantic Relic raises: the Empowered Esben, with no crosses left to hold him, circles the sanctum in a cycle of his own (Rend, armoured icicle crosses, rings) with two signature phases, **Frozen Crosses** (70%) and **Whiteout** (40%).
- **Scroll of the Unwilling, the Summoner's white bag:** Elder Esben 0.5%, Empowered Esben 3%. +9 Wisdom, +5 Vitality, +72 HP, +92 MP; calls Esben's shade for 132 s (five-icicle fans of 100 and a 250 boomerang). Its power, **The Unwilling**: an enemy that dies within 4 tiles of your summons rises as a frozen thrall that fights for you for 8 s, then shatters into ice (250 past defense within 2.5 tiles, slowing); three at a time. A hand-drawn frost scroll.
- **Snow Bats fixed:** they laid carpets of motionless sonar spinners (six every 25 ms, each lasting 5 s); they now send a turning ring of six every 1.6 s and one at you every 0.9 s, which fly off and slow.

- **The Frozen Court fights like the Tomb of the Ancients' gods, harder:** the Crucible floors, banners, caches, shades and last stands stay, the three circle the Heart of Winter at the Tomb's pace (Kaldrun 5 tiles out, Hrimgar 6, Ysolde 7 the other way round), and each of the three now runs on the Tomb's turns. Every new turn opens with a taunt and 3 s behind a blue shield (untouchable). At 40% two **relics** come out (one more at 25%), shards of ice that circle their maker 2 tiles out and fire a three-shot fan every 2-2.4 s until shot down or their maker falls: Kaldrun's slow (Rime Relic, 5,000 health, 45 defence), Ysolde's darken (Aurora Relic, 4,000, 25), Hrimgar's bleed (Reaver Relic, 4,500, 35). Below 6% each **rages**: glowing red, chasing players, its whole kit plus its last stand's attacks and two more relics; the last one standing still makes its last stand below 15% and rages in it below 6%. Each also has a new turn: **Kaldrun** (as Bes) a ring of 20 snowflakes every 6 s from 80% and Armored while his relics are out; **Ysolde** (as Nut) a turning cross of quieting violet ribbons from 80%, and Aurora's Grace now keeps coming every 30 s through her relic turns; **Hrimgar** (as Geb) calls up to three hounds and lobs hailstones from 75%. His **Frenzy** at 50%, which he skipped (see Details), now comes every time.

- **The Ice Tomb's new look:** the tomb's nine kinds of dead, the three Seal Keepers, the three relics, the Frozen Court in each of its forms (Permafrost, Polar Night, the Shattered, their ice blocks and shades, and the Lil' pets made from them) and the tomb's 14 shots redrawn pixel by pixel in the game's own monster style: bold silhouettes, two or three tones a material, solid pixels for the client's outline, glowing eyes and cores. Hrimgar now wears a whole wolf's head, the Frostfang Alpha is blue-maned and the Rime Colossus stone and steel with a gold-sealed lid, so no two look alike.

- **The Void, the Elder Void and the Empowered Void Entity, softer in a group:** the Void Entity 250,000 health solo (was 300,000), +25,000 for each extra player (was +60,000: 20%, now 10%); the Elder Void Entity 400,000 (was 500,000), +20,000 a player (was +37,500: 15%, halved in Elder dungeons, now 10% halved); the Empowered Void Entity 550,000 (1.375 times the Elder's, was about 667,000), +20,000 a player, its shots 17% harder than the Elder's calm and 30% enraged (was 30% and 45%), 10% less.

- **Bosses that teleport show where they'll appear:** a ground mark of its own look (a ring in the boss's colour on the spot, four arcs closing in on it as they turn, a white core growing, then a flash as it arrives; unlike the filling circles of things about to land) shows where a teleporting boss will be, and the boss waits on it: Vesper's Starfall Waltz (violet, 0.9 s), Chronarch's Fracture and Zero blinks in the Crucible (cyan, 0.8 s), the Ghost of Skuld's eight hops round his crypt and his Requiem's return to its heart (green, the 1 s he's unseen; 2.5 s), Daichi the Fallen's jumps between the Mountain Temple's corners (jade, 0.8 s) and the Gilded Thief's blinks (gold, 0.7 s).

### Arena
- **The Ivory Wyvern can be beaten again:** in the Arena the (Elder) Ivory Wyvern could stay invulnerable for good, throwing its four soul flames every 20 seconds. It now moves on to the flames' fight 2.5 s after throwing them, whether or not all four landed, and comes out to fight once they're dead.

### Replays
- **Deaths saved, replayed:** a killing blow taken wearing an amulet that resurrects (or a ring with Guardian Angel) now keeps its replay, as a death does. The Legends have a fifth tab, **Deaths Saved**: the server's 30 latest, each row lit gold with the character, level and stats maxed, what would have killed them and where, the item that saved them (its icon, glowing), the blow they lived through (its damage against the health it met) and how long ago, under a count of every death cheated on the server. Clicking one plays its replay, which ends in gold rather than red: rings of holy light on the player, the item that saved them, "Saved by ..." and whose blow it was; the health bar fills gold, the would-be killing blow is outlined gold in the hit list, and the title reads Death saved.
- **Comments on every replay:** the replay viewer has a Comments tab beside Hits taken (or the C key): what players said, newest at the foot, and a field to add a line (signed in to an account, not a guest; 140 characters, one every 5 s). They last as long as the replay.
- **The Scrying Glass's Replays tab:** a sixth tab beside the chats lists the server's 20 latest deaths (red edge) and deaths saved (gold edge, with the item), newest first, each with who, level, class, what and where, when, and how many comments; clicking one plays it over the game, in the Nexus, with its comments, and Back returns to the glass. The list refreshes every 30 s while shown.

### Game
- **Health and Magic Potions pick themselves up:** standing within a tile of a loot bag you may open (yours or everyone's) moves its Health and Magic Potions onto the HP and MP stacks, up to the stacks' limit (10); the rest stay in the bag. Not from vault, gift, guild or Nexus chests, not in the Marketplace, not while trading or dead. Potions in your inventory aren't touched.

- **What and why:** the owner asked for the Ice Tomb's bosses to be like the Tomb of the Ancients' and chose to keep the floors and add the Tomb's phases, a harder Tomb. Each boss: health turns with a 3 s Invulnerable "blue shield" (`CourtTurn`), relics at 40% and 25% (`Spawn` on entering the turn, `Relic`: orbit the maker, fire, suicide when it's gone), a rage below 6% (`Rage` and `Last Rage`, the latter ordering the Court Heart into the last stand if it isn't already). Kaldrun 80 Rime Wall / 60 Bulwark / 40, 25 Relics / 15 / 6; Ysolde 80 Ribbons / 50 Grace / 40, 25 Relics (Grace every 30 s in each, `GraceCycle`) / 15 / 6; Hrimgar 75 Pack / 50 Frenzy / 40, 25 Relics / 15 / 6. Health, defence and per-player scaling (`BossStats`) unchanged.
- **Bug fixed:** a behaviour's states share one list of names, the last winning. Hrimgar's `ReaverCycle` built "Hunt", "Leap" and so on three times, so his first hunt's leap went into his Fight 3 copy, which has no 50% transition: he never reached his Frenzy. The cycles are now named per turn; `BehaviorStatesTests.FrozenCourtStatesAreTheirOwn` checks the three's names are unique and their turns exist. Noted in source/AGENTS.md.
- **Fits the hall:** the hall's floor is 19.5 tiles across from the middle, thrones at 13.5, pillars at 15 (`IceCourt.cs`, `TombCourtPlan`); the floors reach 18. Ysolde circles at 9.5 (11 before) while her relics are out and the relics stay within 2.4 of their maker, so they clear the thrones; everything moves with `ValidateAndMove`, so a raging boss chasing to the edge keeps its relics on the floor. The map (`Ice Tomb.jm`) is unchanged.
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.IceTomb.cs`, `tools/build_ice_tomb.py` (`RELICS`), `tools/ice_tomb/art.py` (`relic`, `relics`), regenerated `IceTombObjects.xml` (both copies), `IceTombChars16x16.png` (three rows added at the end, nothing moved) and `tools/ice_tomb/types.json` (0xCA86-0xCA88), `source/Tests/BehaviorStatesTests.cs`, `source/AGENTS.md`, the changelog, `website/wiki/dungeons.md`.

Now Kaldrun `Orbit(3, 5)`, Ysolde `Orbit(4, 7)` clockwise, Hrimgar `Orbit(3 / pace, 6)` (his rage chases at the Tomb's 1.5: `ReaverCycle(..., chase: true)`), last stands 2-2.5 on a 3-tile ring, rages chasing at 1.2-1.5, shades 1.2 (0.25). - **What and why:** the owner found the tomb's sprites poor beside the game's. ### The Ivory Wyvern stuck throwing soul flames

- **What and why:** after reaching 60% the Wyvern returns to its spawn, turns invulnerable and tosses the Red, Blue, Green and Black Soul Flames (4 and 8 tiles east and west), then waited (`EntityExistsTransition`) for a Red Soul Flame within 20 tiles before waiting for all four to die. `TossObject` silently drops a toss onto an impassable tile, so where the Red one's spot is a wall (the Arena's ring, a spawn near its edge) it never came: the Wyvern stayed in that state, invulnerable, re-throwing the flames every 20 s. ### Health and Magic Potions picked up from bags
- **What and why:** the owner asked for HP and MP potions to be picked up by walking over bags. Each tick, `Player.AutoPickupPotions` (new `Player.AutoPickup.cs`, called from `Player.Tick`) looks at the world's containers and, for each plain loot bag (`Container` itself, no `DbLink`, not the 0x504 vault chest) that `IMessageHandler.CanTakeFrom` allows (owner, within a tile; guild and Nexus vault rules), takes the `Health Potion` and `Magic Potion` slots that fit in one inventory transaction, then adds them to `HealthPotionStack` / `MagicPotionStack`, as dragging one onto a stack does. Stops early when both stacks are full.
- **Deploys:** the world server only; no message changes, old clients see the stacks' counts change. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: nothing new (the stacks' counts are saved as before).
- **Not done:** no option to turn it off.
