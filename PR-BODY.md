## For players

### King

The royal seal falls where he aims (up to 9 tiles, 260 ms later): 160 up to 680 damage within 3 to 4.5 tiles, and the enemies under it are **Condemned** for 4 to 8 s, taking 5% more damage from every hit, with a gold crown drawn over each. A Condemned enemy that dies, by anyone's hand, pays **Tribute** to everyone who damaged it and the King: a stack each of +10 damage on every shot that strikes an enemy, added after defense, up to 10 stacks (+100); 10 s after a player's last new stack they fade, one a second. The stacks show as gold coins circling the player's feet, and each payment flies a coin from the fallen enemy to each who earned it. The first three of each decree to fall also restore the King's mana (8 up to 18). Drawn: the seal falling out of the sky with its shadow growing, landing in a flash, shockwaves and thrown-up coins, its mark (a ring of twelve marks round a gem) left on the ground; the Condemned's crowns bobbing, blinking in their last second. ### Monk

### Death replays line up

- **What and why:** reported from a real replay (an Elder Undead Lair death): the shots that dealt damage didn't line up with the character. The recording is the server's view: the player's place comes with their moves, a tick or so late and a ping behind, and their hits when their client reported them, while monsters' shots are recorded as the server fires them. Played as recorded, the player trailed the shots by their ping and a tick, and the client's glide added another tick, so a shot crossed the place they had been a moment before, and the numbers came at the wrong moment. Measured on the sample: the drawn player met the shots that hurt them up to half a second after the hit.
- **Client:** `app/src/replay/sync.rs` (new) works out, once per replay, how much earlier the player's recorded places are shown (the lead, 0 to 400 ms, the one that brings the shots that hurt them closest to them; 100 ms on the sample) and, for each hit from a shot, which of its shooter's shots dealt it and when it met them; the hits are then shown at that moment, in the order the server counted them (each shooter's shots reordered if matched the other way round), and the rest (unmatched shots, the ground) as much earlier as the matched ones were. Past the last recorded place the player carries on as they were moving, since the server never hears of their last moments. `app/src/replay.rs`: the player is placed along their recorded places each step (no glide), facing and walking from their path; monsters' shots fly through them (`World::untouchable`, `crates/world`), and the shot matched to a hit ends on them with sparks thrown its way; a tick from before a hit already shown doesn't put the health back up. The hit list and timeline use the times shown.
- **Redeploy:** client only. Nothing on the server or in the replay format changed, so replays already saved line up too. Nobody is disconnected.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/progression.md` (death replays: a hit lands as its shot strikes).
- **Not done:** the player's own shots are still drawn as the server heard of them (a ping late against the monsters); hits a monster deals by touching them keep the server's time.

A link, a search result or the menu's part list opens the sections it points into (`hashchange`, and a click on the same `#`); a filter over cards opens the sections holding matches and hides those with none, then puts them back as they were when cleared; a filter box left at the end of another section moves out between the sections (Dungeons'). ### The Hive, remade

- **What and why:** the owner asked to fully implement the Hive from its original player-made files (github.com/Suestwo/UGC1: TheHive.xml, HiveGen.xml, the floors, key and items). The game already had the dungeon's objects, items and a fixed map, but its behaviours were a stub (every bee wandering and shooting once a second, eggs and Mini Hives only spawning on death, a two-state Queen). Now the layout and the behaviours follow UGC1.
- **Map:** `tools/build_hive.py` (new) draws the rooms from HiveGen.xml's numbers (start circle radius 6 with the Portal of Cowardice, 18 rooms radius 4-7 rolling their monsters from its `default`/`branchEnd` lists, the Queen's radius 10 with 1-2 of each egg) on a 5x4 grid of narrow halls; it writes `source/Shared/resources/worlds/Dungeons/The Hive.jm` (regenerated). `Dungeons.xml`: the Hive's `<RandomLayout passage="3" corridor="3">` with `<Goal path="5">TH Queen Bee</Goal>` (HiveGen's main branch of 7), so `DungeonGenerator` joins 7, 12 or all 18 rooms anew each visit. The honey (`TH honey Heal` turning the floor to honey in UGC1) is painted on the map as small puddles, about 8% of a room.
- **Behaviours:** `BehaviorDb.Hive.cs` rewritten after TheHive.xml: the Queen (`StayCloseToSpawn` 7, the Go / rest / DazeEm / rest cycle, `Grenade` and `TossObject` of eggs, `ScaleHP2(20)`), Fat Bees (25% `TransformOnDeath` into Fat Angry Bees), Fat Angry Bees (`Follow` 8 s on, 2 s off), Small Bees (`Orbit` the Queen, `Charge`), Mini Hive (`Spawn` up to 6, bursts at 25%), eggs (crack below 98%), larvae (`Orbit` the Queen, `Transform` into Fat Bees after 10 s). Loot: the Queen keeps her drops (Dexterity, two Bottled Honey, Mountain drops, Bee Generator 3%) with UGC1's Scepter and Orb at 8.5% (from 5%) and its T5-T7 weapons and T6-T7 armour added; Fat Bees drop T3-T5 armour, Fat Angry Bees T3-T5 weapons, Mini Hives three 50% Bottled Honey. The Queen's root state no longer holds Invulnerable (so `BehaviorStatesTests.Known` loses her).
- **Changed from UGC1:** what hatches during the fight (larvae grown up, and their bees come back angry) is the existing no-experience `TH Fat Bees 2` / `TH Red Fat Bees 2`, with no loot, since the Queen throws eggs without end; the Queen Bee Summoner isn't used (she stands in her room, and `World.BossDied` opens the Realm Portal); the Bee Egg and Wine Cellar Incantation drops are left out (no Bee Egg here; the Incantation drops nowhere else). The objects' stats (health, damage, Bleeding 1.4 s) stay as the game's XML has them, which are the released dungeon's, not UGC1's first draft.
- **Tests:** `RandomDungeonTests` gains the Hive (30 seeds: the Queen once and reachable, the Portal of Cowardice and every egg kind kept).
- **Redeploy:** world server only. Nobody is disconnected; Hives already open keep their old map until they close.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/dungeons.md` (a new Hive section).
