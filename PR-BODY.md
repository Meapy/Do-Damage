Release 2.5.13

## For players

- **Classes:** the Ascendant's halos raise a forcefield instead of healing. You and every ally standing in it take less from each hit while it lasts (shots, damaging ground, everything): 2% with the Worn Halo, 1% more each tier, up to 10% with the T8 Halo of the Archangel. Each field is 1.5 tiles smaller than the old healing circle (4.5 tiles at T0-T1, up to 6.5 from T4) and lasts as long as the halo's heal did (8 to 24 seconds). It looks the part: the halo falls from the sky in a column of light, shockwaves race out, a shimmering hex-lattice dome opens with turning rune rings and a wall of light rising off its rim, it flares round anyone it shields from a blow, and it shatters as it fades. The Ascendant still earns assist loot for every player it covers. The class and halo descriptions are rewritten to match. All nine halos are redrawn (the top three no longer share one sprite), and each tier's forcefield takes its halo's colours and a touch of its own: Worn iron that stutters with holes in its lattice, Rusty flaking rust, Bronze with verdigris, polished Silver, Golden, Eternal Peace's jade with drifting petals, God's Blessing's turning shafts of light, the Seraphim's rim of flame and rising embers, and the Archangel's violet dome with a third rune ring and stars wheeling round it.
- **Website:** the wiki no longer labels classes, items and pets "From Doomed Realms": its class page groups them as "The newer classes", the dungeons page as "Minigames and more dungeons", and the home page's tile is "More to play". The Party Balloons' description welcomes players to Do Damage instead of another server.

## Details

### Ascendant halos: forcefields instead of healing

- **What changed:** the halo ability's `Halo` activate is now `Forcefield` (`ActivateEffects.Forcefield`): `range` the radius, `amount` the percent off each hit, `duration` its time. `Forcefield.cs` (was `Halo.cs`) shields every player in it each tick (`Player.Shield`, held 250 ms so the edge doesn't flicker); `Player.ShieldedDamage` takes the percent off each hit after defense, rounded to the nearest point, in `PlayerHit`, `Player.Damage` and both ground-damage paths. Overlapping fields don't add up: the strongest counts. The caster gets assist credit (`BuffedBy`) for each player in it, a second at a time. Its server-side cooldown (4 s) is kept.
- **Numbers:** T0 Worn 2% / 4.5 tiles, T1 Rusty 3% / 4.5, T2 Bronze 4% / 5, T3 Silver 5% / 5.5, T4 Golden 6% / 6.5, T5 Eternal Peace 7% / 6.5, T6 God's Blessing 8% / 6.5, T7 Seraphim 9% / 6.5, T8 Archangel 10% / 6.5. Durations and mana unchanged.
- **Look (Rust client, `particles/relic.rs`):** `ShowEffect` 41 looks 30 (cast), 31 (a player in it hit, at most every 250 ms each) and 32 (already up when a player comes into view, sent then). The percent rides in `ObjectType`, which the client uses to take the same cut off the damage numbers it shows for your own hits (`bsk_world::combat::shielded`, tested against the server's rounding). New decals `Forcefield` and `FieldFlare`, an aura for the rising wall, a shatter when the aura ends. Older clients don't know these looks; the server also sends a golden Trap ring at its edge every 1.5 s for them, which the new client leaves out.
- **Data:** `tools/port_doomed.py` (`FORCEFIELDS`, halo and class `DESCRIPTIONS`) converts the T0-T6 halos; both `DoomedRealms.xml` copies were changed by hand to what it writes, because the port currently stops early on an unrelated world (`ElderPirateCave` missing from `port_doomed_worlds`). The T7 and T8 halos are hand-written in both `CustomItems.xml` copies. `tools/rework_buff_auras.py` no longer floors the halos' range (re-running it changes nothing).
- **Wiki:** `tools/wiki_gear.py` describes the forcefield; `website/wiki/classes.md` mentions shielding for assist loot.
- **Deploys:** client, servers and website. Ship client and server together (new looks and the `Forcefield` activate name): an old client still plays, but sees only the gold rings and its tooltip doesn't describe the halo. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis data changes.
- **Not done:** the halos keep their `spell/major_heal` sound.

### Forcefields and halos by tier

- **What changed:** `Forcefield.cs` takes a colour per tier (`COLORS`, `ColorOf(item.Tier)` in `AEForcefield`) and sends it in the effect's `Color` (cast, seen, and the older clients' Trap ring); `Player.Shield` keeps the strongest field's colour for the flare round a shielded player. The Rust client (`particles/relic.rs`) looks each colour up in `field_style`: an accent and glint colour in place of the gold field's azure and white, an unsteadiness (T0-T2 stutter and lose lattice cells) and what drifts in it (`Drift`, an aura-tick `field_drift`); God's Blessing adds turning shafts of light, the Seraphim tongues of flame round the rim, the Archangel a third rune ring, an outer rim and wheeling stars. An unknown colour draws as the gold field did. `--effect 140`-`148` (tests) raises tier 0-8 on yourself.
- **Sprites:** `tools/draw_halos.py` draws the nine halos at 16x16 into `client/src/kabam/rotmg/assets/custom/images/AscendantHalos16x16.png` (`ascendantHalos16x16`, registered in `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py`, `client-rs/assets/manifest.json` regenerated). Both copies of `DoomedRealms.xml` (T0-T6) and `CustomItems.xml` (T7-T8) point at it; the Ascendant's description no longer calls the fields golden. The AIR client doesn't register the sheet (as with the other new 16x16 sheets).
- **Deploys:** client and servers, with the rest of this release (the colours come from the server; a client without `field_style` would draw them all in the gold style, tinted). Website for the wiki's sprites. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes.

### The Doomed Realms port tools are gone

- **What changed:** content that came from Doomed Realms is now ordinary game content, edited in place. Deleted `tools/port_doomed.py` and `port_doomed_{allies,economy,monsters,pets,worlds}.py` (they read the Doomed Realms repository, which isn't on this PC, and had stopped working: the port ended early on an unrelated world), `tools/doomed/` (its type registry, remote textures and source art, all already in the XML and sheets) and `tools/pet_base_damage.json`. `DoomedRealms.xml`, `DoomedRealmsGrounds.xml`, `DoomedRealmsWorlds.xml`, `DoomedMonsters.cs`, `BehaviorDb.DoomedMonsters.cs`/`DoomedAllies.cs`/`DoomedPets.cs` and `DoomedEconomyData.cs` lose their "Generated, do not edit" headers. File, class and object names, Redis keys (`doomed.arenaLeaderboard`, `doomed.gearClaimed`) and asset paths are unchanged.
- **`tools/make_elder.py`** stands on its own: `rebalance_elders` and the Elder map builder (`elder_map`, `place_braziers`) moved into it from the port scripts. `python tools/make_elder.py` writes the Elder tiles, monsters and sheet as before (its output is byte for byte what the old script wrote); `python tools/make_elder.py map <WorldClass> ["<file>.jm"]` writes one Elder dungeon's map (the Elder Tomb and Elder Shatters maps come out identical to the committed ones) and prints its `<RandomLayout>`.
- **Docs:** `AGENTS.md`, `source/AGENTS.md`, `client-rs/AGENTS.md` and `client/AGENTS.md` rewritten shorter (972 lines to about 600), with the "generated files" rules replaced by "edit content in place, unless the file names its generator". SETUP-LOCAL.md's "Content ported from Doomed Realms" section is now a short "Classes, minigames and the economy" reference, and the port scripts' names are gone from its change notes. README, the comments in tools, C#, Rust and XML that named the scripts, and `docker-compose.yml` updated. The website and wiki text (`website/index.html`, `website/wiki/*.md`, `tools/build_wiki.py`, `tools/wiki_gear.py`) no longer call content "Doomed Realms"; the Party Balloons' description changed in both `DoomedRealms.xml` copies.
- **Deploys:** website; the balloon text reaches players with the next client and server. No code changes (comments only in C# and Rust), no Redis changes, nobody disconnected.
- **Not done:** C# comments that explain where code came from ("as in Doomed Realms") and the dated history in SETUP-LOCAL.md and the released changelog still name it, as does README's Credits. Running `make_elder.py` today would drop eight Elder loot balloons (`Elder lod ... Loot Balloon`, `Elder lod Ivory Loot`, `Elder shtrs Loot Balloon ...`) from `ElderObjects.xml` (drift from before this change); nothing here regenerated it.
