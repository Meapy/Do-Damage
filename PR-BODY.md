Release 2.6.5: dodmg.com redone in 3D (a flight through Oryx's six stages), Ctrl+K search and a phone menu on every page, and a wiki that's easier to get around; deploys the website only (nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Website:** dodmg.com is redone in 3D. The home page is a flight: a castle town under the moon, through its gate and from portal to portal down Oryx's six stages as you scroll, each portal holding that stage's boss built in 3D from the game's own sprite (the two Stone Guardians, Oryx 1, 2, 3 and 4, and Oryx the Ascended God), each card showing the same boss, its stage and its danger, the sky warming from violet to blood the deeper you go; the wiki, gold store and changelog keep a calm drifting night behind glass panels. **Ctrl+K** or **/** searches the wiki from any page, and phones and narrow windows get a menu button with every link (before, the bar showed only Wiki). The **wiki** is easier to get around: its pages are listed under four sections (Start here, Dungeons and loot, Classes and gear, Every day) with nothing hidden, every page shows where it sits (Wiki › section › page), Previous and Next go through the pages in that order, "On this page" stays beside long pages on wide screens and marks the part you're reading, a button takes you back to the top, and the wiki home has a card for every page. Pages also load faster: the search loads only when you search.

## Details

### dodmg.com in 3D

- **Review first** (the owner asked for a review, a plan, then a Three.js rewrite). Good: content generated from the game's data, a strong pixel identity, SEO and accessibility already done, no framework. Bad: a flat 2D hero, a home page of stacked dark boxes, the six stages as a plain list, a phone bar with only Wiki in it (Gold, Changelog and Discord only in the footer), search only inside the wiki.
- **What:** `website/scene.js` (new) draws one Three.js scene behind every page on a fixed canvas: a gradient sky dome, stars, the moon, an instanced town and castle with lit windows, rising embers, and on the home page six portals past the gate, each with its stage's boss in cubes, one per pixel of the game's own sprite: `tools/build_wiki.py` (`stage_bosses`, `STAGE_BOSSES`) copies the bosses' sheets to `oryx/` and fills `{{BOSS_1}}`..`{{BOSS_6}}` in `index.html`'s stage cards with each sprite cut from its sheet by CSS and its place on the sheet (`data-boss`), which `scene.js` reads back to build the cubes. The cards (`.stop-card`) hold the stage's number, the boss and the danger pips together; the portal of the card nearest the middle of the screen is the one shown, whole in the right half beside it on wide screens, above it on phones. The camera follows the page: the hero, then through the gate to the portal of whichever stop of Six stages down is being read (that stop's card lit, `.stop.active`), then back up for the rest. `website/scene.css` (new): glass panels, the stages as a scroll story, cards rising into view, the search palette, the phone menu. `website/palette.js` (new): Ctrl+K and / open a search over `wiki/search-index.js`, loaded on first use. `website/site.js`: the 2D canvas sky is gone, the phone menu added. `index.html`, `gold.html`, `changelog.html` and the wiki's template in `tools/build_wiki.py` load the new files; `tools/build_site.py`'s docstring.
- **Three.js** is `website/vendor/three.min.js` (r149, MIT, its licence in the file's header), served from the site itself, loaded `defer`. r149 is the last classic build without a deprecation warning in the console.
- **Fallbacks:** without WebGL nothing is added (`html.has-scene` is never set) and the old look stays; with reduced motion the scene is drawn still, again on scroll; the loop stops in a hidden tab and draws fewer pixels if frames run slow. `window.dodScene.jump()` puts the camera where the page is at once, for tests and screenshots.
- **Redeploys:** the website only, with the merge's deploy; nobody is disconnected. No client, server or Redis change.
- **Not done:** nothing for this part (the wiki's layout is the next part).

### The wiki, easier to find your way around

- **Review** (the owner asked to update the wiki and make it easier to use and move through). Before: the sidebar was a flat list of 14 entries in no clear order (five pages shared `nav: 4` and sorted by file name), with a page's sub-pages hidden until you were in its group; Previous and Next followed the `nav:` numbers across groups (Commands, then Getting stronger, then Hall of Champions); "On this page" was a box at the top that scrolled away; nothing said where a page sat; the wiki home linked only some pages; every wiki page loaded the 635 KB search index, searched or not.
- **What:** every guide's front matter names a `section:` (Start here, Dungeons and loot, Classes and gear, Every day) and `nav:` is renumbered to order them (`parent:` and `group:` are gone). `tools/build_wiki.py`: the sidebar lists every page under its section, the current one marked (a gear page marks the guide it belongs to) and scrolled into view; breadcrumbs (Wiki › section › guide › page); Previous and Next walk the pages in that order and name the section when they leave it; `{{gen:wiki-map}}` puts every page as a card under its section on the wiki home (`website/wiki/index.md`, "Every page"); the build stops if a page names no section or its `nav:` splits a section. On screens 1280 wide and up, "On this page" stands beside the page and follows it, the section being read marked (`website/wiki/wiki.js`); a button takes you back to the top on long pages; the search box shows its `/` key.
- **Search index on demand:** the wiki pages no longer load `search-index.js` up front. `website/palette.js` loads it the first time anyone searches (`window.ddWikiIndex`), from the address the page gives it with the index's own `?v=` (`data-index`; `build_wiki.build()` returns it as `INDEX` for `index.html`, `gold.html` and `changelog.html`); `wiki.js` uses the same copy, or loads it itself if palette.js didn't run. On the wiki, `/` goes to the sidebar's box and Ctrl+K to the palette.
- Files: `website/wiki/*.md` (front matter; `index.md`'s last section), `website/wiki/wiki.js`, `website/palette.js`, `website/style.css` (wiki rules), `website/index.html`, `gold.html`, `changelog.html` (`data-index`), `tools/build_wiki.py`.
- **Redeploys:** the website only, with the merge's deploy; nobody is disconnected.
- **Not done:** the guides' text is as it was; only how you get around them changed.
