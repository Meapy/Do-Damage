Release 2.6.10: the Platinum Sword reworked as Rimebrand, the Ice Tomb harder and guided with tall walls, two Frozen Court white bags for the King and the Sharklord, and a Nexus banner for Gold Store sales. Deploys the world server and client; nobody is disconnected. The Ice Tomb's walls, new banners and the white bags' icons need the client and world server together.

## For players

### Gold Store
- **Sales show in the Nexus:** while the Gold Store has a discount on (Black Friday's half price), entering the Nexus shows a gold banner at the top: "Black Friday. All gold is 50% off for the next 21 hours!", counting down while it shows (minutes in the last hour), and a click opens the Gold Store. An account sees it at most once every 10 minutes; a chat line says the same, which is all a client not yet updated shows.

### Items
- **The Platinum Sword is now Rimebrand:** renamed and reworked as the Ice Tomb's own sword, a rare drop from each of the Frozen Court (0.25%, as the tomb's bows and wands); Oryx 4 and the Christmas Oryx no longer drop it. Each swing fires three: a platinum slash, 180-220, that cleaves through every enemy in its path out to 7 tiles, flanked by two frost shards, 35-50 each out to 6 tiles, that slow what they hit for 1.5 seconds. At 100% rate of fire instead of 150% (8 tiles before): up close on one enemy about 285 damage a second, between the T14 and T15 swords, and about 200 from the slash alone at range, the price of twice a sword's reach. A new icon to match the tomb's gear: a platinum blade with rime on the point, a teal guard with a frost gem, a dark steel grip and a frost pommel (it was a plain gold sword). Swords already owned change with it.
- **Two white bags from the Frozen Court:** each of the Court drops them (0.3% each). The King's **Edict of the Frozen Court** (scepter): a Royal Decree (560 within 4 tiles, Condemned 7 s, 16 MP tribute) that also Slows what it Condemns for 2 s, and Winter's Edict: each Condemned enemy that falls Condemns and slows every enemy within 3.5 tiles of it for the rest of the decree, and they spread it again when they fall; +5 Def, +4 Wis. The Sharklord's **Rimejaw** (oceanic spell): a Maw of the Deep (fin 360, bite 620 within 3.25, 55 HP fed) whose bite Slows everything it catches for 2 s and freezes the Bloodied solid (Paralyzed 1.2 s), everything a Megalodon bites; +4 Def, +5 Spd. Both a little under the T8's numbers for it, in the tomb's frost colour, with icons of their own (a rime scepter crowned with icicles, a shark's jaws gaping out of a hole in the ice).

### Dungeons
- **The Ice Tomb, harder, busier and clearer:** the walls stand two tiles tall (frosted brows over dark blocks of ice) and turn see-through while you stand behind one; they no longer pop in only once in sight, leaving the floor beside them hanging over the sky. Passages between rooms are 5 tiles wide (3 before), and every room has a second band of the tomb's dead (131 monsters across the tomb's rooms, 79 before). The quest arrow leads to the nearest Seal Keeper or seal left, then back to the Sealed Door, where a gate of rime rises once all three seals are broken, then to the Court. Each keeper has a last phase: the Rime Colossus calves two Glacier Golems and turns Armored (25%), the Aurora Matron hides untouchable in the dark of four wisps until they're shot down (25%, 20 s at most) and comes out harder, the Frostfang Alpha's Blood Moon (30%) calls the pack and pounces three times in a row. The Frozen Court has three new phases with banners: Kaldrun's **Calving Glacier** (avalanche arms turning round the hall with waves rolling out between), Ysolde's **Frozen Hours** (six hours of aurora burning in turn) and Hrimgar's **Wolf Moon** (three hounds loosed with rime cracking out under you), and its calms are 4.5 s (5.5).

## Details

### The Platinum Sword reworked as Rimebrand
- **Name:** players see **Rimebrand** (`<DisplayId>`); its id stays `Platinum Sword`, so the loot tables, `DoomedEconomyData`'s price, `collection.json`, `/give` and Marketplace listings keep working without a change.
- **What and why:** a plain ported sword (170-190 at 150%, range 8: 270 a second from twice any sword's reach) dropped by Oryx 4 half the time. Now a mid-tier dungeon's prize that keeps its long reach (6-8 tiles, as the owner asked) and pays for it in damage, with a reason to stand close: a `<VolleyProjectiles>1,0,1</VolleyProjectiles>` volley (as the Elysian Staff's) of a `<MultiHit/>` silver slash (the Oryx Champion Slash sprite, with a pale trail) and two Iceblast shards with `Slowed` 1.5 s, `<ArcGap>14</ArcGap>`, so the shards meet one target only up close. The tooltip lists the slash; the shards are an `EffectInfo` ("Rime Shards").
- **Icon:** drawn in `tools/ice_tomb/art.py` (`PLATINUM_SWORD`, laid out as the Sword of Acclaim, in the tomb's palette), added last to `IceTomb8x8.png` (cell 0x80, a new row: the sheet grows to 128x72, no other cell moves) by `tools/build_ice_tomb.py`, which stops if the cell ever moves (`PLATINUM_SWORD_CELL`); the sword's `<Texture>` is `iceTomb8x8` 0x80. Rerunning the build changes nothing else.
- **Files:** `source/Shared/resources/xml/custom/DoomedRealms.xml` and the client's copy (`client/src/kabam/rotmg/assets/custom/xmls/DoomedRealms.xml`), `BehaviorDb.IceTomb.cs` (`IceTombTreasure()`), `logic/doomed/DoomedMonsters.cs` (Oryx 4 and Christmas Oryx tables), `tools/ice_tomb/art.py`, `tools/build_ice_tomb.py`, `client/src/kabam/rotmg/assets/custom/images/IceTomb8x8.png` (regenerated), `tools/wiki_gear.py`, SETUP-LOCAL.md, client-rs/AGENTS.md, the changelog, `website/wiki/`.
- **Deploys:** the world server and the client together (both read the sword's XML: a client not yet updated keeps firing the old single slash, which the server's rate check may refuse, until it updates). Nobody is disconnected.
- **Redis:** nothing migrated; the type code (0xD0F7) is kept, so owned swords take the new stats.

### A Nexus banner for Gold Store sales
- **What:** a player entering the Nexus while a gold discount runs (`GoldStore.BlackFriday`, whose end the account server keeps in `gold.discount.black-friday-2026`) gets a banner and a chat line, at most once every 10 minutes an account (`GoldStore.DiscountToTell`: SET NX on `gold.told.<account>` with a 10-minute expiry, so it holds across world servers and rolling restarts).
- **Message:** no new one. `GlobalNotification` (69) kind 1 carries "<percent off>|<seconds left>|<name>" (`GlobalNotificationMessage.GoldSaleBanner`). Clients before this take only dungeon-key colours from that message and ignore it; they get the chat line.
- **Client:** `app/src/goldsale.rs` (parsing, the hours-then-minutes line, the banner: the community banners' look in gold with the HUD's coin, 12 s, under the quest popups and above the community banners; a click opens `screens::GOLD_STORE`, and the crosshair stays off it), `game.rs` (`gold_sale`), `main.rs`. Three new lines in every `app/lang/*.json`.
- **Server:** `Shared/database/GoldStore.cs`, `WorldServer/core/objects/player/Player.cs` (the Nexus block beside the other event lines), `GlobalNotificationMessage.cs`.
- **Deploys:** the world server and the client; either works without the other. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: `gold.told.<account>` keys, gone after 10 minutes.
- **Timing:** the live Black Friday sale ends 2026-11-28 10:07 UTC, so the banner shows players only if this release deploys before then; after that nothing is sent until there's another `GoldDiscount`.

### The Ice Tomb harder, taller, busier and guided
- **What and why:** the owner found walls showing only once looked at, the floor beside them hanging over the sky: the tomb used line of sight (`VisibilityType` 1), so walls behind walls were sent late and the floor beside them drew the islands' cliffs. Now `VisibilityType` 0 (everything within the 22-tile sight). Taller walls as in the Arena, wider corridors (narrow ones with tall walls read as stacked walls), more monsters, the quest arrow through the whole run, a bit harder with new phases.
- **Files:** `Dungeons.xml` (visibility, `corridor="5"`); `tools/build_ice_tomb.py` (`WALL_HEIGHT` 2 and `<WallBase>`, `PATROLS`, seals `<Quest/>`, the `Court Gate`, the item icons' `EDICT_CELL`/`RIMEJAW_CELL`) and `tools/ice_tomb/art.py` (`wall_base`, `glacier_base`, `edict_scepter`, `rimejaw`), regenerated: `Ice Tomb.jm`, both `IceTombObjects.xml`, `IceTomb8x8.png`, `IceTomb16x16.png`, `types.json` (Court Gate 0xCA85); `IceTomb.cs` (`SealedDoor` raises and removes the gate); `BehaviorDb.IceTomb.cs` (keepers' phases, the Court's three, the gate, the loot); `CrucibleRules.cs` and the client's `crucible.rs` (cues 44-46, every `lang/*.json`); `Player.Leveling.cs` (`FindQuest` skips a dead quest monster: a broken seal stayed the quest); the Rust client's `view.rs` (`draw_wall`: tall walls drawn top-down with `<WallBase>` under the brow, no side against a wall as tall, see-through at 38% while over you, `tall_wall_cover`) and `crates/data` `ObjectDesc.wall_base`.
- **White bags:** both `CustomItems.xml` copies, 0x58B9 and 0x58BA; `ActivateEffect.Spread` and `.Frostbite`; `Player.Decree.cs` (`SpreadDecree`, the slow, an untiered scepter's look at the top tier in its `color`); `Player.Maw.cs` (`Frozen`, the slow, the same look rule); `tools/wiki_gear.py` reads `spread`, `condEffect` and `frostbite`.
- **Deploys:** world server and client together (old clients see the walls one tile tall, the new banners in English, and no icons for the two items). Nobody is disconnected; Redis untouched. Ice Tombs open during the deploy keep their old map until made again.
