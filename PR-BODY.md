Release 2.5.4

## For players

- **Game:** the desktop client's menus (the main menu, sign-in, register, character select, new character and the Sprite Editor) draw at most 30 frames a second, whatever Max frame rate and vsync say, so they use less CPU and GPU. In the game the frame rate is as before.
- **Items:** every one of the 25 Necromantic items' powers now has a look of its own when it works: Broadside's cannonball bursting in fire, smoke and seawater; Widowfang's venom splash; souls flying from kills to the Phylactery and bursting out of it; a burning brand of runes under enemies the Soulfire Staff curses; blood drawn to the Sanguine Wand; the Masquerade Robe's puff of smoke and confetti; a whirlpool when Davy's Bargain pulls you back, and under the Orb of the Drowned Tide's target; lightning from the sky for the Azure Tempest Seal and the Scepter of the Ivory Wyvern; flames for the Wyrmfire Katana; obsidian shards forming and shattering round the Wyrmplate's wearer; a golden ward and falling stars on everyone the Bulwark and the Tome reach; the Crown of Geb's cracks and rocks; the Blobomb's splatter; the Twilight Cataclysm's pillar of fire and spikes of ice; the Forgotten King's red mist; marble pillars striking up; the Skull's pentagram drawn in fire on the ground before its heart erupts; the Bow's black hole. The weapons, the Quiver, the Bulwark of Bes and the Twilight Cataclysm fire shots of their own (a sea-green slash, a venom-dripping fang, a stone arrow, a feathered serpent arrow, a soulfire bolt, a blood drop, a flaming slash, a spinning ankh, a fire-and-ice bolt, a void arrow), each leaving a trail of its colour; and every other shot in the game with a trail now shows it in the new client.
- **Fixes:** fewer lag spikes while anyone explores The Shatters, Oryx's Castle or the Mad Lab, whose walls hide what's behind them: each step there no longer makes a map's worth of memory (239 KB in the Shatters) for the server to clean up, which hurried the collections that pause every world at once. Realm 2 no longer freezes for good for everyone in it (monsters stood still, nothing could be hit, and only leaving the realm helped): it happened when the monsters of a small patch of the starting lands (the shore and lowlands round the spawn) were to come back while every tile of that patch was in some player's sight. The Void's portal, opened with a Vial of Pure Darkness where the Marble Colossus fell (Lost Halls and Elder Lost Halls), no longer opens under the Colossus's Realm Portal, which took every click on Enter: the Realm Portal now opens 3 tiles west.
- **Player sprites:** new looks drawn by players in the Sprite Editor: Coral Medallion (by Trenchant).

## Details

### Sight in worlds whose walls block it: no map-sized array per step

In worlds with `VisibilityType` 2 (The Shatters, Oryx's Castle, Mad Lab), `Player.CalculatePath` worked out what a player sees with a new `bool[map width, map height]` every time they stepped onto another tile: 239 KB for the Shatters' 489×489 map, about 1 MB/s for a player on the move. Arrays that size go to the large object heap, which counts towards full (gen 2) collections, and every collection pauses all worlds at once. It now marks the tiles in a `(2r+1)²` grid round the player (r the sight radius: 961 bytes at the default 15), kept by the player between steps and remade only when the sight radius changes. Same tiles in sight as before: the flood fill is unchanged, it only stops at the edge of the sight square, past which nothing was ever in sight. `Player.SightPath` (public static, for the tests) holds it; `source/WorldServer/core/objects/player/Player.Update.cs`.

Found while looking into the lag and ping spikes on the game VPS since 11/10. They are pauses of 150-330 ms in the world server's ticks (the Tick stats logs): garbage collections of 50-200 ms (`GC pause` lines; next to none before 11/10 16:48) and work on the Nexus's thread, which also runs every dungeon, vault and Arena opened from the Nexus. The VPS's CPU history (sysstat) shows its kernel time per packet at 2-3× the earlier level from 11/10 13:00-15:00 to 11/12 03:00, with no change in traffic, and a fixed tick (a Vault's) taking about twice as long. That is the machine (or something left running on it), not this code, and it has mostly recovered. This fix takes away one steady source of full collections. Server GC, tried as well, made the pauses worse (below), so the collector is unchanged.

Redeploy: world server only; nobody is disconnected (rolling deploy).

### Realm 2's thread no longer stops for good refilling a terrain

Every 60 s `RealmManager.EnsurePopulation` tops up each terrain's monsters (the old realm's 12 terrain types, which Realm 2 uses too) when there are fewer than 75% of its quota, and `Spawn` looked for each one's spot by picking random tiles until one was of that terrain, passable and out of every player's sight (no player within 30 tiles), with no limit. Realm 2's starter land, where everyone arrives, is a circle of radius 50 whose lowland terrains are noise patches: one of them can be under a thousand tiles, all within 30 tiles of players at or near the spawn. Once that terrain's monsters were thinned below 75%, the search could never end and the realm's thread spun on it for good: every player in the realm froze (no `NewTick`s), its Tick stats stopped, and the connections stayed up, so only leaving helped. The Nexus thread, with the dungeons, vaults and the Arena, went on.

Now `Spawn` looks at most 4000 random tiles (`RealmManager.FreeSpot`, public static for the tests) and places nothing when none fits; `EnsurePopulation` stops topping up that terrain until the next pass (and also stops after a bounded number of rolls, where an empty roll of the monster table could loop), and logs once per terrain per realm: `Realm 2 <id>: no free <terrain> tile for <n> more monsters (<there> of <quota> there; <tiles> tiles, <passable> passable, <players> players)`. `SpawnEvent`'s fallback search for an event's spot (the old realm's) has the same kind of limit and logs `no spot for the event <name>` instead of spinning. `source/WorldServer/core/worlds/RealmManager.cs`, `source/Tests/RealmSpawnSpotTests.cs`; SETUP-LOCAL.md's "Changes from upstream".

The tracer had nothing to do with it: it froze without `dotnet-trace` too. The earlier freezes fit: one 1 minute in (the first top-up, 60 s after the realm opened) and one 7 minutes in.

Redeploy: world server only; nobody is disconnected (rolling deploy). A realm already frozen on the live server stays frozen until its players leave and it closes.

- **Can it happen on the live server?** Yes, by the same mechanism: live Realm 2s are made by the same generator, and players at the spawn are common. It wasn't checked in the live logs (reading `deploy.log` / `restart.log` on play.dodmg.com wasn't allowed from this session): look for a realm whose `Tick stats` lines stop while it still has players, with no `Removed Thread` for it.
- **Found on the way, not changed:** `Load` and `Create` are handled on the socket thread, before the player exists, and `World.CreateNewPlayer` there calls `entity.Move`, which inserts the new player into the world's collision map (linked lists per 16×16 chunk) while the world's own thread may be moving others in the same chunk; its pet too (`Player.SpawnPetIfAttached`), and `EntitiesToAdd` is a plain `List`. That's a data race that could tie a chunk's list into a loop. It didn't cause the freeze seen here (the stack was `Spawn`), and it's left for a change of its own (posting the join onto the world's thread).

### Player sprites are a release check

`tools/community_sprites.py check` (new) fails while sprites admins approved in the Sprite Editor wait on the server without being taken into the repository (it lists them), or while the community sheets, the catalog and the item XML are out of step: a catalog cell empty or missing, an item no longer in the XML, or an item no longer pointed at its player sprite or credited (`<SpriteBy>`), as a regeneration could leave it. `--offline` checks only the repository; when the server can't be reached it fails and says so rather than passing. `rework_file` gained `write=False` to tell what it would change. AGENTS.md's "Releasing" has it as a new step 1, before the changelog section is named (`apply` writes under Unreleased); client-rs/AGENTS.md and SETUP-LOCAL.md point to it. Tooling and docs only: nothing to redeploy.

- `apply` skips a new look that is the item's sprite as it is now, pixel for pixel once scaled to the drawing's size (`unchanged`, reading the item's sheet through the client's manifest), and `check` names it as one to turn down in the Review tab: taking it in would change nothing players see and credit the player for someone else's art.
- `apply` keeps a blank line under `## For players` when it writes the first line of an empty section.
- **The owner must:** turn down #2 in the Sprite Editor's Review tab. It's the only approved sprite on the live server: a "new look" for the April Fools Skin Bag that is its existing 8×8 sprite doubled to 16×16, with no pixel changed (the item's description credits Seelpit for its art). Until it's turned down, `check` fails.

### The Void's portal no longer opens under the Colossus's Realm Portal

The Marble Colossus (and the Elder one) is raised on the Colossus room's controller and goes back there to die, so its `DropPortalOnDeath("Realm Portal")` opened on the controller's spot, and so does the tear into the Void (`LH Vial Effect`) that a Vial of Pure Darkness turns into the Void's portal. The two portals overlapped and Enter always took the Realm Portal, so players couldn't reach the (Elder) Void. The Realm Portal now opens 3 tiles west of where the Colossus falls (the regular Colossus's Elder Lost Halls portal was already 3 tiles east). `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.LostHalls.cs`; the wiki's Lost Halls section says where each portal opens.

### Menus hold to 30 fps

While the client is on `Phase::Menu` (every screen before the game), the frame pacing in `client-rs/app/src/main.rs` holds to `MENU_FPS` (30), or to the player's Max frame rate when that's lower (`frame_cap`). It's the same sleep the Max frame rate setting already used, so it applies with vsync on or off; `--bench` still runs uncapped. Desktop only: the browser paces its frames itself (no sleeping on the page's thread), as Max frame rate never applied there. The in-game Escape menu and windows are part of the game and keep its rate.

Redeploy: client only; nobody is disconnected.

### The Necromantic items' looks: an effect per power, their own shots, projectile trails

Each power in `Player.RelicPowers.cs` now also sends `ShowEffect` 41 (`EffectType.Relic`, new) through `RelicFx`: pos2.y says which look (`Fx`, 1-28, the same numbers as the client's `relic::look`), pos2.x its radius, `Duration` how long (the Phylactery's: how many souls), and the target object is what it follows or flies to. They go beside the older generic effects (AreaBlast, Diffuse, Lightning...), which clients before 2.5.4 still show and older clients skip type 41 (the Rust client's `_ => {}`, the AIR client's switch). Per-hit looks are throttled per player (`HitFxDue`: Stone Gaze 350 ms, Bloodthirst 300, Ignition 250). `RelicKill` takes the victim (`Enemy.cs` passes it), for the soul flying from it.

The Rust client draws them in `client-rs/app/src/particles/relic.rs` (new): particles with new motions in `particles.rs` (`Ballistic`: thrown, falling, bouncing; `Swirl`: circling an object as it moves; `Seek`: flying to an object or a spot, leaving sparks), particles that wait (`delay`), fade (`fade`, `opacity`) and grow (`grow`); decals drawn with the ground marks under what stands (`DecalKind`: Ring, Puddle, Sigil, Whirlpool, Cracks, Pentagram, BlackHole, Frost, Scorch, Ward, Stars); auras giving off particles round an object or a spot for the power's time. Particles off (Options) shows none of it; others' spells hidden hides those cast by other players, as before. `--effect 101`-`128` (tests) shows each look on you every 0.9 s.

Projectile trails: the client didn't draw `<ParticleTrail>` at all. `ProjectileDesc.trail` (`crates/data`, colour from the text, magenta when empty as in the AIR client; `intensity`, `lifetimeMS`) and `Particles::trails` (about 50 sparks a second per shot, times its intensity, hidden with the shots they follow) now do, for every projectile that has one (about 120 in the game's XML, the Quiver's and the Bulwark's among them).

New shots: ten Necromantic items fire a projectile of their own instead of their base's, drawn by `tools/build_resurrection.py` (`SHOTS`: shapes worked out per pixel in the shot's own frame, so slashes curve and arrows point; the ankh is pixel art and spins, `<Rotation>150`) into cells 64-73 of `Resurrection8x8.png`, types 0x8C55-0x8C5E kept in `tools/resurrection/types.json`, and given a `<ParticleTrail>` of the item's colour (`TRAILS`). `build_resurrection.py --shots` writes only these into the sheet and both `Resurrection.xml` copies as they are (a full run would rewrite the items tuned by hand since; it adds them at its end too), and running it again changes nothing.

Redeploy: client and world server (the XML, the sheet, the effect); nobody is disconnected. Players on an older client see the old effects and the new shots' art (the XML and sheet come with the client), but no trails or new looks.

- **Not done:** the per-hit looks are throttled per player, not per enemy, so in a group each player's show at most every 250-350 ms. The Phylactery's stored souls aren't shown round its keeper between kills (only the soul flying in and the count note).

### Test stacks start maxed, with a Lil' Oryx

`ServerSettings.testStack` (new, default off): `Player.TestStackStart`, run as a player enters any world, maxes the character as `/max` does (level 20, every stat at its cap), fills health and mana, and makes its pet a Lil' Oryx (60 health and mana every 1.5 s) if it has none or another. `iso_stack.py` and `rolling_restart.py --iso` set it in their world servers' `wServer.json`; the live configs never have it. So scripted tests never run out of mana or die of a level-1 character's health.

- Found while testing: on this PC Windows' dynamic port range is 1024-15000 and Hyper-V reserves 1973-2172, so every low `iso_stack.py` slot's world port (2052+N) is reserved or can be taken by an outgoing connection; slot 13000 (world 15052, account 21081, Redis 19380) runs. Not changed in the tools.
