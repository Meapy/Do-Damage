Release 2.6.7: the Mechanic's turrets have health that allies can heal and buff and monsters can attack, reach further and fall off with range; grown pets catch up with their new size, every Lil' Oryx and boss pet hits at least about 1,510, and a Huge pet at level 30 can be tuned; an admin's /summon brings a player into the Crucible without using one of their climbs. Deploys the world servers and the client (the turret and pet tuning changes need both); nobody is disconnected.

## For players

### Pets
- **Grown pets catch up with their new size:** feeding a grown pet through each size it grew to now raises its damage towards what pets of that size do (85% of the way for Small, Medium and Large, 55% for Huge) and its healing too. Any pet grown all the way to Huge does about 1,190 damage a second at level 30 instead of a few hundred: Lil' Thessal about 1,310 (was 594), Bes, Nut and Geb about 1,430, a Tiny cat about 1,190 (was 54). It heals more as well (Lil' Thessal 77 HP every 2.5 s, was 67).
- **The Lil' Oryxes and boss pets stay ahead:** every Huge pet from its own generator now does at least about 1,510 a second at level 30. Lil' Oryx (1,080 before), Lil' Ferrogrim (1,080), Lil' Nerissa (1,170), Lil' Pyrrhax (1,260) and Lil' Chronarch (1,440) come up to it; the rest (Lil' Wine Cellar Oryx 1,620 up to Lil' Ascended Oryx and Lil' Mysterious One 3,240) are unchanged.
- **Tune a Huge pet at level 30:** in the Pen Keeper's window, click the damage, HP or MP box to make that stronger, then pick what to take it from: 5% moves from one to the other for 1,000 fame a step, up to 25% either way on each, and it can be moved back the same way. The boxes show the tuning, and it stays with the pet in its generator.

### Mechanic
- **Turrets have health instead of a timer:** each turret has health (400 at T0 up to 1,000 at T8; the War Turret 700, Ferrogrim's Bastion Turret 1,100), shown in a health bar under it and in the ring round its plate, and wears it all down over the time it used to last (12 seconds at T0 up to 20 at T8). When its health runs out it blows up as before. Allies' abilities reach it as they reach players standing there: heals (a priest's tome, a vampire blast, Nerissa's tide), Healing (20 a second), Damaging (hits a quarter harder), Berserk (fires a quarter faster), Armored (wears a third slower), Invulnerable (doesn't wear), seals and tomes' boosts (maximum health, attack, dexterity, defense), and cleanses. A turret your priest keeps healing keeps firing.
- **Monsters attack turrets:** monsters still go for players first, but one with no player in its reach turns on the nearest turret: it shoots at it and chases it, and its shots (with their conditions, such as a stun that stops the turret firing) and its bombs hurt it. Their shots stop on the turret instead of flying through it.
- **Further, but weaker far away:** turrets reach about 11 tiles (were 9; the Bastion Turret 11.5 from 9.6). Their shots hit in full out to 3 tiles, then less the further they fly, down to half at the end of their reach.

## Details

### Pets: grown pets catch up, a Huge floor, tuning

- **What and why:** asked to improve pet scaling (a Large Thessal grown to Huge did 594 a second at level 30 against Lil' Wine Cellar Oryx's 1,623), to cross-check every pet while keeping the Oryxes' and boss pets ahead, and to let a maxed Huge pet be fine-tuned, raising one stat for fame while lowering another. Every stone's kit sits on its size's norm (Tiny 49-51, Small 124-127, Medium 249-252, Large 499-600), but a grown pet kept its old size's kit, and the Huge stones ran from 988 (Lil' Ferrogrim) to 3,002.
- **Shared** (`PetLevels.cs`): `Grown(level, baseMax, damage)` climbs, level by level through each size the pet grew to, to `SizeDps[k] / SizeDps[stone]` times `GrownShare` 0.85 (Huge `GrownHugeShare` 0.55) for damage and `SizeHeal` ratios times `HealShare` 0.85 for healing (the norms of `tools/pet_healing.py`); it's folded into `DamageScale` and `HealScale`, so nothing grows at the moment of growing and a never-grown pet is unchanged. `Floor(stoneSize, dps)`: a Huge stone's kit counts as at least `HugeFloor` 1,400. Tuning: `TuneStep` 5%, `TuneMax` 5 steps, `TuneFame` 1,000; `CanTune` (Huge, level 30), `Tune`, `TuneScale`, `Retune`, `Untuned`; `Heal` takes a tune factor. `DbChar.PetTune` (`petTune`, int[3], empty for none), `ItemData.PetTune` (left out when none).
- **World server:** `Player.PetPen.cs`: `PetTune`, applied (with `Floor`) in `PetPower` and `PetPenState`, carried by `RemovePet`, put-in and `GivePetGeneratorBack`; act 14 `TunePet` (`slot` = from x 3 + to; fame through `PayPen`, logged). `Player.UseItem.cs` (using a stone, the test stack's Lil' Oryx), `Player.cs` (load and save), `ImprintHandlers.cs` (keeps it on imprint). `PetPenData` adds the tuning (3 sbytes) and a step's price (int) at its end.
- **Client:** `bsk-proto` `PetTune` (`PetPen.tune`, optional); `petlevels.rs` mirrors `grown`, `floor`, `can_tune`, `tune_scale`, `stone_tune` (and `effective_dps` takes the kit as f32); `hud.rs` tooltips apply the floor and a stone's tuning; `petpen.rs`: when the server sends a step price, the stat boxes are clickable (gold ring on the one to raise), a row under them names it and offers the other two to take from, and each box says "Tuned +10%"; the window grows 44 px for the row. Eight new lines in every `app/lang/*.json`.
- **Redeploy:** world servers and client. Balance and the floor need only the server; an older client's window shows the server's new numbers but its tooltips the old ones, and it can't tune (it ignores the added bytes). Nobody is disconnected. Redis: new `petTune` field on characters and `PetTune` in item data, nothing migrated.
- **Wiki:** `tools/wiki_gear.py` (the Huge floor in the cards' numbers and order, a "Grown to Huge" line on every other pet's card), `website/wiki/pets.md` (growing, tuning, the Huge row, the Lil' Oryx and boss pet tables, Bes and Thessal grown).
- **Not done:** not tried in game; the shares (85%, 55%), the floor and the tuning price are first guesses for playtesting. Tuning moves healing and damage at the same 5%, though a percent of damage is worth more than a percent of healing.

### Admin /summon into the Crucible costs no climb

- **World server** (`World.cs`, `DoomedRealmsCommands.cs`, `CrucibleWorld.cs`): `World.Invite` takes `free: true`, which an admin's `/summon` passes and which marks the account as brought in free to that world (`IsComped`). When a climb begins, `CrucibleWorld.Begin` skips `CrucibleAccess.Spend` for those accounts, so a player with no climbs left can be summoned into the lobby by an admin and the climb doesn't count against their day. Players' own `/summon` and `/visit` are unchanged.
- Not covered: an admin's summon between two world servers (`RemoteVisits`), and a climb already under way (the Crucible takes nobody new then, admins included).
- Redeploy: world servers only, rolling, nobody disconnected. No Redis data changed.

### Mechanic: turrets with health, range and falloff

- **What and why:** asked for turrets with health that allies can heal and buff like players, wearing down over time instead of a timer, dying at 0, with a bit more range and damage that falls off over it.
- **World server:** `Turret.cs` is a vulnerable `StaticObject` with the blueprint's `hp` (`ActivateEffect.TurretHp`, default 600) and a `MaximumHealth` stat; each tick it wears `hp / duration` (Armored x2/3, defense boost -1% a point to -50%, Invulnerable/Invincible none, Bleeding +20/s, Healing -20/s unless Sick) and blows up at 0 (`Detonate`, as before). `Heal`, `Buff`, `Boost` (MaxHP raises max and health; Attack, Dexterity, Defense as percents; `noStack` refreshes) and `TurretsNear` take allies' abilities; `Player.Turrets.cs` `HealTurrets`, `BuffTurrets` (positive conditions only), `BoostTurrets` are called from HealNova, VampireBlast, ConditionEffectAura, StatBoostAura, the tomes' shared conditions and boosts, RemoveNegativeConditions (cleanse), Disco Lights (`Player.UseItem.Doomed.cs`) and Nerissa's tide (`Player.CruciblePowers.cs`). Damaging, Attack boosts and Weak scale its shots and Bastion slam; Berserk and Dexterity its fire rate; Paralyzed stops it firing. Looks 55/56 now carry how long its health lasts at its plain wear. Falloff: `Turret.Falloff` (full to 3 tiles, half at its reach), passed as `ServerPlayerShoot.FalloffReach` (not sent) into `ValidatedProjectile` and applied in `Player.EnemyHit`. Player shots no longer hurt a turret as a vulnerable static object. `Turret` is public now, for the tests.
- **Content:** both `DoomedRealms.xml` copies: the blueprints' `hp`, Tower1-9 and SpecialTower shots 680 ms (were 560); the Mechanic's description. `tools/build_crucible.py`: the Bastion Turret's `hp="1100"`, its shot 960 ms (was 800) and description; rerun, both `CrucibleObjects.xml` copies (the rest of its output unchanged).
- **Client:** `particles/turret.rs` (the plate's ring shows health; its last-seconds warning and the plate's end come from the health left), `view.rs` (a health bar under turrets), `bsk-world` `combat.rs` (`turret_falloff`, used for our own turret shots' hits), `hud.rs` (tooltip: HP and wear, falloff, "Allies' heals and buffs reach it"; the short line names its HP), translations in every `app/lang/*.json`.
- **Redeploy:** world servers and client together. An older client still sees turrets and their shots, but its plate disappears at the old time even if the turret was healed, it shows no health bar and its damage numbers ignore the falloff (the server's are right). Nobody is disconnected. No Redis data changed.
- **Wiki:** `tools/wiki_gear.py` (the blueprints' line: HP, wear, falloff, blast at 0 health), `website/wiki/classes.md` (the Mechanic's turrets), `crucible.md` (the Bastion Turret), `gear-statistics.md` (a note that the Mechanic's measured row predates this).
- **Docs:** SETUP-LOCAL.md "Changes from upstream"; AGENTS.md (a ground rule to write down testing pitfalls) and client-rs/AGENTS.md (the pitfalls met here).
- **Not done:** pets heal only their owner, not turrets. The balance (HP per tier, falloff to half) hasn't been playtested.

### Mechanic: monsters attack turrets

- **What and why:** asked for bosses and minions to attack turrets, still going for players first and turning to turrets only when they can't reach a player.
- **World server:** `EntityUtils.GetAttackTarget` (the nearest player, else `Turret.NearestFor`, the nearest turret in the same reach; hostile monsters only, never allies' summons) replaces the player lookup where monsters aim or chase: `Shoot` (and its lowest-health form), `Grenade`, `Follow`, `Chase`, `Charge`, `ShootFormation`, `RingAttack`, `TossObject`, `TossObject2`, `Swirl`; `NewFollow` and `NewCharge` through `World.FindAttackTarget` (turrets within 15 tiles); the second engine's (`DoomedRuntime.cs`) `GetNearestEntity` when it isn't a `gate` check. Transitions and gate checks still look for players only, so a turret never wakes a boss or moves a phase on. `Turret` implements `IPlayer` and stays in the enemies' collision map. Clients only report hits on their own player, so `TurretShots.cs` follows each hostile monster's shot fired within reach of a turret on the server (hooked in `World.BroadcastIfVisible` and `BroadcastEnemyShootIfVisible`, ticked in `World.UpdateLogic`): along `ValidatedProjectile.GetPosition` in 20 ms steps, stopped by walls and missing ground as the client stops it, a hit within 0.5 tiles, piercing shots going on; at most 2,000 followed a world. `Turret.Hurt` takes defense off, applies the shot's conditions and sends a `DamageMessage`; `Turret.HurtAround` takes `Grenade`'s and `ThrowAttack`'s blasts. At 0 health the turret blows up as when worn down.
- **Client:** `bsk-world` `combat.rs` `hit_target`: a monster's shot ends on a turret (the client sends OtherHit, as for a shot hitting another player); the hit's number comes from the server's `DamageMessage`.
- **Redeploy:** world servers and client together. An older client sees the damage numbers, but monsters' shots fly through turrets on its screen. Nobody is disconnected. No Redis data changed.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/classes.md` (the Mechanic's turrets).
- **Not done:** `Aoe` (it only hurts enemies), the Black Between's own blasts and monsters' contact damage don't reach turrets. Not tried in game; the server's and the client's idea of a hit can differ by a step on fast shots.
