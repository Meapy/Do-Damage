Release 2.6.4: the Crucible, a boss-only tower over the Arena (five champions, floors that change as they fight, a white bag each), a Gilded Realm Pass can rally a community goal (everyone's help counts double until it ends, and the top prize is theirs too) and shows the way to a dungeon's boss, and the chat's emoji are redrawn and brought up to date (salute included); deploys client and world servers (players are not disconnected; the Crucible needs both)

## For players

- **The Crucible:** a tower of nothing but bosses, over the Arena. Up to six climb its five floors together, each floor a shape of its own (a star fort, a ragged atoll, a forge hall in a cross, the rings of an orrery over open sky, a star of marble), and on each a champion arrives: **Ferrogrim, the Iron Warden** (iron grates rise and sentinels hold them, blades of fire sweep the floor, his hammer whirls), **Nerissa, the Tidecaller** (bands of tide, then the sea floods everything but the coral isles, a whirlpool, a maelstrom), **Pyrrhax, the Forge Tyrant** (lava veins, a meteor storm, the floor's rim crumbling as lava pours in, a molten core), **Chronarch, the Hour Thief** (hour and minute hands sweeping, the clock's hours chiming half the floor at a time, echoes of him, the clock running backwards) and **Vaelith, the Crucible Sovereign**, who raises the ghosts of the four below one by one, each turning his floor into theirs, then breaks the Apex and fights Unbound.
  - Every phase is called out on screen and each champion has its own aura (forge sparks, circling spray, embers and smoke, turning gears, falling feathers of flame) that builds as it's worn down (molten iron dripping and the forge beating through the floor, ripples and columns of sea, scorched floor and lava bursting up, the clock's face and time tearing both ways, the tower's sigil and pillars of light, at a third and at two thirds of its health gone) and a burst for each phase. Your pet and summoned allies climb with you. Between floors you're healed and the Ascension Sigil turns; then you climb on foot: a way up builds itself out of the sky ahead of you, winding up to the next floor in that floor's stone with its pillars along it, the floor comes together round you as you reach it, and everything behind breaks away and falls. Shots fly on over the gaps in its floors and the sky beside them instead of stopping at the edge. It has its own sky, which changes floor by floor, its own music and a leaderboard (`/crucible status`).
  - **Getting in:** reach round 50 in the Arena (rounds reached before count) or hold a Gilded Realm Pass. **One climb a day**, two with the Pass (a new day at 00:00 UTC); leaving the lobby before the climb begins costs nothing. Its portal is sunk into the Nexus's shrine across Realm 2's vortex from the Arena's (a vortex of fire in a lip of iron and gold, the floors' gems lighting in turn, embers rising off it), or type `/crucible`. Falling sends you to the Nexus instead of killing you. There's no fame for a climb: the champions' loot is the reward.
  - **White bags**, 1% each, an ability for a class each, with a power of its own: **Ferrogrim's Bastion Turret** (Mechanic: his sentinel, firing shards and slamming the ground, breaking armor and Armoring allies beside it), **Nerissa's Tidepearl** (Druid: a substitute of five tridents and a heal, then three waves of tide that hurt enemies and heal allies), **Pyrrhax's Meteor Mortar** (Pirate: a molten shell arcing to where you aim, then four meteors and a burning crater), **Chronarch's Coat of Stolen Hours** (Blademaster: Armored and Damaging, then time stops round you while the clock's hand sweeps twice) and **Vaelith's Final Aria** (Bard: Inspired, then four verses of rings of light, the last a crescendo that breaks armor and sends allies berserk).
  - The wiki has a page for it (The Crucible), and the items are on their gear pages.
- **Realm Pass:** a **Gilded** pass has two new perks.
  - **Rally a community goal.** Open the Warcaller's window, pick a goal and press **Rally x2** (once on each goal). From then until the goal ends everyone's help with that goal counts **double**, in the goal's total and in each helper's own share, so the whole realm climbs its tiers twice as fast. The realm is told ("Name rallied the realm!") in a banner and in chat, the goal's card burns gold with "x2 · rallied by Name" (and "+N" when others rallied it too), and the goal shows an **x2** in the list. Rallying a goal that is already burning changes nothing for the others, but still shares you its top prize. The one who rallied also shares the goal's **top prize** once it ends, whatever their place in its top 3 (they still need the goal's minimum share of their own). Without a Gilded pass the button is there, dimmed with a lock, and says what it takes.
  - **The way to the boss.** In a dungeon, a trail of small gold arrows runs along the **shortest walk** to its boss, flowing toward it and fading out ahead, with a chip at the foot of the view: the boss's picture, an arrow turned the way to walk and how many tiles are left. It finds its way round walls, goes through doors only when there's no other way, follows the boss as it moves and goes away once you're close. Options, Gameplay has a switch to turn it off.
  - Both are on the pass's **Gilded perks** tab and the wiki's Realm Pass, Community Goals and Dungeons pages.
- **Chat:** the emoji you can type in chat and speech balloons are up to date. All of the old emoji (the 2014-style pictures) are **redrawn** in a clearer style, and **every emoji added since** is there: the salute (`:saluting_face:`, or `:salute:`), the melting, shaking and face-holding-back-tears faces, the pink and light-blue hearts, the new hands, animals and foods, people, and the flags, over 1,900 in all. Every code that worked before shows the same emoji (a nicer one); the animated ones and the bags are as they were.

### Rallying a community goal

- **Rules** (`CommunityGoals.cs`): `COMMUNITY_BOOST` (175, the run's key) from a client; `Rally` checks the account isn't a guest, the world isn't a test world, the pass is Gilded (`PassTracker.IsGilded`), the run is running and not maxed out (`CommunityData.Finished`). The account goes into the Redis set `community:<run>:rallied` first (an account rallies a run once, whichever server it asks; taken back if the next step fails), then a Lua script on the run's hash `community:<run>` sets `boostUntil` to the run's end (`boostBy` the first rallier's name) and counts `boosts`. `CommunityGoals.Count` multiplies each event's amount by `rallyMultiplier` while the run is rallied, in its total and the helper's share (the shared-event de-duplication still counts an event once), using this server's own `Rallies` map, which each 5-second refresh also fills from Redis `boostUntil`, so the other world server of a rolling deploy counts it as well. `RunState` carries `BoostUntil`, `BoostBy` and `Rallied` (the set's size).
- **Top prize:** `Claimable` adds the top's prize when the goal has ended and the account is in the top `TopCount` **or rallied** it (the minimum share still applies, and `community:<run>:claimed`'s `255` still keeps it to one claim). `Mine` reads the set for each run.
- **Data** (`pass.json`, `PassCommunityBoost`): `community.rallyMultiplier` 2 (the rally lasts the run out), in its `_about`; the wiki builds its numbers from them (`tools/wiki_pass.py`: `pass_rally_mult`).
- **Messages:** `COMMUNITY_DATA` gets a section after the goals (older clients stop before it): whether the viewer can rally, the minutes (now always 0: it lasts the run out) and the multiplier, then each goal's rally (key, ends, by, whether ours, how many). `COMMUNITY_NOTICE` kinds 6 (to everyone: "X rallied the realm!") and 7 (the rallier's answer). Rust client: `community.rs` (the pill: Rally button, burning pill with the time left, "Rallied" chip, locked chip; the card's gold edge, the list's x2; the top prize note "Yours: you rallied it"), `proto` (`CommunityBoost`, `CommunityRally`, the new `CommunityData` fields and a test), `pass.rs` (a perk card).
- **Redeploys:** world servers and client together. An older client never sees the button (so can't rally) but its goals count double all the same, and its window shows the rally's banner as any other. An older server never says it can rally, so a newer client shows the locked chip. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: new keys `community:<run>:rallied` and the fields `boostUntil`, `boostBy`, `boosts` on the run's hash, kept as long as the run's; nothing to migrate.

### The dungeon guide

- **Server:** `GUIDE_SUBSCRIBE` (176, a bool) from a client that can show it, `DUNGEON_GUIDE` (177) to it: the boss's type and place, the walk's length and every second tile of it. `Player.Guide.cs` (`HandleGuide`, every 500 ms): for a Gilded player in a dungeon (`InDungeon`: a world with quests that isn't a realm, the Nexus, a vault, a test world, a minigame, a guild hall, a daily-quest world or stacked floors) whose `Quest` is a living boss, it makes the path again when the player or the boss has moved 3 tiles, or every 6 s (every 5 s after a failed search) and sends it; an empty one takes the guide away. `core/guide/GuidePath.cs`: A* over the world's `GroundTile`s (no-walk ground and the objects that fill their square block; eight ways, a diagonal only past two open squares), to within a square of the boss; if no way is open it searches again letting doors and barriers (objects filling their square) be crossed at a cost of 6 each, so it points through the door; at most 120,000 squares looked at.
- **Client** (`guide.rs`): the trail is the path from the player's feet on (the tiles passed are dropped), arrows every 1.8 tiles flowing toward the boss at 3.2 tiles a second, a brightness wave running along it, a glow, faded in by the feet and out over 46 tiles, drawn in screen space from the camera so a turned camera turns them; hidden when under 7 tiles of walking are left or the path is 16 s old. The chip at the foot of the view. Options, Gameplay: `dungeonGuide` (default on); off, the client tells the server (`GuideSubscribe 0`) and nothing is sent.
- **Redeploys:** world servers and client together. An older client never subscribes and gets nothing; an older server ignores the subscribe. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: none.

### Emoji brought up to date

- **Art:** the sheet `client/src/kabam/rotmg/assets/custom/images/Emotes.png` (16 pixels a cell, 16 cells a row) was Apple-style art from 2014, 880 cells, no emoji after Unicode 8. `tools/update_emotes.py` (new, rerunnable, downloads its inputs into a folder you name) redraws every cell that is a plain emoji (858 of the 880; the rest are `:kappa:` and the cell nothing used) from Google's **Noto Emoji** 72 px PNGs (Apache 2.0; the country flags come from the region-flags set beside them, public domain), trimmed and fitted to 16 pixels, and **appends** a cell per emoji the sheet lacked (1,032, in the order of GitHub's gemoji list), taking the sheet from 256 x 880 to 256 x 1920. Every old cell keeps its number, so every old code (and the `:)`, `<3` and similar shortcuts) is unchanged.
- **Codes:** `client/src/kabam/rotmg/emotes/Emotes.as` gets an `addEmote` line per new code, gemoji's names (`:saluting_face:`, `:melting_face:`, `:shaking_face:`, `:pink_heart:`...) plus **`:salute:`**. `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py` was rerun: `client-rs/assets/manifest.json` now lists 1,941 emotes (was 902). No Rust code changed; the client already draws whatever the manifest holds.
- Credits: Noto Emoji in the README's Credits; `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md` (Chat), `website/wiki/commands.md` (Emoji in chat), `SETUP-LOCAL.md` ("Changes from upstream").

### Pull requests on Testing

- **What and why:** the owner asked for each pull request to be deployed to the testing server, so it can be tried before it's merged. `.github/workflows/testing.yml` (new) runs `deploy/deploy.py --testing-only` on opening, pushing to, reopening or readying a pull request (not drafts or forks), and puts `main` back on Testing when one is closed without merging into `main`; "Run workflow" deploys any branch there.
- **How:** `--testing-only` builds only `source/WorldServer` and the Testing bundle (`stage_world` and `stage_testing`, taken out of `build` and `build_website` unchanged), and `install-site.sh --testing-only` installs only Testing, with a rolling deploy; the website, Caddy, the Discord bot and its tools are left alone. Both a full deploy and a Testing one write `/opt/bsk/testing.txt` (which pull request or commit, when), which `--status` and the install show.
- **Testing was out of reach for browser and https players** (everyone on the Rust client): the gateway took any target on port 2051 for its own world proxy (`Router::is_proxy` compared only the port), so `wss://play.dodmg.com/ws/169.58.244.146:2051` went straight to one of Europe 2's world servers, and Testing never saw the sign-in. Now only a loopback host on that port is this machine's proxy (`is_local_proxy`, with a test); other machines' world servers are connected to as they are. Found when PR #81, on Testing, still sent its admin to Europe 2.
- **Rank 6 lets you into Testing too.** Testing's door (`HelloHandler`) only took the account's `admin` flag, and the owner's account is rank 6 without it, so it was sent on to Europe 2 even with the gateway fixed. `DbAccount.IsAdmin` (the flag or rank 6, as the sprite review, `/autoaim` and the Champions board already count it) now decides there and in `MessageHandlers.IsEnabledOrAdminOnly`, whose Testing check also refused a whitelisted player unless they had the flag as well.
- **Files:** `.github/workflows/testing.yml` (new), `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (a comment), `deploy/deploy.py`, `deploy/remote/install-site.sh`, `client-rs/gateway/src/world.rs`, `source/Shared/database/account/DbAccount.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/net/handlers/HelloHandler.cs` and `MessageHandlers.cs`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md` ("Pull requests on Testing", "Changes from upstream").
- **Redeploys:** the world servers (rolling), the gateway (a new slot while the old one hands over) and the website machine (the new `install-site.sh` and `testing.txt`); nobody is disconnected. Redis: none. Until the gateway is deployed, "Testing (admin)" goes to Europe 2 for browser and https clients.
- **For the owner:** it's off until set up (SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Pull requests on Testing"): create the `testing` environment with `VPS_SSH_KEY`, `VPS_KNOWN_HOSTS` and `VPS_CONFIG` (best a key of its own that only the website machine takes, since a pull request's workflow can read that environment's secrets), then set `TESTING_DEPLOY_ENABLED` to `true`.
- **A test client per pull request:** the workflow's `client` job (Windows, no secrets) builds the same commit's desktop client and keeps it 14 days as the run's artifact, `Do-Damage-testing-pr<number>`, so client and server changes are tried together on Testing. Unsigned, so it never updates itself and no player's client takes it. It signs in at the repository variable `TESTING_CLIENT_ACCOUNT`, by default `http://play.dodmg.com:8080` (plain TCP, straight to Testing, which works before this release's gateway is live; the password goes unencrypted); `https://play.dodmg.com/api` once it is.
- **Not done:** nothing is published from a pull request. Testing plays on the live Redis, so pull requests that change what's saved stay drafts until tried on an isolated stack.
