Release 2.5.7: chat channels, picked with tabs on the chat box or /all, /local, /guild and /party, a softer Empowered Malus with every Empowered boss made doable, and Necromantic Harvest (relics twice as often until 1 December) (deploys the client, the world server and the website; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Chat:** tabs on the chat box's top edge (All, Local, Guild, Party) pick where the lines you type go, the box taking the channel's colour, and `/all`, `/local`, `/guild` or `/party` in front of a line sends just that line there. `/all` reaches players on every world; `/local` is the world you are in, as plain chat always was.
- **Dungeons:** Empowered Malus (the Necromantic Relic in the Elder Cultist Hideout) is easier but still hard: his shots 15% harder than the Elder Malus' (was 30%; enraged 25%, was 45%), his own attacks 10% faster (was 25%; enraged 25%, was 45%), Cult Sigils' marks lighter, his Blood Moon Ritual's three souls at 1.2% of his health each (was 2%), four rings of 8 bolts during the chant (was six of 10) and one ring of 20 if it holds (was two of 24). Every Empowered boss: no telegraphed mark deals more than 350 before its damage scale (at most ~508 enraged, before defense: under a 670-health Wizard), Last Breath's three rings of marks have gaps of over a tile (6, 10 and 14 marks of radius 1.2, were 8, 14 and 20 of 1.4 with no gaps), and the Soul Nova waits until a phase attack is over.
- **Events:** Necromantic Harvest, from the deploy until the end of 30 November (UTC; it ends by itself at 1 December 00:00): every Elder boss drops a Necromantic Relic twice as often, 10% instead of 5% for each player who did at least 2% of the damage, before loot boosts. The Nexus says so on arrival, `/boosts` lists it, the wiki's Necromantic Relic page has a note, and everyone online hears when it ends.

- **Items:** the Coral Medallion drops half as often from Elder Thessal the Mermaid Goddess (0.5% instead of 1%).

## Details

### Chat channels

- **What changed:** `/all` and `/local` are read as channels in `PlayerTextHandler` and go through the same checks as plain text (name, mute, spam; `/all` alone answers with its usage). `/all` publishes a new `ChatType.All` over the inter-server chat channel, and every world server delivers it to its players (`Player.AllReceived`: recipient `*All*`, no speech bubble). Plain text, `/guild` and `/party` work as before. On the client, `hud::ChatChannel` (All, Local, Guild, Party) draws four tabs on the chat box's top edge (`draw_chat_tabs`, `ChatClickTarget::Channel`): each sized to its label with a dot in its channel's colour, the picked one joined to the box like a browser tab, and the box's edge and glow in that colour (All amber, Local pale lilac, Guild green, Party blue, as their lines in the chat); the others lighten under the mouse; Enter sends the line through `ChatChannel::send_line`, which puts the channel's command in front unless the line already starts with `/`. The choice lives in `App.chat_channel` and isn't saved.
- **Files:** `source/Shared/isc/ChatType.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/net/handlers/PlayerTextHandler.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/miscfile/ChatManager.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/objects/player/Player.Chat.cs`, `client-rs/app/src/hud.rs`, `client-rs/app/src/main.rs`, `client-rs/app/src/login.rs` (the `--chat <channel>[:text]` test flag, in `client-rs/AGENTS.md`), `client-rs/app/lang/*.json` (Local, Guild and Party added to all ten tables; All was there), `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/getting-started.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the client. Nothing in Redis. Until every world server runs this build, `/all` only reaches the players on the ones that do: an older world server doesn't know the new chat type and drops it.
- **Not done or known to be off:** `/all`, `/local`, `/guild` and `/party` aren't in the `/help` list (they aren't command classes).

### Deploy from the PC: gateway build in WSL

- **What changed:** on Windows, `deploy/deploy.py` (`build_gateway`) no longer runs cargo on the checkout through `/mnt/c`, which was slow and made WSL show its "Performance Tip" pop-up on every deploy. It sends `client-rs` (without `target`) into WSL as a tar on stdin, unpacks it in `~/.cache/bsk-gateway-src` and builds there (same target folder, `~/.cache/bsk-gateway`). Linux and the GitHub workflow build as before.
- **Files:** `deploy/deploy.py`.
- **Redeploy:** nothing; it only changes how a deploy from the PC builds.

### Empowered Malus softer, and every Empowered boss doable

- **What changed:** `Empowered.Tuning` (calm and enraged damage multiplier and haste) with a per-boss table, `Empowered.Tunings`; only `Elder LH Malus 2` differs from the default (1.15 / 1.25 damage, 1.1 / 1.25 haste; the default stays 1.3 / 1.45 and 1.25 / 1.45). His Elder behaviour cycles four cultists' patterns and fires five at once in his Fury, so the Empowered speed-up made it a wall of bullets. `Arena.BlastCap` (350) caps every phase blast's base damage: the outliers were the Void Entity's Singularity (515), the Marble Colossus's Colossal Wheel (396), the Void's and Colossus's Last Breath marks and the Forgotten King's Royal Decree (355). Last Breath's opening rings (`EmpoweredPhases.LastBreathRings`) were solid walls with no gap, so the only way out was to outrun all three in 1.6 s. The Soul Nova holds while a phase attack plays (`_stage`). Malus' Cult Sigils: line marks 0.85x (was 1.0x), sigils 1.25x (was 1.5x); Blood Moon Ritual: souls `RitualSoulShare` 1.2% each (2% made three souls of ~7,000 health to kill in 6.5 s), four rings of 8 bolts 1.5 s apart, one ring of 20 if the ritual holds. I looked through the other 29 signature phases for any with no safe spot: the Void's Event Horizon (bands between its rings), the Archmage's Arcane Prison (walk out once the runes land) and the Sentinel's Collapsing Bridge (the other half of the checkerboard) all leave a way out, so they stay as they are.
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/core/resurrection/Empowered.cs`, `EmpoweredPhases.cs`, `EmpoweredSignatures.cs`, `source/Tests/EmpoweredPhaseTests.cs`, `website/wiki/necromantic-relic.md`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server only (rolling; nobody is disconnected). Nothing in Redis, no XML or client change.
- **Not done or known to be off:** not fought by real players; the other Empowered bosses' own (Elder) attacks and speed are unchanged.

### Coral Medallion drop halved

- Drop chance 0.01 to 0.005 in `source/WorldServer/logic/doomed/DoomedExtraLoot.cs`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`. Redeploy: the world server only (rolling; nobody is disconnected). Not built or run here.

### Necromantic Harvest: relics twice as often until 1 December

- **What changed:** a new effect on the timed events (`serverSettings.events`, as Halloween Sweet Treats and The Voice in the Void): `relicChance` multiplies the Necromantic Relic's chance (`resurrection.json`'s 5%) in `BossResurrection.AddRelicDrop`, capped at a sure drop; the 2% damage threshold stays and the loot boosts apply as before. The event "Necromantic Harvest" in `wServer.json` runs from 2026-11-13 00:00 to 2026-12-01 00:00 UTC with `relicChance` 2, so it is on from the deploy and off at midnight UTC going into 1 December with no further deploy (the VPS clock is UTC). `ServerEvent.Effects` describes it, so the Nexus login line, `/boosts` and `EventsTick`'s end announcement name it. The wiki's Necromantic Relic page gets a note from `{{gen:relic-event}}` (`gen_relic_event`, from `wiki_data.server_events()`), shown while such an event is running or to come when the site is built.
- **Files:** `source/Shared/resources/ServerEvents.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/resurrection/BossResurrection.cs`, `source/WorldServer/wServer.json`, `source/Tests/ServerEventsTests.cs`, `tools/build_wiki.py`, `tools/wiki_data.py`, `website/wiki/necromantic-relic.md`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the website. Nothing in Redis, no client change.
- **Not done or known to be off:** no start announcement on the deploy itself (an event already on when a world server starts isn't announced, by design: players see it in the Nexus and `/boosts`). The wiki note stays on the page after 1 December until the next deploy rebuilds the site. The Testing server on dodmg.com runs the same `wServer.json`, so the event is on there too.
