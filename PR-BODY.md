Release 2.6.9: death replays line up, and super elixirs that last. Deploys the world server, client and website; nobody is disconnected.

## For players

### Death replays
- **Hits land where they hit:** the shot that hurt the character now meets them on screen, the damage number and its sparks coming as it strikes, and the monsters' shots that missed fly past them as they did on the player's screen. The character no longer trails behind the shots (about a tenth to a quarter of a second): they're shown where they really were, walking smoothly along their path instead of gliding from tick to tick. Replays already saved play this way too.

### Wiki
- **Oryx's drops are on the wiki:** the Lil' Oryx, Lil' Wine Cellar Oryx and Lil' Infernal Oryx pets, the Infernal forge shards and the rest of Oryx 1's, Oryx 2's, Oryx the Infernal Mad God's and the Oryx Guardian Task Master's loot show on the Pets, gear and dungeon pages, so searching or filtering for them finds them.
- **The wiki is quicker to find your way around:** a long page opens as a list of its sections, each with its first line and what it holds ("153 entries", "61 rows", "3 tables"); open one, or all, and the wiki remembers. Tables under their own headings (each weapon kind on Gear statistics, each goal's loot, each forge list) open one at a time; a long table shows its first 8 rows with "Show all", a filter and columns that sort when clicked; a link, a search result or the browser's find opens what it points into. The page's parts are listed under it in the menu instead of beside the page, so tables and cards have the whole width (no table scrolls sideways at 1,440px but a few on Gear statistics), item cards fit three or more to a row, and screenshots stand beside the text on wide screens. At 1,440px every page is 1,000 to 3,500px tall before you open anything: Community Goals was 49,800px, Gear statistics 46,400, Pets 40,200, Dungeons 23,100.

### Items
- **Super elixirs last:** the Super Elixir of Magic (250 MP a drink, 9999 doses) used to vanish after its first drink. It now keeps its doses: each drink takes one off, the slot and the tooltip show how many are left, and it goes with its last. Dropped, traded or stored, it keeps its count.
- **The Super Elixir of Health:** a new red twin of the magic one, 250 HP a drink and the same 9999 doses. It's in the arena goal (Roar of the Crowd): its 400-fame tier now gives both elixirs, and its loot rolls it as often as the magic one (uncommon).

## Details

### Death replays line up

- **What and why:** reported from a real replay (an Elder Undead Lair death): the shots that dealt damage didn't line up with the character. The recording is the server's view: the player's place comes with their moves, a tick or so late and a ping behind, and their hits when their client reported them, while monsters' shots are recorded as the server fires them. Played as recorded, the player trailed the shots by their ping and a tick, and the client's glide added another tick, so a shot crossed the place they had been a moment before, and the numbers came at the wrong moment. Measured on the sample: the drawn player met the shots that hurt them up to half a second after the hit.
- **Client:** `app/src/replay/sync.rs` (new) works out, once per replay, how much earlier the player's recorded places are shown (the lead, 0 to 400 ms, the one that brings the shots that hurt them closest to them; 100 ms on the sample) and, for each hit from a shot, which of its shooter's shots dealt it and when it met them; the hits are then shown at that moment, in the order the server counted them (each shooter's shots reordered if matched the other way round), and the rest (unmatched shots, the ground) as much earlier as the matched ones were. Past the last recorded place the player carries on as they were moving, since the server never hears of their last moments. `app/src/replay.rs`: the player is placed along their recorded places each step (no glide), facing and walking from their path; monsters' shots fly through them (`World::untouchable`, `crates/world`), and the shot matched to a hit ends on them with sparks thrown its way; a tick from before a hit already shown doesn't put the health back up. The hit list and timeline use the times shown.
- **Redeploy:** client only. Nothing on the server or in the replay format changed, so replays already saved line up too. Nobody is disconnected.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/progression.md` (death replays: a hit lands as its shot strikes).
- **Not done:** the player's own shots are still drawn as the server heard of them (a ping late against the monsters); hits a monster deals by touching them keep the server's time.

### Oryx's drops on the wiki

- **What and why:** reported as "the wiki search is still broken": filtering the Pets page for "infernal" showed only pets that Oryx the Infernal Mad God drops among others, never his own Lil' Infernal Oryx. The filters worked; the pet wasn't on the page. The wiki reads each monster's loot from its `.Init("name", ...)` block, and Oryx 1, Oryx 2, Oryx 4 and the Task Master start theirs with a constant (`.Init(O4, ...)`, `private const string O4 = "Oryx the Mad God 4";`, in another file of the partial class), so their loot was never read, and a pet nothing gives is left off.
- **Tools:** `tools/wiki_data.py` (`_load_behaviours`) reads every behaviour's `const string` names first and takes an `.Init` that names one. The wiki gains Lil' Oryx, Lil' Wine Cellar Oryx and Lil' Infernal Oryx on the Pets page, the loot of Oryx's Chamber, the Wine Cellar and Oryx's Inferno on the Dungeons page (77 dungeons, was 75), and those drops on the gear pages and in search (1,939 entries, was 1,934). Oryx 4's old generated loot list, which the hand-written one replaces in the server, no longer shows on the gear pages (a rainbow bow at 10%, a platinum sword at 50%, a platinum wand at 20%: he doesn't drop them).
- **Redeploy:** website only. Nobody is disconnected.

### The wiki: sections that fold, tables that open one at a time, the whole width

- **What and why:** the owner's reports: the Pets page's size table squeezed into a narrow column and scrolled sideways, "and others like it"; then that pages were still long, narrow and full of empty space, asking for tables "into drop downs, so you can expose what you want to look at" and the best wiki they could have. Every table was capped at 780px, the width of running text; "On this page" took a column beside the page; the long pages ran to 20,000-50,000px.
- **Folding (`website/wiki/wiki.js`):** each ## section becomes a `<details class="fold">` panel: its heading, chips for what it holds (entries, rows, tables, topics) and, closed, its first sentence or two. A page taller than 2.2 windows starts with every section closed; a short page starts open. What the reader opens or closes is remembered per page (`localStorage` `dd-wiki-folds`), with Open all / Close all over the sections. Three or more ###/#### headings over tables or card lists become `details.acc` groups that open one at a time (and again a level down: Gear statistics' Weapons, then each weapon kind). A table of 12 or more rows gets a filter; outside a group, one of more than 12 rows shows 8 with "Show all N rows"; any of 6 or more rows sorts by a column when its head is clicked (numbers as numbers; a third click puts it back). A link, a search result or the menu's part list opens the sections it points into (`hashchange`, and a click on the same `#`); a filter over cards opens the sections holding matches and hides those with none, then puts them back as they were when cleared; a filter box left at the end of another section moves out between the sections (Dungeons'). Gear pages fold each group of items, class pages each group in a tab. Printing opens everything. Without the script the pages are as before, all open.
- **Builder (`tools/build_wiki.py`):** the "In short" box is in the page's head, beside its screenshot; "On this page" is a list under the page's own entry in the sidebar (`ul.nav-toc`, marked as it's read) instead of a column beside the page. `tools/wiki_community.py`: a goal's rewards are a tier to a line (`dl.g-tiers`).
- **Styles (`website/style.css`):** the section panels (an open one's heading stays at the top while it's read), groups, table tools and sortable heads; a table is as wide as it needs, up to the whole column; text at most 820px; the wiki up to 2,000px wide with smaller gutters and gaps; item icons in tables 36px; item cards with their sprite beside their name and the rest across the card, from 310px wide; screenshots float beside the text where a section is 1,150px wide or more; the wiki home's six tiles in a row on a wide screen.
- **Measured** (every wiki page and two gear pages in the browser at 390, 1,024, 1,440 and 1,920px): nothing is wider than the screen, no filter box is hidden. Tables scrolling sideways at 1,440px: 35 before, 7 after the width change, all on Gear statistics.
- **Redeploy:** website only. Nobody is disconnected.

### Super elixirs keep their doses; the Super Elixir of Health

- **What and why:** the owner's report, "fix the super elixirs, both the health and mana ones". The only one in the game, `Super Elixir` (shown as Super Elixir of Magic), had `<Doses>9999</Doses>` but no `<SuccessorId>`, and the server's doses are only ever a chain of items (Elixir of Health 7 becomes 6, and so on): used, its slot became the (missing) next item, so it went after one drink. There was no health one.
- **Server:** `Shared/database/character/inventory/ItemData.cs` gains `Doses` (left out while full) and `DosesLeft` / `WithDoses`. `Player.UseItem.cs`: a consumable with more than one dose and no successor stays in its slot and has its data's `Doses` counted down, its slot sent again (the client empties it when it asks), and goes with its last dose, its data cleared. Items with a successor are as before. `ImprintHandlers.cs` keeps `Doses` when it rebuilds an item's data. The data moves with the item as imprints do (swaps, drops, trades).
- **Content:** `Super Elixir of Health` (0xD13E, drLofiObj2 0xe8, the red vial beside the magic one's) in both copies of `custom/DoomedRealms.xml`; `resources/data/community.json`: the arena goal's 400-fame tier gives it too, and its loot has it at weight 7, like the magic one.
- **Client:** `app/src/hud.rs` reads the doses left from the slot's data (`slot_doses`) for the number on the slot and the tooltip's Doses line; `app/src/game.rs` no longer empties the slot while it's drunk unless it's the last dose.
- **Redeploy:** world server and client; the content needs both (the new item's XML). Older clients work: the server sends the slot again, though they show 9999 until updated. Nobody is disconnected.
- **Redis:** nothing to migrate. A Super Elixir already in a character's inventory has no count yet, so it starts full.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/items.md` (Potions: the super elixirs); the generated Community Goals page and search pick up the health one by themselves.
