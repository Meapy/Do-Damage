Release 2.5.13

## For players

- **Classes:** the Ascendant's halos raise a forcefield instead of healing. You and every ally standing in it take less from each hit while it lasts (shots, damaging ground, everything): 2% with the Worn Halo, 1% more each tier, up to 10% with the T8 Halo of the Archangel. Each field is 1.5 tiles smaller than the old healing circle (4.5 tiles at T0-T1, up to 6.5 from T4) and lasts as long as the halo's heal did (8 to 24 seconds). It looks the part: the halo falls from the sky in a column of light, shockwaves race out, a shimmering hex-lattice dome opens with turning rune rings and a wall of light rising off its rim, it flares round anyone it shields from a blow, and it shatters as it fades. The Ascendant still earns assist loot for every player it covers. The class and halo descriptions are rewritten to match. All nine halos are redrawn (the top three no longer share one sprite), and each tier's forcefield takes its halo's colours and a touch of its own: Worn iron that stutters with holes in its lattice, Rusty flaking rust, Bronze with verdigris, polished Silver, Golden, Eternal Peace's jade with drifting petals, God's Blessing's turning shafts of light, the Seraphim's rim of flame and rising embers, and the Archangel's violet dome with a third rune ring and stars wheeling round it.
- **Classes:** the Father swings **censers** instead of tomes: a new line of nine, the Tin Censer (T0), Iron, Pewter, Silver and Cathedral Censers, the Censer of the Bulwark, of the Unbroken Vigil, of the Iron Choir and of the Last Rite (T8), dropping with the other T0-T8 abilities and forged up the tiers like any of them. A censer lays a grey **ward** over the Father and every ally within 4.5-6.5 tiles: a shield of 50 (T0) to 170 (T8) points, more with Wisdom (about 270 at T8 with maxed Wisdom), that takes the damage of every hit before health does, for 4 to 8 seconds or until it breaks. It shows as a grey layer over the HP bar (and "+N" beside the bar's label) and as a shell of steel plates round each warded player, losing a plate for every twelfth spent; it's raised with incense swirling round the Father and rolling out to its edge over a turning sigil of shield plates, plates flying in round each ally and locking shut, sparks off it as it's struck, and a shatter when it breaks. A blow it takes whole shows as a grey number. The Censer of the Last Rite's ward bursts when it breaks, for 400 to every enemy within 3 tiles. A censer can be swung every 4 seconds; damage over time goes through the ward. The Father's tome, if he had one equipped, moves to his inventory. The Ascendant's forcefield has a ring of hex cells at its heart instead of a six-pointed star.
- **Dungeons:** the **Elder Cemetery**. The Ghost of Skuld leaves its portal half the time, and the Elder Cemetery Key (from its three bosses, and 300 in the key shop) opens one anywhere: the Haunted Cemetery's four worlds with every monster in its Elder version. Each boss has a phase of its own: the Ghost Bride's **Last Waltz** below 30% (her ghosts at her side, bolts wheeling round her, a veil of light between dances), the Grave Caretaker's **Last Burial** below 40% (armoured, six blue flames thrown round him, two spirals), and Skuld's **Requiem** at 10% (he doesn't die there: from the centre of his crypt he sings, with flaming skulls, rising zombies and turning crosses, until he's beaten). Each drops a Potion of Max (Speed, Wisdom, Vitality), T13/T14 gear and the key; Skuld drops the Cemetery's items at twice the chance. All three can be raised with a Necromantic Relic (25 bosses now), each with two signature phases (the Bride's Bridal Procession and The Last Waltz, the Caretaker's Open Graves and Lantern Sweep, Skuld's Danse Macabre and Plague Requiem) and an item at 6%, one for each class that had none:
  - **Halo of the Ghost Bride** (Ascendant): a 12% forcefield for 26 seconds in rose-white with drifting petals, and its **Eternal Vow**: a hit that would kill a player in it leaves them at 1 health and untouchable for 1.5 seconds instead, once every 45 seconds per player.
  - **The Gravekeeper's Censer** (Father): a 212-point ward for 9 seconds, and **Grave Flames**: three blue flames circle the Father and fly, one every 0.4 seconds, at the nearest enemy within 5 tiles, each bursting for 300 past defense.
  - **Pestilence of Skuld** (Assassin): the strongest poison, 585 on impact and 2,604 over 6 seconds in a 7.5-tile cloud, and its **Contagion**: an enemy that dies of it bursts into a new cloud that poisons those nearby at half strength, up to three times a throw.
- **Website:** the wiki no longer labels classes, items and pets "From Doomed Realms": its class page groups them as "The newer classes", the dungeons page as "Minigames and more dungeons", and the home page's tile is "More to play". The Party Balloons' description welcomes players to Do Damage instead of another server.
- **Game:** dying no longer throws you straight out. Once "You died" has been told, you **rise as a ghost** from your grave, pale, see-through and glowing faintly blue, with soul motes drifting up round you, and the world turns cold and grey. For up to 10 minutes you can drift anywhere with the movement keys (through walls, over water and lava), **follow a player** still in sight (the banner's arrows step through them, guildmates first, and your ghost glides after them), chat, whisper, use guild and party chat (your words show pale blue) and ping. Everyone sees your ghost. Nothing can hurt it, enemies ignore it, and it can't shoot, use items, loot, trade, teleport or take portals. **Continue** on the banner (or the Nexus key) fades into the usual fame summary; the ghost also moves on when its time is up or its world closes. Options, Gameplay, "Stay as a ghost when you die" turns it off.

## Details

### Lingering at the grave

- **What changed (servers):** new `Player.Grave.cs`: a client that sends 168 `GRAVE` 255 (once in each world, `GraveHandler.cs`) is kept as a ghost at death. `Player.Death` records the death as before (save, `Database.Death`, gravestone, announcement), then `TryLinger` sends `DEATH` and 169 `GRAVE_INFO` (`GraveInfo.cs`, the ms allowed: `GhostMs`, 10 minutes), clears `Dead` so the world keeps the player, sets `Ghost`, removes conditions and any ward, and sends stat 220 `Ghost` to everyone. `HandleIO` drops a ghost's messages except moves, chat, pings and the acks (`GhostMessages`); `PlayerTextHandler` allows only `GhostCommands` (tell, party, guild, who, online, ignore, unignore, position, servers, help, uptime; `CommandManager.NameOf`) and keeps a ghost's words from monsters' chat triggers; `ChatManager.Say` colours them pale blue (not for admins). The Move handler skips projectile checks and passability for a ghost. `Player.Tick` runs only `GhostTick` (its time). A ghost is `IsInvulnerable`, never `IsVisibleToEnemy` (Empowered bosses included), takes no conditions (`CanApplyCondition`), and is left out of `DamageCounter.GetPlayerData` and `GetLootCredit` (so no loot, XP or quest credit), `BuffedBy`, `Shield`, `GiveWard`, the Scrying Glass's picks, trades and teleports. `Client.Reconnect` disconnects a ghost instead (`LeaveAsGhost`): a world closing, a hand-over, a summon. Its time running out also disconnects it. The client closing the connection (Continue) saves the dead character exactly as a death's disconnect always did (checked: `alive.<id>` stays empty, `dead` stays set).
- **What changed (Rust client):** `bsk-proto` `Grave` and `GraveInfo`, `stat::GHOST`. `game.rs`: after a `Death` it keeps reading (a closing connection waits for the death to play out), a `GraveInfo` makes a `Grave`, and at 3 s (`GRAVE_RISE_MS`, sooner on a click) the death gives way to the ghost; `haunting()` blocks shots, abilities, items, potions, portals, trades, teleports and buying; `escape()` (the Nexus key and button) and the banner's Continue fade out for 650 ms and end the game, which goes on to the fame summary as before; `followable` and `ghost_follow` pick the player followed. `bsk-world`: `World::ghost` and `ghost_to`, `movement.rs` `drift` (one speed, `GHOST_SPEED` 0.0088 tiles/ms, through anything inside the map, gliding after `ghost_to` and settling 1.2 tiles behind), no ground damage, shots pass through ghosts. `bsk-render`: `ScreenFilter::Spirit` (post.wgsl mode 4, moonlit desaturation). `view.rs`: ghosts drawn see-through with a cool tint, a pale glow outline, a flicker and a slow bob, with no shadow or health bar. New `grave.rs`: the red of the death lifting, a cold vignette, a ring and soul motes round the ghost, the banner (how you died, the time left, ◀ follow ▶, Continue). Options, Gameplay: "Stay as a ghost when you die" (`lingerAtGrave`, on by default). Five new lines in all 11 languages.
- **Compatibility:** the AIR client, older Rust clients and `bsk-bot` never send 168, so they're disconnected at death as before (checked with the bot). Older clients ignore stat 220, so they draw another player's ghost as a normal player. A new client on an older server never gets `GRAVE_INFO` and plays the death as before.
- **Deploys:** client and servers together (new messages 168 and 169, stat 220). Website for the wiki (`getting-started.md`, "When you die"). Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes.
- **Not done:** older clients don't see ghosts as ghosts. A ghost's pet stays with it (and its shots' hits are dropped by the server). A ghost counts toward a realm's player count for event scaling while it stays.

### Ascendant halos: forcefields instead of healing

- **What changed:** the halo ability's `Halo` activate is now `Forcefield` (`ActivateEffects.Forcefield`): `range` the radius, `amount` the percent off each hit, `duration` its time. `Forcefield.cs` (was `Halo.cs`) shields every player in it each tick (`Player.Shield`, held 250 ms so the edge doesn't flicker); `Player.ShieldedDamage` takes the percent off each hit after defense, rounded to the nearest point, in `PlayerHit`, `Player.Damage` and both ground-damage paths. Overlapping fields don't add up: the strongest counts. The caster gets assist credit (`BuffedBy`) for each player in it, a second at a time. Its server-side cooldown (4 s) is kept.
- **Numbers:** T0 Worn 2% / 4.5 tiles, T1 Rusty 3% / 4.5, T2 Bronze 4% / 5, T3 Silver 5% / 5.5, T4 Golden 6% / 6.5, T5 Eternal Peace 7% / 6.5, T6 God's Blessing 8% / 6.5, T7 Seraphim 9% / 6.5, T8 Archangel 10% / 6.5. Durations and mana unchanged.
- **Look (Rust client, `particles/relic.rs`):** `ShowEffect` 41 looks 30 (cast), 31 (a player in it hit, at most every 250 ms each) and 32 (already up when a player comes into view, sent then). The percent rides in `ObjectType`, which the client uses to take the same cut off the damage numbers it shows for your own hits (`bsk_world::combat::shielded`, tested against the server's rounding). New decals `Forcefield` and `FieldFlare`, an aura for the rising wall, a shatter when the aura ends. Older clients don't know these looks; the server also sends a golden Trap ring at its edge every 1.5 s for them, which the new client leaves out.
- **Data:** `tools/port_doomed.py` (`FORCEFIELDS`, halo and class `DESCRIPTIONS`) converts the T0-T6 halos; both `DoomedRealms.xml` copies were changed by hand to what it writes, because the port currently stops early on an unrelated world (`ElderPirateCave` missing from `port_doomed_worlds`). The T7 and T8 halos are hand-written in both `CustomItems.xml` copies. `tools/rework_buff_auras.py` no longer floors the halos' range (re-running it changes nothing).
- **Wiki:** `tools/wiki_gear.py` describes the forcefield; `website/wiki/classes.md` mentions shielding for assist loot.
- **Deploys:** client, servers and website. Ship client and server together (new looks and the `Forcefield` activate name): an old client still plays, but sees only the gold rings and its tooltip doesn't describe the halo. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis data changes.
- **Not done:** the halos keep their `spell/major_heal` sound.
