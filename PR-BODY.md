Release 2.6.8: the Druid reworked: pearls thrown into tide pools that pulse, then called home through the enemies, with new sprites, a new look and five Armoire skins; the wiki remade, easier to use, with screenshots; and the Void finished: less health, better loot, the Omnipotence Ring, and a raised Void Entity that widens its platform; a Pet tab on the character card. Deploys the world servers and the client together (the pearls need both), and the website; nobody is disconnected.

## For players

### Druid
- **Pearls are thrown where you aim** (up to 8 tiles). Where one lands it splashes the enemies within 2 tiles (120 at T0 up to 380 at T8), then lies in a **tide pool** (reaching 2.5 tiles round it at T0 up to 3.5 at T8, for 3 to 5 seconds). Every half second the pool hurts the enemies in it straight through their defense (30 up to 120) and slows them, and mends the allies standing in it (5 up to 20 health; turrets too).
- **The pearl comes home:** when its pool's time is up it rushes back to you wherever you are, striking every enemy within about a tile of its way (180 up to 600), and heals you when it's back (50 up to 250), a fifth more for each enemy it hit, up to double. A faint lane on the ground from the pool to you shows where it will come, blinking in its last second.
- **Juggle the tide:** one pearl out at once up to T2, two from T3, three from T6; throwing one more calls the oldest home at once. Mana costs are as before (50 to 95).
- **New looks:** all nine pearls are redrawn (a wisp of magic, lightning, a vine, a halo, a ring of water, shadow, blood, a golden setting, chaos), and the throw, the pool, the recall and the homecoming each have their own effects.
- **Nerissa's Tidepearl** is a Druid's pearl too, above the T8 (420 splash, 130 a pulse, 650 on the way home, a 260 heal, three out at once), and its three waves of the tide now roll out from where it lands.
- **A new look and five skins:** the Druid is redrawn (an antlered hood, a sashed cloak, a pearl wand). The Armoire sells it five skins, each its own figure with something quiet round it: the **Deepwater Oracle** (coral crown, driftwood staff; bubbles rise round them and a ripple spreads at their feet), the **Autumn Warden** (antlers in red leaves, fur mantle, golden sickle; a leaf now and then falls from the antlers), the **Moonbloom Druid** (white hair, midnight robe, moonflower staff; a pool of moonlight and drifting motes), the **Mosshide Hermit** (toadstool cap, moss beard, lantern; three fireflies) at 200 gold, and the 16 by 16 **Archdruid** behind a stag's skull (eyes glowing in it, leaves circling, grass springing up in its footsteps) at 800. Dyes work on the Druid and on every skin.

### Pets
- **Your pet on your character card:** a third tab, **Pet**, beside Stats and Records, shows your pet out wherever you are: its look, name, size and level, a thin bar with the food to its next level (green once it's at its last), and what it does now: damage a second, the HP and MP it heals and how often (shortened to 67/1.5s on a short card), and the food to its next level in numbers. With no pet out it says so. It's the same as the Pen Keeper's window tells you, kept up to date every 10 seconds while the tab is open.

### Website
- **The wiki is remade to be easier to use.** Its home page starts with a big search box (with searches to try), six "Where do you want to start?" tiles (I'm new, I want to get stronger, I want loot, a better item, to pick a class, I have a few minutes), "the game in one minute" and a card with a picture for every page.
- **Every guide starts with an "In short" box:** the few things most players come for, before the details. Each has a plain one-line description under its title, and most have an in-game screenshot beside it (the Forge, the Enchanter, the Marketplace, the Pen Keeper, the quest log, the Realm Pass, community goals, potion storage, the main menu, the controls, a realm with its events); click one to see it full size.
- **Easier to get around:** the sidebar has five sections (Start here, Getting stronger, Dungeons and bosses, Classes, gear and pets, Things to do) that fold, with the page you're on open. Getting started puts "Your first hour" up top and splits Options into a line per tab.
- **Search understands questions:** "how do I get stat potions" finds stat potions (it skips filler words and matches plurals).
- **The search boxes on long lists work again:** the filter on the Pets, Dungeons, gear, class and Necromantic Relic pages hid nothing you could see; now it hides every card that doesn't match.

### The Void
- **Less health:** the Void Entity has 300,000 health (was 400,000), the Elder Void Entity 500,000 (was 800,000, nearly three times the Elder Marble Colossus) and gains 15% for each extra player, as the other Elder bosses do (was 10% of the higher figure). Raised with a Necromantic Relic it has about 670,000 (was 1,000,000).
- **Better loot:** the Bow of the Void, the Quiver of the Shadows, the Armor of Nil and the Sourcestone drop at 0.6% each (Elder 1.2%, raised 2.4%; were 0.4%, 0.8% and 1.6%), The Hollow Sourcestone at 1.5% (raised 3%), the Elder Potion of Max Life at 5% (was 2.5%).
- **The Omnipotence Ring** (+125 health and mana, +8 attack, defense and speed, +6 dexterity, vitality and wisdom) drops from the Void Entity at 0.3%, the Elder one at 0.6% and the raised one at 1.2%. **Judgement:** every 10 seconds, while an enemy is within 8 tiles, light out of the sky strikes the three nearest for 450 each, ignoring defense, and its wearer is Damaging and Berserk for 3 seconds. **Undying:** once every 2 minutes, a hit that would kill its wearer leaves them at 1 health, Invulnerable for 2 seconds; then a third of their health comes back and Judgement strikes at once.
- **The raised Void Entity widens the Void:** as it rises a black hole opens under it and pulls the void in, the new rim is traced in violet, the platform surfaces outwards ring by ring from its old edge (16 tiles) to 23 while the evil, the deep and the depths roll back before it, violet dust bursting along the front and streams of the void pouring out of the Entity; lightning marks the four new spots for its projections (25.5 tiles out), and a shockwave rolls out to the new rim. It goes back to the platform's middle for its splits, wherever its remains lay.
- **Among the stars:** both Voids show the starry void sky of the Black Between round the platform, and the whole platform is in view (28 tiles) from anywhere on it.

## Details

### Druid: tide pearls

- **What and why:** asked to rework and finish the Druid. Its pearls only spawned an unseen substitute that fired one volley and healed the Druid: nothing to aim, nothing to play around. Now a pearl is a thrown pool you place, and its recall is a skillshot: the Druid positions so the enemies stand between them and their pools, and can throw again to call one home early.
- **World server:** `core/objects/TidePearl.cs` (an invisible anchor, 0x0711, as the forcefield's: its looks, pulses every 500 ms, recall at its end, gone with its Druid), `core/objects/player/Player.TidePearls.cs` (`AETidePearl`: the throw, landing after 380 ms, the oldest recalled past `pearls`; `PearlPulse`; `RecallPearl`: enemies within 1.1 tiles of the way, `TidePearl.OnWay`, struck as it passes, then `PearlHome`'s heal, +20% per enemy up to double), `Player.UseItem.cs` (refuses a pearl thrown onto a wall before the mana is spent), `Player.CruciblePowers.cs` (`NerissaTide`, from where it lands), `ActivateEffects.cs` (`TidePearl` in place of `PearlAbility`), `ActivateEffect.cs` (`pulseDamage`, `pulseHeal`, `pearls`). `AEPearlAbility`, Substitute1-9's behaviours and the Tidal Substitute's are gone.
- **Content:** the nine pearls in both `DoomedRealms.xml` copies (new activate, descriptions, no projectiles, the `druidPearls16x16` sprites; Substitute1-9 removed), the Druid's description; Nerissa's Tidepearl in `tools/build_crucible.py` (rerun: `CrucibleObjects.xml`, both copies; the Tidal Substitute gone, its type code kept in `tools/crucible/types.json`). Sprites: `tools/draw_pearls.py` writes `DruidPearls16x16.png`, registered in `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py` (rerun: `client-rs/assets/manifest.json`).
- **Client:** `particles/tide.rs` (relic looks 61-65: the throw, the splash and pool, one already down, the recall, the homecoming; the pool drawn with the ground marks: water, the tide's arms, a ripple each pulse, the hovering pearl, the lane home, our own's time left), wired in `particles.rs` and `particles/relic.rs` (`Pen::arms` shared); the server's fallback rings round a pool are left out. `hud.rs`: the pearl's tooltip and its one-line summary. 13 new lines in every `app/lang/*.json`, "Summons a {object}" removed.
- **Redeploy:** world servers and client. An older client keeps its own copy of the item XML, so until it updates it shows the old tooltips and sprites and sees only a ring at each pool's edge; the pearls still work for it, the server doing it all. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: nothing stored changes (the pearls keep their type codes).
- **Wiki:** `tools/wiki_gear.py` (the pearls' "On use" line), `website/wiki/classes.md` (the Druid's pearls), `crucible.md` (Nerissa's Tidepearl), `gear-statistics.md` (a note that the Druid's measured row is out of date).
- **Not done:** the numbers are first guesses for playtesting. The recall and homecoming were seen only through the tests and code: the scripted ability key fires in fewer than half the test client's runs. The hovering pearl in a pool is drawn as a shaded orb in its tier's colours, not its sprite.

- **What and why:** asked to redraw the Druid and add skins, a 16 by 16 among them, each its own figure (not a recolour), restrained, and working with dyes.

### The Void finished

- **What and why:** asked to adjust the Elder Void Entity's health, make the raised one widen its arena with an animation and particles, add the Omnipotence Ring with an impressive passive, and finish the Void so it's worth doing. The Elder Entity had 800,000 health (twice the regular 400,000, itself nearly three times the Colossus's 150,000) for loot at Malus' level.
- **Health:** both `lostHallsObjects` copies (`LH Void Entity` 300,000), `tools/make_elder.py` `ELDER_HP_FIXED` (`Elder LH Void Entity` 500,000; rerun: both `ElderObjects.xml`, only that line changed), both `Resurrection.xml` (the Empowered one, by hand as before), `BehaviorDb.Void.cs` `ScaleHP2(elder ? 15 : 20)`.
- **The widening platform:** `core/resurrection/VoidArena.cs` (new), called from `EmpoweredGround.Widen` as `ColossusArena` is. It finds the platform's middle from its tiles, moves each square's ground one or more bands nearer (platform to 23.5 tiles, Pure Evil to 26.5, Deep to 28.5, Depths to 30.5, the abyss to 31.6; the invisible landmarks count as Pure Evil), timed by distance over 2.3 s from 0.7 s, in 80 ms buckets that send the tiles to everyone. Effects: the Bow of the Event Horizon's black hole (`Relic` look 28) under the Entity for 3.3 s, `Concentrate`, rim `GroundMark`s, `Diffuse` bursts and `Stream`s along the front, dark ripples on the sea's new edge, `Lightning` and marks at the four new spots, then `AreaBlast`, `BlastWave` to the rim, rings and two shakes. `Entity.SetSpawnPoint` puts the Entity's spawn at the middle (its remains may lie 7 tiles off), and `TossObject`'s new `rangeFor` throws the projections `VoidArena.ProjectionRange` out: 25.5 where the platform has widened (a `ConditionalWeakTable` of worlds), 18.5 elsewhere.
- **The Omnipotence Ring** (0x0251, already in the game's data, dropped by nothing): `<RelicPower>Omnipotence</RelicPower>` and two `EffectInfo`s in both `Equip` copies; `Player.RelicPowers.cs` `JudgementTick` (from `RelicTick`; the `SkyBolt` look 13 in gold and lightning from its wearer to each target, 120 ms apart) and `VowedDamage` (after the Ghost Bride's vow, which keeps its order: Undying with the Ascendant's forcefield look 30, gold, for 2 s; the heal 150 ms later). Loot in `BehaviorDb.Void.cs` and the Empowered roll in `resurrection.json`; `collection.json` by hand (the ring in `all`, the Void, the Elder Void and the Realm, where `build_collection.py` puts it: a full run rewrites 50 unrelated lines, it has drifted).
- **The worlds:** `Dungeons.xml` (The Void) and `DoomedRealmsWorlds.xml` (The Elder Void): `<Background>8</Background>` and `<MinSightRadius>28</MinSightRadius>`.
- **Redeploy:** world servers and client (the ring's tooltip and the health shown are in the client's XML; an older client shows the ring without its powers, which still work). Nobody is disconnected. Redis: nothing stored changes.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/dungeons.md` (the Void's loot and ring, the raised Entity's platform); the ring's page and the drop tables come from the data.
- **Not done:** no music of its own for the Void. The numbers are first guesses for playtesting.
