Release 2.5.5: Empowered bosses fight in phases, and a slider for other players' shots in a tidier Visuals tab (deploys the client and the world server; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Dungeons:** Empowered bosses (raised with a Necromantic Relic) fight in phases. As their health falls they enter new ones, each named over the boss ("Phase II: Soul Harvest") with a moment's shield: **Soul Harvest** below 80% (a mark under every player that strikes and slows when it fills, bolts flying out of it), **Soul Storm** below 60%, **Death Spiral** below 30% (counter-turning soul shards with rings of bolts) and **Last Breath** below 10% (a three-second shield, three rings of marks landing outward and rings of heavy soul orbs, then orbs and shards every few seconds to the end). The bosses of the raid-like dungeons and the Trench have two phases of their own at 70% and 40%, 30 in all, with their own shots: the Forgotten Sentinel (Collapsing Bridge, Sentinel's Gaze), the Twilight Archmage (Twilight Nexus, Arcane Prison), the Forgotten King (Royal Decree, The Last Court), the Marble Colossus (Earthsplitter, Colossal Wheel), Malus (Cult Sigils, Blood Moon Ritual: kill his three cultist souls before the chant ends or each heals him; kill them all and his armour breaks), the Void Entity (Event Horizon, Singularity), Bes (Shield Wall, Wrath of Bes), Nut (Starfall, Celestial Orbit), Geb (Fissures, Sun Disk), Limoz (Spore Mines, Thornburst), Nikao (Tidal Surge, Frozen Deep), Pyyr (Meteor Shower, Dragon's Breath), Feargus (Petrifying Roar, Obsidian Wings), the Ivory Wyvern (Prismatic Breath, Wyrm's Judgement) and Thessal (Riptide, Drowning Depths). The wiki's Necromantic Relic page describes every phase.
- **Game:** Options, Visuals is in three parts under headings: **World** (Pets' shots, particles, shadows, health bars, damage numbers, crosshair), **Other players** (sliders for players, **shots and spells**, pets and pings; switches for their pets' effects and their damage numbers, moved from Gameplay) and **Chat** (size, background, speech balloons). Other players' shots are a slider now, 100% down to Hidden in steps of 5%, instead of two switches (their shots and spells, their pets' shots): it fades their projectiles, their pets' shots, the sparks and trails of both, their spells and their Necromantic items' powers (the black hole, the pentagram, auras, decals and the rest, even when they show on a monster), and leaves your own as they are. A setting of "off" carries over as Hidden.

## Details

### Empowered boss phases

An Empowered boss had the same soul attacks from start to end (Soul Nova, Wailing Souls, the Soul Storm below 60%, the rage below 30%). It now opens phases as its health falls (`SoulPhase`: a name, a threshold, a line, a period, how long its attack plays), announced with a notification over the boss, a blast wave, the boss's line in chat, an info line to everyone in the world, and a 1.2 s shield. Only one phase attack plays at a time (its `Busy` time, then 1.2 s), the ready one with the lowest threshold first, so a late fight stays readable; each phase's clock runs at the boss's haste (1.25, 1.45 enraged). The Soul Nova and the Wailing Souls run as before, alongside.

- **Every Empowered boss** (`EmpoweredPhases.cs`): Soul Harvest (80%, every 9 s), Soul Storm (60%, the old storm made a phase, every 10 s), Death Spiral (30%, every 11 s) and Last Breath (10%: once its burst, then every 6.5 s).
- **The raid bosses and the Trench** (`EmpoweredSignatures.cs`): two phases each at 70% and 40%, 30 in all, as listed above. Malus' Blood Moon Ritual summons three Wailing Souls (2% of his health each) while he's shielded for 6.5 s; each one left heals him 2.5%, none left gives him Armor Broken for 5 s.
- **How they're made:** the Black Between's `Strike` helpers (ground marks that fill before they land, `Strike.Land` for the hit, `FireFrom` for shots from any spot: pillars, walls of tridents, whirlpools), wrapped in an `Arena` (targets, positions, delayed steps that stop if the boss dies or leaves). Damage is the boss's soul bolt (`soulDamage` in `resurrection.json`) times a factor, through the boss's `DamageScale`, as its other shots.
- **New shots** on every Empowered form: 92 a heavy, slow soul orb and 93 a fast soul shard; on the 15, 100-103 their signature shots (`SIGNATURE_SHOTS` in `tools/build_resurrection.py`: each boss's own projectile art, such as Thessal's Trident and Thunder Swirl or Malus' Lightning and Fire shots, at a share of the soul bolt's damage). Only those projectiles were added to both `Resurrection.xml` copies: a full `build_resurrection.py` run also adds each Elder dungeon's key to its Empowered boss's second roll and changes older items' aura ranges (6.5 to 7), which was left as it is.
- **`/empower <boss> [health %]`** (admin, `Command.Empower.cs`): raises a boss's Empowered form where you stand, as a relic would, optionally at a share of its health (every phase above it opens at once). The raise itself moved to `BossResurrection.RaiseAt`, which the remains use too.
- **Wiki:** `wiki_data.py` reads the signature phases (name, threshold, and the comment over each) from `EmpoweredSignatures.cs`; each of the 15 bosses' cards has an "Its own phases" row, and `website/wiki/necromantic-relic.md` has a section on the phases.
- Files: `source/WorldServer/core/resurrection/` (`Empowered.cs`, `EmpoweredPhases.cs`, `EmpoweredSignatures.cs`, `BossResurrection.cs`, `BossRemains.cs`), `source/WorldServer/core/commands/admin/Command.Empower.cs`, `tools/build_resurrection.py`, both `Resurrection.xml`, `tools/wiki_data.py`, `tools/build_wiki.py`, `website/wiki/necromantic-relic.md`, `source/Tests/EmpoweredPhaseTests.cs`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, SETUP-LOCAL.md.
- **Redeploy:** world server (rolling, nobody is disconnected) and the client (its XML has the new shots; a client from before this release doesn't have shots 92-103 in its data and may not draw them as intended), and the website for the wiki. Nothing in Redis.
- **Not done or known to be off:** the phases' damage and timing are first values, tried in god mode by one admin, not by a group; tune `Every`, `Busy` and the factors after real fights. A boss's own fight can overlap its phases (for example the Forgotten King's Remembered phase at 10% with Last Breath).

### Other players' shots slider and the Visuals tab

The Visuals tab was eight switches in two columns under six sliders, mixing the world, other players and the chat. Other players' shots were a switch, which hid their projectiles and the spells shown on them but not their Necromantic powers that show on monsters (most of them: the Venom splash, Hellbrand, Ignition, Sky Bolt, the cannonball's AoE...), since a `ShowEffect` only named its target.

- **Settings** (`options.rs`): `others_shots: f32` (`otherShotsOpacity`, default 1) replaces `ally_shots` and `pet_shots`; `allyShots=0` from an older file reads as 0, `allyShots=1` as 1, `petShots` is dropped (other players' pets' shots now go with their shots). Defaults on the Visuals tab also reset the shots, chat size and chat background.
- **The tab** (`draw_visuals_tab`, with `slider_row` and `switches` shared with Graphics, Sound and Gameplay): headings World, Other players, Chat. "Other players' damage numbers" moved from Gameplay to Other players ("Damage numbers"). New lines translated in all ten tables, eight old ones removed.
- **Drawing** (`view.rs`, `particles.rs`, `particles/relic.rs`, `game.rs`, `bsk-world` `Impact::others`): other players' projectiles (their pets' included) are drawn at that opacity, their hit sparks and trails too, and none at 0. Their `ShowEffect`s are made with `Particles::muting` set, which every particle (`Particle::dim`, applied when drawn), decal (`Decal::dim`) and aura (`Aura::muting`, for the particles it keeps giving off) made then carries.
- **Server** (`ShowEffect.cs`, `Player.RelicPowers.cs`): `ShowEffect` has a `SourceId`, written after `ObjectType` only when set; the Necromantic powers' effects (`RelicFx`, `RelicEffect`) set it to the player. The Rust client reads it as an optional trailing field (`source_id: Option<i32>`). Clients from before this release stop reading before it, as they ignore trailing bytes, so they're unaffected. Effects without it are judged as before (another player as the target).
- Files: `client-rs/app/src/{options,particles,game,view,main}.rs`, `client-rs/app/src/particles/relic.rs`, `client-rs/crates/proto/src/messages.rs`, `client-rs/crates/world/src/combat.rs`, `client-rs/app/lang/*.json`, `source/WorldServer/networking/packets/outgoing/ShowEffect.cs`, `source/WorldServer/core/objects/player/Player.RelicPowers.cs`, `website/wiki/getting-started.md`, `website/wiki/pets.md`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, SETUP-LOCAL.md.
- **Redeploy:** the client, the world server (rolling, nobody is disconnected) and the website for the wiki. Nothing in Redis. Either side works with the other's older version: an older server's effects simply carry no player (Necromantic powers on monsters then aren't faded).
- **Not done or known to be off:** other effects other players' abilities show on monsters (not Necromantic) still don't name their player, so they show at full strength; only the Necromantic powers were given `SourceId`. A player who had only "Other players' pets' shots" off now sees those shots until they lower the new slider.
