Release 2.5.9: Oryx 4 fights a fight of his own (new attacks, a second phase, his own minions) and is armoured instead of invulnerable, right click can be bound, and /visit, /summon and /swho reach across servers during updates (deploys the client, the world server and the website; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Dungeons:** Oryx 4 is never invulnerable once awake, in his Inferno or an arena: his shields, warnings and the arena's wake and Doom Ring make him Armored (defense doubled, 50 to 100) instead, and for about half his Inferno fight (was invulnerable four fifths): 2.5 s of warning before each attack (was 4 s), shields up half as long and down twice as long (his usual 3.5 s shut / 1 s open is now 1.75 s / 2 s), and no shield through whole parts of his attacks (Minion Toss, the Artifact Dance's last stretch, Circle Combat's and Bullet Dance's shielded turns, the last spell of Make Minions and the Hadouken now open and shut again). Oryx 1 fights as before. In his Inferno he throws and calls his own burnt **Infernal Minions** (8,000 health, was 4,000; defense 20, was 12; shots 77 and 105, were 55 and 75; the green star is a hellfire one) and **Infernal Assassins** (7,000 health, defense 18, shots 148 and 108, were 110 and 80) in place of Oryx 1's, both firing a quarter faster. And he fights a fight of his own, not just a harder Oryx 1: the Bullet Dance and the Orbitals give way to three new attacks, the **Hellfire Spiral** (four arms of fire bolts turning round his throne, then back, with slowing rings every 3 s), the **Meteor Storm** (meteors all over the Pyre every quarter second, a bigger slowing one on the nearest player, white superblasts) and the **Soul Hunt** (he names a player, chases them with fire bolts and leaves burning ground behind him, slowing rings for everyone); and at **half health he ignites** once (a triple ring of fire, he grows) and a two-armed hellfire sweep turns round him for the rest of the fight.
- **Player sprites:** new looks drawn by players in the Sprite Editor: Archdemon's Soulfire Staff (by Joseph).
- **Updates and restarts:** while an update or the scheduled restart moves players over to the new world server, `/visit` and `/summon` reach a friend on the other one too (the Yes/No box, `/yes`, `/no`, `/visit cancel` and the usual rules all work as before), and `/swho` lists everyone on both servers by world. The "A new server has come online: Europe 2" and "Server, Europe 2, is no longer online" messages each update showed are gone.
- **Controls:** the right mouse button can be bound in Options, Controls like any key (shown as "Right click"), for example to Use ability, to cast at the cursor. It acts as the key only over the world, with no window or chat open; right clicks on the HUD (pinning a player's card, Ctrl+right-click breaking an item down, marking a vault chest) work as before.

## Details

### Right click as a bindable key

- **What changed:** the right mouse button binds as the `ContextMenu` key (`options::RIGHT_CLICK`, saved as `ContextMenu` in the settings file, labelled "Right click"), as the side buttons bind as BrowserBack/Forward. `App::right_button` handles the button: while Options is waiting for a key it binds; otherwise it always queues the usual right click, and, only when something is bound to it, the game is being played, no window, menu or chat is open and the mouse is left of the HUD bar, it also presses the key. Letting go always releases it, so a held ability (the ninja stars) is thrown wherever the button comes up. Scripted `right@` mouse steps go through the same path. The rebinding prompt now reads "Press a key, right click or mouse 4/5…" (translated in all 10 tables, with the new "Right click" label).
- **Files:** `client-rs/app/src/main.rs`, `client-rs/app/src/options.rs`, `client-rs/app/lang/*.json`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/getting-started.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the client (and the website for the changelog and wiki). Nothing on the server, nothing in Redis.
- **Not done or known to be off:** not tried in game: the binding is covered by a unit test, the in-game press isn't. A keyboard's Menu key (`ContextMenu`) does the same as right click.

### Visits between world servers

- **What changed:** during a hand-over friends are split between the old and the new world server, and `/visit`, `/summon` and `/swho` only saw their own server's players ("Player not found!"). A name not found locally is now looked up in the other world servers' `playerList` (sent in every ISC ping; hidden players skipped, admin-only servers for admins only), and the request goes over Redis on a new `Visit` channel (`VisitMsg`: Ask, Asked, Refused, No, Withdraw, Accept, Go, Tell, AdminVisit) to the server the player is on. Both servers keep the request in their own `VisitBook` (new `Request.Server` / `RemoteId`; the asker's side waits 3 s longer, `Ask(graceMs)`), so cooldowns, the box, `/yes` / `/no`, `/visit cancel` and running out behave as on one server. The server whose world the player goes into checks `VisitRules` with the mover's account (`Player.VisitPlace(world, accountId)`), invites them, marks them as having been in, shields their arrival and makes a reconnect ticket (`ConnectionManager.AddTicket`); the mover's own server sends their client a `Reconnect` with that key (`Reconnect.SetKey`), `Host` "" when both servers share a public address (the world proxy routes the world id to its slot), else the other server's address (Testing). Admins go, or summon, straight away. A server without this (`ServerInfo.crossVisits` false: only the old slot on this release's own deploy) gets a clear refusal instead of a request that would run out. `/swho` merges every world server's players (the hand-over partner under the same name; other servers' lines start with their name; Testing for admins only), using the new `PlayerInfo.WorldDisplay` for world names; `/online` names each server once. `ChatManager` no longer announces the account server, admin-only servers or a world server of its own name joining or leaving the network.
- **Files:** new `source/WorldServer/core/RemoteVisits.cs`, `source/Shared/isc/data/VisitMsg.cs`, `source/Tests/CrossServerVisitTests.cs`; `source/Shared/isc/Channel.cs`, `isc/data/PlayerInfo.cs`, `ServerConfig.cs`, `VisitBook.cs`; `source/WorldServer/core/GameServer.cs`, `ConnectionManager.cs`, `miscfile/ChatManager.cs`, `objects/player/Player.Visits.cs`, `commands/player/Command.Visit.cs`, `commands/player/Command.Online.cs`, `commands/doomed/DoomedRealmsCommands.cs` (`/summon`, `/swho`), `networking/packets/outgoing/Reconnect.cs`; `tools/build_wiki.py`, `website/wiki/commands.md`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the website (changelog, wiki). No message between server and client changed, so no client change; nothing in Redis. Testing gets it with the same deploy. The account server builds against the changed `Shared` but needs nothing new.
- **Not done or known to be off:** `/help` calls still only reach players on the caller's own server. On this release's own deploy the old slot runs the previous build, so visits to its players are refused with a "try again once they've moved over" until it empties. Visits between Europe 2 and Testing were not tried (no second machine in the test); they use the other server's `serverInfo.address` and port, as Testing's `sendPlayersTo` does.

### Oryx 4 armoured instead of invulnerable

- **What changed:** players said Oryx 4 "goes invisible" (his health bar hides while he's invulnerable) far too often. Measured from his states, Oryx 1's fight as Oryx 4 ran it kept him invulnerable about 80% of the time. `OryxSpec` (the settings an Oryx fights Oryx 1's fight with) gains `ShutFor` and `OpenFor` (scales on every shield spell's shut and open times) and `Guarded` (whether the in-attack invulnerable stretches stay: CC grenade turns 0 and 3-5, BD turns 2-5, MT 1-3 and MT Watch, AD Attack 3, and the endless last "Shut Again" of Make Minions and the Hadouken, which unguarded shut for a while and open again). A fourth setting, `Shield`, is the effect all of the fight's shields, warnings and set-up moments give (`Invulnerable` by default); Oryx 4 sets it to `Armored`, so once awake he's never invulnerable. His "Awaken" (4 s as you come near) is armoured too; only "Dormant", with nobody within 15 tiles, stays invulnerable. In an arena, his Doom Ring and his wake (`ArenaWake` gains an effect parameter, invulnerable for the other Oryxes) are armoured. Oryx 4 sets `Pause = 2500` (was 4000), `ShutFor = 0.5`, `OpenFor = 2.0`, `Guarded = false`; Oryx 1 keeps the defaults, so he's unchanged. The short set-up moments (CC/BD set-up, Chase Course and White Planet's start and throw, MT 0, his last stand's start) stay shielded (armoured for Oryx 4). New helpers `O1ShieldOnce`, `O1Guard`, `O1Guarded`; `O1Shield`, `O1Chooser`, `O1Grenades` and `O1Bullets` take the spec. New test `Oryx4IsNeverInvulnerableOnceAwake` fails if any of his states but "Dormant" makes him invulnerable.
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxChamber.cs`, `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxDoomed.cs`, `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxArena.cs`, `source/Tests/BehaviorStatesTests.cs`, `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/endgame.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the website (changelog, wiki). Nothing in Redis.

### Oryx 4's own minions

- **What changed:** Oryx 4 threw and called Oryx 1's Minions and Assassins. `OryxSpec` gains `Minion` and `Assassin` (the names his fight uses everywhere, the last stand's `Order`s too); Oryx 4 sets `Infernal Minion of Oryx` (0x5998) and `Infernal Assassin of Oryx` (0x5999). They're burnt copies of Oryx 1's made by `tools/build_hash_cellar.py` (`BURNT_OBJECTS`; `BURNT_FIELDS` for health and defense, new `BURNT_DAMAGE` scaling every shot): Minion 8000 HP, def 20, shots x1.4 (77, 105), its Green Star a Hellfire Star; Assassin 7000 HP, def 18, shots x1.35 (148, 108). Their animated sprites are burnt rows: the right-facing ones after the court's four on `HashCellarChars16x16.png` (the court's rows unchanged), the Minion's down-facing one on the new `HashCellarCharsDown16x16.png`, registered in `gen_rs_manifest.RS_ONLY_CHARS`. Behaviours: Oryx 1's minions' became builders, `OryxMinion(fire)` and `OryxAssassin(fire)`; the Infernal ones fire with cooldowns x0.75. The Infernal Minion keeps the Minion's loot (T7 weapon 20%, Magic Potion 3%).
- **Files:** `tools/build_hash_cellar.py`, `tools/hash_cellar/types.json`, `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py`, regenerated `source/Shared/resources/xml/custom/HashCellarObjects.xml` and the client's copy, `client/src/kabam/rotmg/assets/custom/images/HashCellarChars16x16.png` and `HashCellarCharsDown16x16.png` (new), `client-rs/assets/manifest.json`; `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxChamber.cs`, `BehaviorDb.OryxDoomed.cs`; `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/endgame.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the client (new objects and sheet: like the Sanctum's pillar turrets, an older client knows neither minion, so it shows them and their shots poorly or not at all, and takes no hits from those shots, until it updates), the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the website. Nothing in Redis.
- **Not done or known to be off:** the Assassins' burnt sprite is dark; the numbers are a first pass, not tuned in a real group fight.
- **Not done or known to be off:** the share of time invulnerable is worked out from the state timings, not measured in a full kill; Armored doubles his defense to 100, so weak weapons do little while he's shielded (the minimum damage still lands); in an arena only his wake and Doom Ring changed, his cycle's timings didn't.

### Oryx 4's own attacks and second phase

- **What changed:** players found Oryx 4 "just Oryx 1 but harder". `OryxSpec` gains `Draws` (his own attacks, added to each of the chooser's pools), `Skips` (Oryx 1's attacks he never draws) and `Attacks` (their states, added to the fight). Oryx 4's spec moved into `O4Spec()`: he skips the Bullet Dance and the Orbitals and draws three of his own (`O4Attacks`), each with a 2.5 s warning and armoured shields: the Hellfire Spiral (15 s on his throne: four Hellfire Bolt arms every 0.2 s turning 11 degrees a volley, reversing after 7 s; a ring of 12 slowing Hellfire Stars every 3 s), the Meteor Storm (14 s: scattered meteors, `Grenade` 2 tiles/140 every 0.25 s anywhere within 13 tiles of his spawn; a 3-tile, 220, slowing one on the nearest player every 1.4 s; a Superblast every 2.6 s) and the Soul Hunt (12 s: `HuntRandomPlayer` names a player and he follows them, Fire Bolt triples at them, rotating Hellfire Star rings, and a range-0 `Grenade` every 0.45 s that lands where he was, a burning trail). His second phase: new `HpLessOnceTransition(0.5, "Ignite")` takes him, once, from anywhere in the fight into "Ignite" (4 s armoured: back to his throne, grows to 195, rings of 24 Superblasts, Hellfire Bolts and Hellfire Stars), and new `BelowHealth(0.5, ...)` keeps a two-armed Hellfire Bolt sweep turning round him through the rest of the fight. Oryx 1 is unchanged (no draws or skips).
- **Files:** `source/WorldServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxDoomed.cs`, `BehaviorDb.OryxChamber.cs`, new `source/WorldServer/logic/transitions/HpLessOnceTransition.cs` and `source/WorldServer/logic/behaviors/BelowHealth.cs`; `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/endgame.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md`.
- **Redeploy:** the world server (rolling; nobody is disconnected) and the website. It uses only projectiles Oryx 4 already has, so no client change of its own. Nothing in Redis.
- **Not done or known to be off:** numbers are a first pass, untried by a real group in the Pyre (tested in a vault); the spiral's rotation counter is shared by every Oryx 4 at once, as `Shoot`'s `rotateAngle` is everywhere (only one is ever up).
