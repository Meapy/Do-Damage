Release 2.5.0: the Black Between, restored Candyland waves and Necromantic Boss drops in the Collection; link your Discord for a Potion of Maxy, password resets and the Verified role (deploys the client, the servers, the website and the Discord bot; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Discord:** link your game account to your Discord account. On the main menu, signed in with a registered account, **Link Discord** (with a line under it reminding you, until you have) opens a screen that gives a 6-character code, good for 10 minutes; type `/link code:<code>` in the Do Damage Discord and the screen changes by itself. The first link puts a **Potion of Maxy** in your vault's gift chest (once per game account and per Discord account). In the Discord you get the **Verified** role and your nickname becomes your game name; nobody there can change their own nickname any more. Linked, the bot answers `/account` (your account, fame, living characters, guild), `/resetpassword` (a new password and the email you sign in with, shown only to you; one reset in 10 minutes) and `/unlink`; the menu's Discord screen unlinks too. Linking is never required. The wiki's FAQ has "Why link my Discord?" and "I lost my password".
- **The Black Between:** a new endgame dungeon beyond Oryx 5, for players in Enthroned gear. The Mysterious One, the voice of Realm 2, and his children Vesper, the Dusk Star, and Aurel, the Dawn Blade, fight three trials on a platform floating in the void, each with survival phases, and before each sickening one he heals you to full. Every shot of theirs (bosses, copies, echoes, spectres, Dusk Mines and the Gravity Well) goes on through whoever it hits, so a couple of players in front can't take the hits for the group: everyone has to dodge. Once the fight starts the bridge falls away into the void (it used to burn), all but the landing where players arrive: anyone on the bridge is moved onto the platform, and whoever comes late (a /help, the Rift) lands safely on the landing but can't cross until the Mysterious One falls. It has its own music and boss sounds.
- **The way in:** when Oryx the Ascended God falls, the Mysterious One's Rift opens in the Elysium a quarter of the time, and every time during the event The Voice in the Void (10 November to 1 December).
- **Loot:** life and mana potions (about Oryx 5's count), every Max potion at 2.5%, the Unstable Essence (two for everyone who finishes the fight, at least 1% of his damage, then a third at 11.25%), and six new abilities with powers of their own (white bags, 1% each): Quiver of the Solar Cyclone, Trinity Trap, Helm of the Dawn Blade, Prism of the Dusk Veil, Cloak of the Planar Rift and Vial of Cascading Dusk.
- **Corruption:** the Cosmic Anvil he leaves behind spends an Unstable Essence to change a piece of Enthroned gear, for better or worse; corrupted gear has a purple edge. What it does is left for players to find out.
- **Oryx 3 and Oryx 5:** the gates that shut behind you in Oryx's Sanctuary and Oryx's Elysium now show as they close; before, they blocked the way unseen.
- **Boss health:** a boss scaled up for a bigger group (the Oryx chain's, the Black Between's) scales back down when players leave the dungeon, keeping the fraction of health it had.
- **Dungeons:** Candyland rooms spawn waves of ten creatures. Clear the wave and leave the room for the next boss; defeated bosses no longer leave a realm portal behind.
- **Game:** imprinted gear's border is thinner; Darkness darkens your view; ground telegraphs show on raised floors (the Nexus islands) too.
- **Vault:** the Collection's Dungeons view shows the 19 unique items from Empowered Elder bosses under **Drops from Necromantic Boss**, and the Black Between's six abilities in a group of their own.

## How it works

- **Content** comes from one generator, `tools/build_black_between.py` (art in `tools/black_between/`): the sprites, the item and monster XML (server and client copies, types 0x8D00-0x8DFF), the map and `types.json`. The music and sounds come from `tools/black_between/sound.py` (MP3s committed under `web/music` and `web/sfx/monster`).
- **Shots pierce:** every enemy shot in the dungeon is `<MultiHit/>` (the generator's `foe()`; 18 shots gained it, the players' items are unchanged). The client then lets a shot that hits another player fly on to the next, and the server keeps each player's copy live (`OtherHit` and `PlayerHit` only disable a shot without MultiHit), so no server code changes. The client bundles the XML, so this needs the new client; an older one still stops the shot on the first player it reaches, for itself only.
- **The bridge into the void:** the Observatory Heart's `Fight` state now paints the causeway `Empty` (0xFF, no ground) instead of the burning `Black Between` ground, all but the pad (`CausewayPad`: from the row before the Spawn region to the causeway's end), and a new `ClearTheCauseway` first moves anyone between the platform and the pad onto the platform at the causeway's mouth (a square with no ground is solid to the client: they'd be stuck). It comes back when the Mysterious One falls, as the burning ground did. Two engine changes make a square that loses its ground show: the server now sends 0xFF for a square a player was sent ground for (`Player.AddSightTile`; never for one they weren't, so nothing else changes), and the Rust client takes 0xFF as the square going back to never sent (`World::set_tile`): the sky shows and it can't be walked on. An older client draws the `Empty` ground's black square there instead, still not walkable.
- **Unstable Essence:** the Mysterious One's 1% threshold now has two `ItemLoot("Unstable Essence", 1)` before the 11.25% roll, so everyone who finishes gets two at least. Servers only.
- **The fight** is `BehaviorDb.BlackBetween.cs`, with its behaviours in `logic/behaviors/BlackBetween.cs`. The invisible Observatory Heart at the middle runs the arena (floors, starfall, the Constellation Path). Ground telegraphs use a new `ShowEffect` 40 (`GroundMark`), drawn by the Rust client; older clients see only the hits. The world gives players a sight radius of at least 35 there (`<MinSightRadius>`).
- **The way in:** `OpenTheBetween` on Oryx the Ascended God, with an event flag `mysteriousOne` (`ServerEvents.cs`, the event in `wServer.json`).
- **The abilities'** powers are `<RelicPower>`s worked in `Player.BetweenPowers.cs`.
- **Corruption:** `CorruptHandler.cs`, `Shared/resources/Corruption.cs`, the client's anvil tab (`forge/corrupt.rs`), and `ItemData` gains `Corruption` and `Unique` (JSON, left out when empty: no migration). **New messages:** `CORRUPT` (160) and `CORRUPT_RESULT` (161), numbered clear of yours, so the client and servers ship together.
- **Fixes beside it:** `PlaceObject` now gives placed objects an id and refreshes players' squares (the Oryx 3 and 5 gates); the client draws Darkness and puts ground marks on the floor they came at.
- **Boss scaling:** `BossStats` now takes a player's share of added health off again when they leave the world (gone out, dead, disconnected), keeping the boss's health fraction; walking out of range changes nothing, so it can't be used to shrink a boss.
- **The Collection:** `tools/build_collection.py` adds the Empowered Elder bosses' unique items; `collection.json` regenerated.
- **Translations:** the anvil's lines are in every language table. The wiki shows the dungeon, the gear and a Corruption section; the changelog and SETUP-LOCAL.md are updated.
- **Deploy:** client, servers and website. Nobody is disconnected and no Redis data changes.

### Discord account linking

- Why: there was no way back into an account whose password was lost (no email is ever sent), and nothing tied a Discord member to a player.
- Account server: `Shared/database/DiscordLinks.cs` (codes, linking in one Redis transaction, the reward, unlinking, resets), `App/Controllers/DiscordController.cs` (the bot's requests: `discord/link`, `unlink`, `account`, `resetPassword`, each with the shared secret), `AccountController` `discordCode` and `discordUnlink` (the client's, with the login), `<Discord linked="..">name</Discord>` in the character list's `<Account>` (only when a secret is set, so the client knows whether to offer it), `ServerSettings.discordSecret` (or `BSK_DISCORD_SECRET`), `Database.ClearLoginFails`, `DbAccount.DiscordId`/`DiscordName`.
- Bot: `tools/discord/dodmg.py` (`link_secret`, `Accounts`, `link_reply`) and `bot.py` (`/link`, `/account`, `/unlink`, `/resetpassword` with a confirm button, all ephemeral; `--try "<command>" --as-member <id>` answers one without Discord; `--dry-run` prints sample answers). New settings, both optional: `BSK_ACCOUNT`, `BSK_LINK_SECRET`.
- Client: `Screen::Discord` (`screens.rs` `draw_discord`), the main menu's Link Discord button and reminder line (or "Discord: name, Manage" once linked), a link on the Account screen, `Request::DiscordCode`/`DiscordUnlink` and the 4-second character list poll while a code shows (`main.rs`), `login::open_link` (Open Discord: the browser), 26 new lines in all ten language tables, the test flag `--screen home|account|discord|discord-code`.
- Verified: `bot.py` `verify` gives a linked member the Verified role (`DISCORD_VERIFIED_ROLE`) and their game name as nickname on `/link`, takes both away on `/unlink`, and puts them back on `/account` (a member who left and came back). Unlinking in the game reaches the bot within a minute: the account server keeps those Discord ids in the set `discord.unlinked`, which the bot asks for every minute (`discord/unlinked`, `DiscordLinks.UnlinkedInGame`). `setup_server.py` makes the Verified role, writes its id into the bot's settings, takes **Change Nickname** from @everyone and any other role that gives it (staff, managed roles and administrators aside), and says so if the bot lacks Manage Roles or Manage Nicknames or sits below the role.
- Deploy: `deploy.py` `discord_link_secret` makes the secret from the bot's token in `deploy/discord.local.env` (HMAC-SHA256, as the bot does; `BSK_LINK_SECRET` there overrides) and writes it into the account server's staged `server.json`; `install.sh` keeps the installed one when a deploy has no bot settings. Nothing for the owner to set.
- Redis: new fields and keys only (`account.<id>` `discordId`, `discordName`, `discordReward`; `discord.accounts`; `discord.rewarded`; `discord.code.*`, `discord.codeFor.*`, `discord.tries.*`, `discord.reset.*` for 10 minutes each). Nothing to migrate.
- Redeploy: account server (restarts: sign-ins pause a few seconds), client, and the Discord bot (restarts; it registers the four new slash commands). The world server isn't touched. Nobody is disconnected. An older client with the new server just never shows the screen; the new client with an older server shows no reminder.
- For the owner, before or after the release (it changes the live Discord server, so it isn't done here): `python tools\discord\setup_server.py --env deploy\discord.local.env` shows the plan, and with `--apply --refresh-messages` it makes the **Verified** role, takes Change Nickname from @everyone, updates the FAQ message ("I lost my password", "How do I get Verified") and writes `DISCORD_VERIFIED_ROLE` into `deploy/discord.local.env`. Then copy that file's contents into the `DISCORD_ENV` repository secret (`gh secret set DISCORD_ENV < deploy\discord.local.env`), or the deploy from GitHub installs the bot's settings without the role and nobody is marked Verified. In Discord's Server Settings, Roles: the bot's role needs **Manage Roles** and **Manage Nicknames** and must sit above Verified (the script says if it doesn't). The deploy from GitHub needs the `DISCORD_ENV` secret (it has it) for the first deploy to set the link secret.
- Docs: changelog (Discord), the wiki's FAQ and Getting started, SETUP-LOCAL.md ("Discord", "Linked accounts"; "Changes from upstream"), source/AGENTS.md (Redis data), client-rs/AGENTS.md (`--screen`).
- Not done: members already in the server keep whatever nickname they have until they link; Discord lets no bot rename the server's owner or anyone ranked above the bot (they get the role, and are told); a linked member who leaves and rejoins has no role until they use `/account` (the bot has no members intent). A player online when they link sees the potion at their next visit to the vault. The real bot's slash commands weren't run against Discord (that needs the live bot's token): the same code paths were run with `--try`. In a browser, Open Discord may be stopped by the pop-up blocker; the invite is written on the screen. The staged `server.json` is readable by any user on the VPS, like the rest of `/opt/bsk/app`.

### Candyland waves and bosses

- `BehaviorDb.Candyland.cs`: each of the six map spawners now summons ten random Candy Creatures
  when a player enters, then respawns a wave after it is cleared. Its boss spawner waits until
  local Candy Creatures are gone and all players leave the room before choosing a boss; this
  repeats after each boss. Removed the Candy Gnome's portal-on-death behavior.
- Redeploy: world servers only; no client release or protocol change, no Redis migration, and nobody is disconnected.
