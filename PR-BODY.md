Release 2.6.3: skins sold at the Armoire, Scrying Glass's Ask to join and gear, a bigger Arena that keeps its bosses in the ring and never sticks on an invulnerable monster, /discord and the new Discord invite; deploys client, world servers, account server, website and the Discord bot (players are not disconnected)

## For players

- **Skins:** the **Armoire** in the Nexus (and in guild halls) sells skins now. It shows every skin of your character's class: the ones you own to put on, the rest with their price (200 gold, in red when you're short), the level a skin still needs, or "Realm Pass" for the pass's season skins. Pick one for sale and it asks first ("Buy Merlin for 200 gold?", with your gold); buying puts it straight on, and it's yours on every character of that class, as when bought on a class's page.
- **Scrying Glass:** the big view now shows the gear of the player you're watching, in the picture's corner under their level and class (hover an item for its tooltip, imprints and all). Its new **Ask to join** button sends them the `/visit` request: they get the Yes/No box, and a Yes takes you straight to them, into a realm too (which `/visit` itself doesn't reach). The reply ("Asked…", "didn't let you in", a cooldown) shows under the picture.
- **Arena:** the ring is **8 tiles wider and taller**, the extra room between its four side rooms and the middle, and monsters spawn a little further out (within 22 tiles of the middle, was 18). Once the gates shut, **bosses and their monsters stay inside the ring**: none can cross its walls, and one that gets out anyway (a teleport, a toss) is put back on the nearest floor. Before, the Ghost of Skuld's teleports took him to spots of his own crypt's map, over the walls, and anything outside the ring was quietly taken away, so a wave could end without its boss dying. Once a wave's bosses and its own monsters are dead, whatever they summoned that's still about 15 seconds later fades away ("The stragglers fade away."), so nothing a boss leaves behind holds up the next wave. Skuld's invulnerable flaming skulls no longer come as monsters of their own in the Graveyard rounds (Skuld still throws his).
- **Dungeons:** the Grave Caretaker's blue flames come after the players once he falls, instead of waiting where he left them. In the Arena, those left behind stood still out of reach and held the next wave up.
- **Items:** the **Cloak of the Planewalker**'s tooltip said its cooldown was 1.5 seconds, but since 2.6.1 it can't be used again until you've been seen for half a second after its 5.5 seconds of invisibility: 6 seconds in all. Its cooldown is now **6 seconds**, so the tooltip, the wiki and the ability's cooldown bar say so, instead of the bar filling at 1.5 s and the cloak then buzzing an error. The **Prism of the Bloody Surprise** (3 s invisible) now has a **3.5 second** cooldown for the same reason (it had none of its own, so 0.5 s).
- **Arena:** a wave no longer sticks on a monster that can't be hurt. At wave 37 (the Cultist Hideout) a Green Cnidarian, dealt in from the Cnidarian Reef, stayed invulnerable for good: the Reef's jellyfish wait for the Royal Cnidarian to set them free. They now break free when no Royal is near (in the Reef too, if he falls first), and once a wave has no boss left, any monster that has stayed untouchable for 20 seconds fades away.
- **Game:** **`/discord`** shows the invite to the Do Damage Discord in a window with an **Open** button that opens it in your browser (older clients get the link in chat), and every Discord link (the client's Discord button and account-linking steps, the website, the wiki, the news) points to the new invite, discord.gg/YTYWTFDRCD.

## Details

### Skins sold at the Armoire

- **World server** (`ReskinHandler.cs`): a `Reskin` for a skin the account doesn't own now buys it, with the account server's rules (`/account/purchaseSkin`): not a Realm Pass skin, the class at the skin's level (the best of the class's stats and the character's own level), and its `<Cost>` (200 gold by default), through `Database.PurchaseSkin` (an atomic `HINCRBY` on credits, given back if it goes below 0). The player's gold stat follows, a chat line says "Skin bought for 200 gold." or why not, and the skin goes on.
- **Client** (`npc.rs`, `main.rs`): the Armoire window lists every selectable skin of the class (`screens::class_skins`) in rows of five with its state under the name: nothing when owned, its price in gold (red when short), "Level N", or "Realm Pass"; locked ones are dimmed and can't be clicked. One for sale opens a question in the window with the skin, "Buy {name} for {cost} gold?", "Yours for good, on every character of this class." and the gold (or "Not enough gold", without a Buy button); Back or Escape returns to the shelves. Once the character wears the bought skin, the client adds it to the account's owned skins, so the character list and the Armoire show it owned without signing in again.
- One new line, translated in every `client-rs/app/lang/*.json`; the rest were there.
- `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/getting-started.md` (where skins are bought), `SETUP-LOCAL.md` ("Changes from upstream").
- **Redeploys:** world servers and client; no message changes. Older clients only ever send `Reskin` for skins they own, so they behave as before. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: no migration; purchases write `credits` and `skins` as the account server does.

### Scrying Glass: Ask to join and the watched player's gear

- **World server** (`SpectatorHub.cs`, `Player.Visits.cs`, `MessageIds.cs`; `Shared/VisitRules.cs`, `Shared/VisitBook.cs`): a new `SPECTATE` action, 8, from the big view asks the one shown to let the viewer in, through `Player.AskToVisit(glass: true)`: the same request, box, cooldowns, `/yes` and `/no` as `/visit`. `VisitBook.Request.Glass` remembers it came from the glass, and `VisitRules.Refusal(realm: true)` then lets a Yes take the visitor into a realm too (refused when it's closing, `Place.Closed`, full, or they're there already); a realm trip isn't marked as "been in". Everything else is refused as `/visit` refuses it.
- **Client** (`glass.rs`, `main.rs`): an **Ask to join** button beside "Stay on this player" (hidden while the glass shows the Nexus), the server's reply lines since the ask shown under the picture for 8 s (green, or red for an error), and a box at the picture's foot with the watched player's level, class and four equipment slots (imprint marks, the item tooltip on hover). The gear comes from the public stats the feed already carried; no new message.
- One new line ("Ask to join"), translated in every `client-rs/app/lang/*.json`.
- `client-rs/CHANGELOG.md`, `website/wiki/getting-started.md` and `commands.md`, `SETUP-LOCAL.md` (under the Scrying Glass entry).
- **Redeploys:** world servers and client, together. An older server ignores action 8 (nothing happens on the click); older clients never send it. Nobody is disconnected. Redis: nothing new.

### The Arena keeps its fights in the ring

- **Why:** a player's screenshots: in an Elder Graveyard round (wave 31) the Ghost of Skuld was over the ring's wall, and a blue flame (the Grave Caretaker's Arena Blue Flame, "Forgotten Soul") couldn't be got rid of. Skuld's `MoveTo`s are absolute positions in his crypt's map ((24, 18), (17, 26)...), outside the Arena's ring; monsters walk through the ring's walls (`Entity.TileOccupied` only stops them at `EnemyOccupySquare` objects and no-walk ground). The Caretaker's flames orbit him and only charge a player within 3 tiles; with him dead they stood where they were, and the fight wasn't over until they were dead.
- **Server:** `World.MonsterMayStand` (new, true everywhere else). `DoomedBattleArena` overrides it: while the gates are shut, only the ring's inside (`_inside`, gateways not included). `Entity.ValidateAndMove` doesn't move a monster (an `Enemy`, allies included) from inside to a place it may not stand; `MoveTo` skips a target there. `BringBack` puts anything outside on the nearest floor each tick (it was `LeaveWorld`). `ClearStragglers`: when nothing is left of the fight's own (a name from its rounds' bosses and pools, a `HealthBarBoss` or `Quest` monster) and no boss rush or Empowered boss is to come, what's left after `StragglerMs` (15 s) is taken away with a notice. `SpawnRadius` 18 to 22. `/arenatest probe` now shuts the gates as a game does, and its report adds "went out:" with whatever was outside the ring at any moment.
- **Content:** `BehaviorDb.HauntedCemeteryGraves.cs`: the Arena Blue Flame (and its Elder version) goes to a new `Hunt` state (follows players within 30 tiles, bursts at 1) when no Caretaker (or Empowered Elder Caretaker) is within 20 tiles; this is in the Haunted and Elder Cemetery too. `ArenaSeason2.cs`: "Flying Flame Skull" and "Elder Flying Flame Skull" out of Skuld's Crypt's and the Elder Graveyard's monsters (always invulnerable, they only die with Skuld).
- **Map:** `source/Shared/resources/worlds/Dungeons/Admin Arena.jm` (the Arena's and the Admin Arena's): columns 46 and 70 and rows 50 and 74 (plain floor, symmetric about the middle at 58, 62) each copied four times, the copies keeping the ring's walls but none of the trees, rocks or other decorations; 124 × 127 to 132 × 135. The doors, gateways, middle and chests are all read from the map, so they follow.
- **Docs:** CHANGELOG (Arena, Dungeons), `website/wiki/progression.md` (The ring), `website/wiki/dungeons.md` (the Cemetery), SETUP-LOCAL ("The Arena keeps its fights in the ring").
- **Deploys:** world server (and the website for the wiki). Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes; no client change. An Arena game running when the world server rolls keeps the old map until it ends.

### Plane walk cooldowns tell the truth

- **Why:** a player's screenshot: the Cloak of the Planewalker still showed "Cooldown: 1.5 secs" after 2.6.1's rule that a plane walk needs 0.5 s of being seen (`Player.UseItem.cs` `PlaneWalks`), which makes the real wait 5.5 + 0.5 = 6 s. The client starts its cooldown bar from `<Cooldown>`, so the bar was ready at 1.5 s and the key only buzzed until 6 s.
- **Content:** `<Cooldown>` 1.5 to 6 on the Cloak of the Planewalker (`source/Shared/resources/xml/prod/EmbeddedData_EquipCXML.xml`), a new `<Cooldown>3.5</Cooldown>` on the Prism of the Bloody Surprise (`source/Shared/resources/xml/custom/DoomedRealms.xml`); the AIR client's copies kept in step. The visibility rule and its tooltip line stay (it still matters after invisibility from elsewhere). The Elder Cloak of the Planar Rift is left as it was, as before. The comment in `Player.UseItem.cs` updated.
- **Docs:** CHANGELOG (Items), SETUP-LOCAL ("Plane walks wait until you've been seen"). The wiki's gear pages take the cooldown from the XML (checked: "Cooldown 6s").
- **Deploys:** world server (it serves the XML to the client) and the website for the wiki. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes; no code change in the client.

### Arena: no wave stuck on an untouchable monster

- **Why:** a live screenshot of wave 37, the Cultist Hideout (Ironclad): the players couldn't end it ("bugged", "who even spawns it"). The green monster with the shield was a CR Green Cnidarian: a quarter of each round's monsters come from the whole tier's pool, and the Reef's Blue, Green and Pink Cnidarians start in invulnerable states ("Orbit", "Outer", "Wall", "Closer") that only the Royal Cnidarian moves them out of. With no Royal they were invulnerable for good. (The Elder Flying Flame Skull, which looks alike, was ruled out: alone in a vault it removed itself after one volley.)
- **Server:** `BehaviorDb.CnidarianReef.cs`: each invulnerable state goes to `Free` when no "CR Royal Cnidarian" is within 15 tiles (its orbit range), in the Reef too. `DoomedBattleArena.ClearUntouchable` (`DoomedWorlds.cs`): once no boss is left (a name from the fight's rounds' bosses, `HealthBarBoss` or `Quest`, and no boss rush or Empowered boss to come), a monster untouchable (Invulnerable, Invincible, Stasis or Paused) for `UntouchableMs` (20 s) on end is taken away with "The stragglers fade away.", even one from the fight's own pools (which the 15-second straggler rule leaves alone).
- **Docs:** CHANGELOG (Arena, Dungeons), `website/wiki/progression.md`, SETUP-LOCAL (the Arena entry).
- **Deploys:** world server. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes.

### Code scanning fixes

- **What:** fixes for GitHub's code scanning alerts. The account server's log lines that carry a value from a request or from PayPal (order ids, PayPal's errors and statuses, the webhook's event, Discord ids, a sprite verdict) go through the existing `ForLog()` (line breaks removed, so a value can't forge log lines): `source/App/Controllers/GoldController.cs`, `DiscordController.cs`, `SpritesController.cs`. The wiki's search only uses its page's `data-wiki` attribute as the links' start when it is "" or `../` steps (`website/wiki/wiki.js`). The grave screen's mote hash calls its second number a seed, not a salt (`client-rs/app/src/grave.rs`), which the scanner read as cryptography.
- **Left as they are (false positives):** account ids in log lines ("cleartext storage of sensitive information": an account id isn't a secret), the gateway's plain-http requests to the account server on the same machine, `deploy.py` writing the Discord bot's `discord.env` (the deploy's job), and `bot.py`'s `--dry-run`/`--try` printing a made-up or test-account password. Each wants dismissing in GitHub's Security tab.
- **Deploys:** account server, website and client; nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes. Nothing players can notice.
