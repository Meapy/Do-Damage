Release 2.6.5: a last stand for every Crucible champion, calmer Crucible floors, tide hits that count and Vaelith kept off the sky, a Crucible Leaderboard and Hall of Champions boards, climbs of up to four joined only at the start; pets grown to the next size at the Pen Keeper (only Huge pets, once Oryx-sized, past 100%); dodmg.com redone in 3D (a flight through Oryx's six stages), Ctrl+K search and a phone menu on every page, and a wiki that's easier to get around; deploys the world server, the account server, the client and the website (nobody is disconnected; growing a pet needs both client and server)

## For players

- **Armored:** the Armored effect is less protective for players now: it adds half of the player's defense instead of doubling it. It works the same as before for mobs and bosses.
- **The Crucible:** the floors are easier to follow and just as hard. When a floor changes shape, everything that will fall cracks at once and then falls in a wave from the middle; the shapes that keep turning do it in a step every few seconds (crack, then shift) and hold still in between, where before their edges cracked and fell all the time. The sweeping blades and tides, Pyrrhax's lava veins, Ferrogrim's hammer rings and Chronarch's clock hands move slower and are wider (the minute hand no longer whips round at nearly three times the hour hand's speed). Damage from Nerissa's tide and the clock hands now always counts: before, it often showed a number and took no health. Vaelith no longer ends up stuck in the sky when the Apex reshapes under him, and in Unbound he follows you round the halo instead of hanging over the void. A climb takes **up to four**, and only from the start: once it begins nobody can join, by the portal, `/crucible`, `/visit`, `/summon`, `/help` or the Scrying Glass (a full lobby sends the next player to a new one). The **Crucible Leaderboard** trophy stands beside the Arena's in the Arena court, with its own window and a sign showing the best solo and group climbs: ranked by floors cleared, then time to the last of them, listing only those who were there when that floor fell and never fell themselves, solo and group apart, never a climb with an admin in it, and starting fresh with this update. The Hall of Champions gains **Crucible Solo** and **Crucible Group** boards (statues on the tower too), their top ten getting +10%, +6% or +3% loot in the Crucible. Every champion now makes a **last stand** at the end of its health (12%, Vaelith at 8%), a new phase with a floor of its own: Ferrogrim's **Last Bastion** (the inner keep's molten walls close in on him, two gates in each to slip out through), Nerissa's **Tsunami** (a lagoon, great waves over it from side after side, one dry lane through each), Pyrrhax's **Molten Sea** (the crucible floods but for a slag-rimmed island drifting across it), Chronarch's **Zero Hour** (the hours burn a third at a time, stepping round, under both hands) and Vaelith's **Final Aria** (three arms of flame the other way, gold falling on everyone and a crown of stars).
- **Website:** dodmg.com is redone in 3D. The home page is a flight: a castle town under the moon, through its gate and from portal to portal down Oryx's six stages as you scroll, each portal holding that stage's boss built in 3D from the game's own sprite (the two Stone Guardians, Oryx 1, 2, 3 and 4, and Oryx the Ascended God), each card showing the same boss, its stage and its danger, the sky warming from violet to blood the deeper you go; the wiki, gold store and changelog keep a calm drifting night behind glass panels. **Ctrl+K** or **/** searches the wiki from any page, and phones and narrow windows get a menu button with every link (before, the bar showed only Wiki). The **wiki** is easier to get around: its pages are listed under four sections (Start here, Dungeons and loot, Classes and gear, Every day) with nothing hidden, every page shows where it sits (Wiki › section › page), Previous and Next go through the pages in that order, "On this page" stays beside long pages on wide screens and marks the part you're reading, a button takes you back to the top, and the wiki home has a card for every page. Pages also load faster: the search loads only when you search.
- **Mechanic:** every turret does twice the damage, shots and final blast alike, and every shot pierces enemies (the lower tiers' didn't).
- **Pets:** a pet at its last level can be **grown to the next size** at the Pen Keeper, for fame or gold: Tiny to Small 1,000 fame or 100 gold, Small to Medium 3,000 or 200, Medium to Large 8,500 or 450, Large to Huge 25,000 or 1,000. It keeps its level and what it ate, is drawn its new size's height, and feeds on to the new size's last level (once the pen takes that size). The biggest size, the Lil' Oryxes' and the boss pets', was called Oryx and is now **Huge**. **Only Huge pets go past 100%**: every other size now tops out at 100% damage and 140% healing at its last level (was 120% and 160%; Bes, Nut and Geb about 540 a second instead of 650), while Huge pets still reach 120% and 160% at level 30. Growing never weakens a pet: it stays at 100% through Small, Medium and Large and climbs on to 120% once it's Huge. The window shows the grow buttons on the pet's card at its last level, and a generator's tooltip shows the size it was grown to.

## Details

### Armored, weaker for players only

- **Asked:** reduce the Armored effect's effectiveness for players while leaving mobs and bosses unchanged.
- **Rules:** `StatsManager.DamageWithDefense` gives a player target 1.5x defense (the original defense plus half of it), while enemies and other entities keep 2x defense. `StatsManager.GetDefenseDamage` uses the 1.5x rule for incoming damage to players. Armor piercing and Armor Broken behavior is unchanged, and Armored still prevents those effects from removing defense.
- **Client:** `client-rs/crates/world/src/combat.rs` passes whether the target is a player and mirrors the 1.5x versus 2x rules; its defense test covers both.
- **Redeploys:** world server and Rust client together. Nobody is disconnected. No Redis migration.

### The Crucible: calmer floors, ground hits that count, Vaelith on the floor

- **Asked:** the owner found the difficulty right but the moving floors and the damaging floors hectic and hard to follow; smoother, the difficulty kept or a little higher. Vaelith got stuck in the sky when the floor changed. A player reported Nerissa's water showing damage taken while their health didn't drop.
- **Reshaping (`ArenaForm`, `logic/behaviors/CrucibleArenas.cs`):** on a new form every square that will fall cracks at once and falls in the wave out from the middle (no random jitter any more); before, each cracked `warnMs` before its own fall, so the cracks rippled too. Moving forms (`ArenaForms.Moves`) now step every `beatMs` (4 s): `warnMs` before each step, the squares that go crack, and at the step the floor jumps on as far as it has turned, the same average speed as before. Before, the form was re-sampled every 250 ms, which on a tile grid made its edges flicker, cracking and falling non-stop.
- **Hazards (`logic/behaviors/Crucible.cs`, `BehaviorDb.Crucible.cs`):** sweeping arms are painted every 100 ms (was 150, so they jumped less); Blades, Tempest, Whirlpool, Maelstrom, the Apex's Iron and Unbound turn 20-25% slower with arms 2.2 wide (were 1.6-1.7); the clock's hands 14 and 32 degrees a second, 2.8 and 2.0 wide (were 18 and 50, 2.4 and 1.4), Reversal 1.15 times that (was 1.3); the tide rolls at 7.5 squares a second, 3 deep, with a 1.7 s warning (was 9.5-10, 2.5 deep, 1.5 s), every 5.5 s (the Apex's 6.5 s, so two never cross); lava veins grow a square every 60 ms with a 1.1 s glow (40 ms, 0.9 s); Hammerfall's rings 6.5 squares a second (8). A strip of warning ahead of the arms and tides was tried and dropped at the owner's word: the movement reads by itself.
- **Ground hits refused (`Player.Ground.cs`, `World.cs`):** the client reports a ground hit when it takes it, and the server checked the square as it was when the message arrived; a tide 2.5 deep at 10 squares a second had often moved on by then, so the hit was refused while the client had shown it. Now a Crucible painter that paints over damaging ground tells the world (`World.GroundPaintedOver`), and for 1.5 s a hit reported on that square counts with that ground's damage (`RecentDamagingGround`). The client's roll was already used up either way, so the shared random sequence stays in step.
- **Vaelith:** the Apex's `ArenaForm` had `moveFoes: false` ("hovers over the void"), so wherever the floor fell under him (the fortress's pits, the sky between the cross's arms, the halo's empty middle) he was left on sky and couldn't move off it. Now he's set on the nearest floor like every other monster, and in Unbound he stays within 18 squares of the middle (was 6, all of it void) and follows players round the halo.
- **Redeploys:** the world server (rolling, nobody disconnected). No client or Redis change.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/crucible.md` (how floors reshape and turn, the tide, the clock hands, Vaelith).
- **Not done:** the ground-hit fix wasn't seen in game (it needs someone without god mode walking through a tide); it follows from the code.

### Growing a pet, Huge pets, 100% for the rest

- **What and why:** the owner asked for a pet fed to its last level to be grown in size for fame (1,000 to 25,000) or gold (100 to 1,000); first as the next size with its 60%-120% spread over the new levels, then (asked during the work) that only the last size go past 100% so growing never weakens a pet, and that the last size be called Huge rather than Oryx.
- **Rules:** `source/Shared/pets/PetLevels.cs`: `GrowPrice` (1,000/100, 3,000/200, 8,500/450, 25,000/1,000; geometric from 1,000 to 25,000 and 100 to 1,000, rounded), `GrownSize`, and `Power(level, max, baseMax)`: every size but Huge climbs to `FullShare` (100% damage) at its stone's last level; a grown pet stays there until Huge, then climbs to 1 (120%) at level 30; a native Huge pet climbs the whole way as before. `DamageScale`, `HealScale`, `DrawScale` and `Heal` take the stone's last level. `Sizes`' last is "Huge". The client's `petlevels.rs` (`power`, `grown_size`, `stone_grown`) matches; `PetLevelsTests` and the client's tests check both (growing never lowers damage at any level, Huge reaches 120%, the rest 100%).
- **Content:** `petSize="Oryx"` is `petSize="Huge"` in both copies of `DoomedRealms.xml`, `BlackBetweenObjects.xml` and `CrucibleObjects.xml` (the attribute only, as `tools/boss_pets.py` now writes it; the generators weren't rerun); `tools/boss_pets.py`, `tools/pet_healing.py` (healing unchanged: its rolls are by pet id, the size by index) and `tools/wiki_gear.py` (cards at the last level: 100% or 120% by size) say Huge.
- **Server:** `Player.PetPen.cs`: `PetGrown` (sizes grown), `PetSizeOf`, `PetMaxLevel(type, grown)`, `PetGrowPrice`, acts 11 and 12 (`GrowPet`, paid through `PayPen`, which `GrowPen` now uses too), the level-up message says when it can grow, `PetPower` draws a grown pet its new size's height against its stone's (`SizeHeights`, Huge now 1.7). `PetPenData` adds the size as grown and the grow price at its end. `Player.cs` loads and saves `petGrown`; `UseItem`, `/removepet`, the Pen Keeper and imprints carry `ItemData.PetGrown` on the generator.
- **Client:** `petpen.rs`: the card's size as grown (from the server; an older server's stone's), fame and gold grow buttons on the card at the last level; the pen's chips ring the grown size. `hud.rs`: a generator's tooltip shows its grown size, its level against the new last level and its numbers by `power`; `pet_size_name` knows Huge. Proto: `PetGrow` after `PetLevel` in `PetPen`. New lines "Huge" and "Grow it to a {size} pet:" in all ten languages.
- **Redeploys:** world servers and client together. An older client reads the XML's new `petSize="Huge"` as no size (its tooltips show no level; the server is unaffected) and has no grow buttons. Nobody is disconnected.
- **Redis:** new fields `petGrown` (characters, 0 = none) and `PetGrown` in a generator's slot data. Nothing is migrated: no pet was grown before.
- **Balance note:** every Tiny to Large pet at its last level loses a sixth of its damage (120% to 100%) and healing (160% to 140%); Huge pets are unchanged. Pets migrated in 2.6.4 at two thirds of the way are now at about 87% instead of 100% for those sizes.

### The Crucible: a last stand for every champion

- **Asked:** more phases for each boss, a bit harder, impressive and unique.
- **What:** each champion gets a last phase below 12% of its health (Vaelith 8%, after Unbound): a 2.6 to 3 s untouchable rise (its cue, the ground shaking, a flash, a line), then the phase to the end. Each has a floor mechanic none of the others has:
  - Ferrogrim, **Last Bastion** (cue 24): a new form, `keep` (the inner keep, square, round towers at the corners), and a new painter, `ClosingWalls`: square walls of Molten Grate closing from the keep's edge to the middle at 2.2 squares a second, one every 6.5 s, each with two 3.2-wide gates on opposite sides that stay at the same share of the side as it closes. He holds the middle with his kit a little faster, the anvil's cross and double rings. Tempest's banners go when it ends.
  - Nerissa, **Tsunami** (cue 25): a new form, `lagoon` (an islet, a ring of Deep Tide crossed by four shallows, a reef ring with four spires), and a `TideSweep` four deep with a single five-wide lane, every 6.5 s; two eels in the lagoon. She stays on her islet.
  - Pyrrhax, **Molten Sea** (cue 26): a new painter, `SafeIsland`, on the crucible form: everything within reach (the spout too) glows for 2 s but an island 4.5 squares across rimmed with Cooling Slag, floods with Forge Lava for 6 s while the island drifts at 1.4 squares a second, then drains for 3.5 s; the island starts somewhere new each time, always on floor.
