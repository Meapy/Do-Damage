Release 2.6.9: the Monk reworked, death replays line up, super elixirs that last, and the Hive remade. Deploys the world server, client and website; nobody is disconnected. The Monk's Flowing Strike needs the world server and client together.

## For players

### Monk
- **The Monk flows:** the Monk fights up close with their fists, and every third punch that lands builds a **Chi**, a golden orb circling them (up to five; they fade one a second after 4 seconds without a punch landing). The fountains (Fountain Droplet T0 to Heart of the Fountains T8, and Power of the Fountains T7) are now the **Flowing Strike**: the Monk flows to where they aim (6 tiles at T0 up to 8 at T8, stopping short of a wall), striking every enemy within about a tile of the way (120 up to 440 damage), and a fountain bursts where they land: 180 up to 630 damage round them, and 110 up to 380 health to them and the allies near them. The strike spends every Chi, each making the burst and the heal a fifth stronger (double with five); spending all five is a **Flow**: the enemies in the burst are Armor Broken for 3 seconds and the Monk goes Berserk for 4. Drawn: the Chi orbs (spinning faster, joined by a ring, with all five), the Monk's afterimages and a streak of water along the way, a column of water and a lotus of light where they land, a petal turning gold for each Chi spent, and for a Flow five orbs flung out and a whirl of gold. The fountains have their own icons, a palm of water in a ring of prayer beads, by tier.
- **A new look and five skins:** the Monk is redrawn (a shaven head with a topknot, a saffron robe with a crimson sash, prayer beads, wrapped fists). The Armoire sells the Iron Lotus, the Stormfist Disciple, the Ember Fist and the Jade Wanderer at 200 gold and the 16 by 16 Celestial Grandmaster at 800, each with something of its own round it (a lotus underfoot and drifting petals; lightning jumping between the fists; burning fists and rising embers; mist and bamboo leaves; circling golden beads, glowing eyes and palms, lotuses in its steps). Dyes work on the Monk and every skin.

### Death replays
- **Hits land where they hit:** the shot that hurt the character now meets them on screen, the damage number and its sparks coming as it strikes, and the monsters' shots that missed fly past them as they did on the player's screen. The character no longer trails behind the shots (about a tenth to a quarter of a second): they're shown where they really were, walking smoothly along their path instead of gliding from tick to tick. Replays already saved play this way too.

- **The Crucible Leaderboard lists its top ten:** its window always shows places #1 to #10 for solo and for group climbs, a dash where no climb holds a place yet (the live board has five of each), so the places still to take show. ### Items
- **Super elixirs last:** the Super Elixir of Magic (250 MP a drink, 9999 doses) used to vanish after its first drink. It now keeps its doses: each drink takes one off, the slot and the tooltip show how many are left, and it goes with its last. Dropped, traded or stored, it keeps its count.
- **The Super Elixir of Health:** a new red twin of the magic one, 250 HP a drink and the same 9999 doses. It's in the arena goal (Roar of the Crowd): its 400-fame tier now gives both elixirs, and its loot rolls it as often as the magic one (uncommon).

### Dungeons
- **The Hive, remade after its maker's design:** the Hive (first a player-made dungeon, Suestwo's UGC1) is laid out anew each run from 20 round rooms (radius 4 to 7, the start 6, the Queen's 10), always five rooms between the start and the Queen, joined by 3-wide halls, with puddles of slowing, sinking honey and chunks of comb. Each room holds 3-5 Fat Bees, 0-3 Fat Angry Bees, 2-5 Small Bees or a Mini Hive, and a few Maggot Eggs of one kind; the Queen's room has eggs of all three round her.
- **The bees:** Fat Bees shoot aimed stingers, and a quarter turn into Fat Angry Bees when they die; Fat Angry Bees chase you for 8 seconds at a time and make you bleed; Small Bees charge and sting on contact, circling the Queen near her; a Mini Hive sends out a Small Bee every 3 seconds (up to 6) and bursts into 2-3 more at a quarter of its health; a Maggot Egg cracks at its first hit into 2-3 larvae, which circle the Queen, nip and slow, and turn into Fat Bees after 10 seconds.
- **The Queen Bee** stays unhurt until you come within 7 tiles, then cycles: 3 seconds of rings of 8 stingers, 2 seconds resting unhurt (flashing green, drifting after you), 3.5 seconds of three dazing globs of honey at a time, 2 more resting; throughout she throws a slowing honey bomb (100 within 3 tiles) every 5 seconds and a Maggot Egg every 3 (at most 8 near her). Her health grows a fifth for each extra player. Bees that hatch in the fight (from her eggs or a room's) give no experience or loot.

### The Monk reworked: Chi and the Flowing Strike, a new look and five skins

- **What and why:** the owner asked for the Monk to be finished and made fun, after the Druid and Mechanic. Its ability was a copy of the Priest's tome (`HealNova` and a `Teleport`), its sprite a plain block of yellow, its description "." and its fountains shared one icon.
- **Server:** `Player.Flow.cs` (new): Chi (`ChiHit` from `Player.Projectiles.cs`, once per weapon shot, on fists only: `FistSlotType` 200; `ChiTick` fades it) and the `FlowStrike` activate (`ActivateEffects.FlowStrike`, `AEFlowStrike` from `Player.UseItem.cs`): `FlowEnd` finds the way (stopping before the first square `World.IsPassable` refuses, and not past a shut Oryx-chain gate, `CanAbilityMoveTo`), the enemies within `FlowPathWidth` of it are struck as the Monk passes (`TidePearl.OnWay`), the Monk moves with `Shift` (`TeleportPosition` on stacked floors), and the burst lands `FlowDashMs` later (`RelicDamage`, `ActivateHealHp`, `HealTurrets`; with all five Chi `ArmorBroken` and `Berserk`). The Chi is a new stat, `StatDataType.Chi` (224), sent to everyone only when it's not 0 at first, as `Aura` is; older clients ignore it. The strike's look is the Relic `ShowEffect` look 66 (`LOOK_FLOW`), sent before the move so clients still have the Monk where they set off.
- **Content:** the nine fountains (`CustomItems.xml` 0x5814-0x581b and Power of the Fountains 0xD1AE in `DoomedRealms.xml`, both copies of each): one `<Activate maxDistance totalDamage impactDamage radius amount range>FlowStrike</Activate>` each (MP, stats, bags and heal amounts unchanged), a new description and their icons on `monkFountains16x16`. The Monk's `<Description>` (both copies).
- **Art (`tools/draw_monk.py`, new, as `draw_druid.py`):** the Monk's block (index 30) of `doomed/images/players.png` and `playersMask.png` with a dye mask; `MonkSkins8x8.png`, `MonkSkins16x16.png` and their masks, `MonkFountains16x16.png`; `MonkSkins.xml` (both copies, types 0xE220-0xE224, `<Cost>` 200 and 800, `<SkinLook>`). Registered in `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py` (`monkSkins8`, `monkSkins16`, `monkFountains16x16`, the XML in `RS_ONLY_OBJECT_XML`); `client-rs/assets/manifest.json` regenerated.
- **Client:** `app/src/chi.rs` (new): the orbs round any player with Chi, drawn after the wards (`view.rs`), with `ChiMemory` (`particles.rs`) for a new orb's flash and the spent ones flaring out. `app/src/particles/flow.rs` (new): look 66 (`relic::look::FLOW`), the way, the burst and the lotus (drawn with the ground marks). `app/src/auras.rs`: the five skins' looks (`lotus`, `storm`, `ember`, `jade`, `grandmaster`). `app/src/hud.rs`: the Flowing Strike's tooltip; ten new lines in every `lang/*.json`. `crates/proto` `stat::CHI`. `--effect` now takes values over 255 (`main.rs`, `Particles::test_effect`): `--effect 261`-`266` were silently ignored before.
- **Redeploy:** world server and client together: an older client shows no Chi, no strike and the old tooltip (the Monk still moves and the burst still lands); an older server leaves the fountains' new XML unknown to it. Nobody is disconnected.

- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/classes.md` (the Monk's Chi and Flowing Strike, its skins); `tools/wiki_gear.py` describes `FlowStrike`. The generated Monk and Fountain pages pick up the rest.

### Death replays line up

- **What and why:** reported from a real replay (an Elder Undead Lair death): the shots that dealt damage didn't line up with the character. The recording is the server's view: the player's place comes with their moves, a tick or so late and a ping behind, and their hits when their client reported them, while monsters' shots are recorded as the server fires them. Played as recorded, the player trailed the shots by their ping and a tick, and the client's glide added another tick, so a shot crossed the place they had been a moment before, and the numbers came at the wrong moment. Measured on the sample: the drawn player met the shots that hurt them up to half a second after the hit.
- **Client:** `app/src/replay/sync.rs` (new) works out, once per replay, how much earlier the player's recorded places are shown (the lead, 0 to 400 ms, the one that brings the shots that hurt them closest to them; 100 ms on the sample) and, for each hit from a shot, which of its shooter's shots dealt it and when it met them; the hits are then shown at that moment, in the order the server counted them (each shooter's shots reordered if matched the other way round), and the rest (unmatched shots, the ground) as much earlier as the matched ones were. Past the last recorded place the player carries on as they were moving, since the server never hears of their last moments. `app/src/replay.rs`: the player is placed along their recorded places each step (no glide), facing and walking from their path; monsters' shots fly through them (`World::untouchable`, `crates/world`), and the shot matched to a hit ends on them with sparks thrown its way; a tick from before a hit already shown doesn't put the health back up. The hit list and timeline use the times shown.
- **Redeploy:** client only. Nothing on the server or in the replay format changed, so replays already saved line up too. Nobody is disconnected.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/progression.md` (death replays: a hit lands as its shot strikes).
- **Not done:** the player's own shots are still drawn as the server heard of them (a ping late against the monsters); hits a monster deals by touching them keep the server's time.

### The wiki: sections that fold, tables that open one at a time, the whole width

- **What and why:** the owner's reports: the Pets page's size table squeezed into a narrow column and scrolled sideways, "and others like it"; then that pages were still long, narrow and full of empty space, asking for tables "into drop downs, so you can expose what you want to look at" and the best wiki they could have. Every table was capped at 780px, the width of running text; "On this page" took a column beside the page; the long pages ran to 20,000-50,000px.
- **Folding (`website/wiki/wiki.js`):** each ## section becomes a `<details class="fold">` panel: its heading, chips for what it holds (entries, rows, tables, topics) and, closed, its first sentence or two. A page taller than 2.2 windows starts with every section closed; a short page starts open. What the reader opens or closes is remembered per page (`localStorage` `dd-wiki-folds`), with Open all / Close all over the sections. Three or more ###/#### headings over tables or card lists become `details.acc` groups that open one at a time (and again a level down: Gear statistics' Weapons, then each weapon kind). A table of 12 or more rows gets a filter; outside a group, one of more than 12 rows shows 8 with "Show all N rows"; any of 6 or more rows sorts by a column when its head is clicked (numbers as numbers; a third click puts it back). A link, a search result or the menu's part list opens the sections it points into (`hashchange`, and a click on the same `#`); a filter over cards opens the sections holding matches and hides those with none, then puts them back as they were when cleared; a filter box left at the end of another section moves out between the sections (Dungeons'). Gear pages fold each group of items, class pages each group in a tab. Printing opens everything. Without the script the pages are as before, all open.
- **Builder (`tools/build_wiki.py`):** the "In short" box is in the page's head, beside its screenshot; "On this page" is a list under the page's own entry in the sidebar (`ul.nav-toc`, marked as it's read) instead of a column beside the page. `tools/wiki_community.py`: a goal's rewards are a tier to a line (`dl.g-tiers`).
- **Styles (`website/style.css`):** the section panels (an open one's heading stays at the top while it's read), groups, table tools and sortable heads; a table is as wide as it needs, up to the whole column; text at most 820px; the wiki up to 2,000px wide with smaller gutters and gaps; item icons in tables 36px; item cards with their sprite beside their name and the rest across the card, from 310px wide; screenshots float beside the text where a section is 1,150px wide or more; the wiki home's six tiles in a row on a wide screen.
- **Measured** (every wiki page and two gear pages in the browser at 390, 1,024, 1,440 and 1,920px): nothing is wider than the screen, no filter box is hidden. Tables scrolling sideways at 1,440px: 35 before, 7 after the width change, all on Gear statistics.
- **Redeploy:** website only. Nobody is disconnected.

### The Hive, remade

- **What and why:** the owner asked to fully implement the Hive from its original player-made files (github.com/Suestwo/UGC1: TheHive.xml, HiveGen.xml, the floors, key and items). The game already had the dungeon's objects, items and a fixed map, but its behaviours were a stub (every bee wandering and shooting once a second, eggs and Mini Hives only spawning on death, a two-state Queen). Now the layout and the behaviours follow UGC1.
- **Map:** `tools/build_hive.py` (new) draws the rooms from HiveGen.xml's numbers (start circle radius 6 with the Portal of Cowardice, 18 rooms radius 4-7 rolling their monsters from its `default`/`branchEnd` lists, the Queen's radius 10 with 1-2 of each egg) on a 5x4 grid of narrow halls; it writes `source/Shared/resources/worlds/Dungeons/The Hive.jm` (regenerated). `Dungeons.xml`: the Hive's `<RandomLayout passage="3" corridor="3">` with `<Goal path="5">TH Queen Bee</Goal>` (HiveGen's main branch of 7), so `DungeonGenerator` joins 7, 12 or all 18 rooms anew each visit. The honey (`TH honey Heal` turning the floor to honey in UGC1) is painted on the map as small puddles, about 8% of a room.
- **Behaviours:** `BehaviorDb.Hive.cs` rewritten after TheHive.xml: the Queen (`StayCloseToSpawn` 7, the Go / rest / DazeEm / rest cycle, `Grenade` and `TossObject` of eggs, `ScaleHP2(20)`), Fat Bees (25% `TransformOnDeath` into Fat Angry Bees), Fat Angry Bees (`Follow` 8 s on, 2 s off), Small Bees (`Orbit` the Queen, `Charge`), Mini Hive (`Spawn` up to 6, bursts at 25%), eggs (crack below 98%), larvae (`Orbit` the Queen, `Transform` into Fat Bees after 10 s). Loot: the Queen keeps her drops (Dexterity, two Bottled Honey, Mountain drops, Bee Generator 3%) with UGC1's Scepter and Orb at 8.5% (from 5%) and its T5-T7 weapons and T6-T7 armour added; Fat Bees drop T3-T5 armour, Fat Angry Bees T3-T5 weapons, Mini Hives three 50% Bottled Honey. The Queen's root state no longer holds Invulnerable (so `BehaviorStatesTests.Known` loses her).
- **Changed from UGC1:** what hatches during the fight (larvae grown up, and their bees come back angry) is the existing no-experience `TH Fat Bees 2` / `TH Red Fat Bees 2`, with no loot, since the Queen throws eggs without end; the Queen Bee Summoner isn't used (she stands in her room, and `World.BossDied` opens the Realm Portal); the Bee Egg and Wine Cellar Incantation drops are left out (no Bee Egg here; the Incantation drops nowhere else). The objects' stats (health, damage, Bleeding 1.4 s) stay as the game's XML has them, which are the released dungeon's, not UGC1's first draft.
- **Tests:** `RandomDungeonTests` gains the Hive (30 seeds: the Queen once and reachable, the Portal of Cowardice and every egg kind kept).
- **Redeploy:** world server only. Nobody is disconnected; Hives already open keep their old map until they close.
- **Wiki:** `website/wiki/dungeons.md` (a new Hive section).
