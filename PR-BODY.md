Release 2.6.1: Arena Season 2, the sword classes reworked, Ascendant forcefields and Father wards stack, Necromantic items and the Necklace of Light drop half as often, the Vial of Cascading Dusk reworked, no more endless invisibility from plane walking, a world server crash fixed, daily rewards and gifts need 5 stars, the Gold Store (buy gold with PayPal); deploys client, servers and website (players are not disconnected)

## For players

- **Arena:** Season 2, *Rise of the Fallen*. Every wave is now a round from one of nearly 80 dungeons and corners of the realm, in six tiers: Realm 2's bosses and the realm's gods first, then the Godlands, the Snake Pit, Candyland and the Mad Lab, the Manor, the Docks and the Library, the Tomb, Oryx's Castle and the Elder dungeons, and from wave 30 Oryx, the Shatters, the Cultist Hideout, the Void and the Reef, up to Oryx 4, the Ascended God and the Elder Shatters, Cult and Void. Each tier deals its rounds from a shuffled pile, so nothing repeats until the rest have come. A round can be a Horde (no boss, three times the monsters), a Clash (two dungeons at once), a Boss Rush (three bosses one after another), a Throwback (an old boss back as a Legendary one) or, every tenth wave, a Showdown (the Godlands' four gods, the Tomb's three guardians, the Battle for the Nexus's four bosses, Oryx's court, the Elder Cult). From wave 6 rounds may carry a twist: Elite, Swarm, Ironclad, Frenzy, Bounty (double fame), Blessing (you hit harder for 20 seconds) or Golden (full loot). From wave 12 an Empowered Elder boss may rise in the middle of a fight: rare, announced to the whole server, and worth 200 fame to everyone there.
- **Arena:** a banner shows each round as it begins (its name, shape, twist and bosses), the box under the wave counter keeps its name, and the vote panel shows what the next fight will be. Empowered bosses and a Boss Rush's next boss get banners too.
- **Arena:** the leaderboard keeps Season 1 exactly as it ended, on its own tab, and Season 2 starts afresh; Season 2 runs also show the Empowered bosses slain and the round the run fell at. `/leaderboard 1` lists Season 1. The trophy's sign shows the season's record, a new season record from wave 10 is announced, and the Hall of Champions' Arena boards follow the current season.

- **Classes:** the sword classes (Warrior, Knight, Paladin, Pirate) get a shared passive, **Frontline**: 15% less damage from every hit, and the sword heals 1.5% of the damage it deals (at most 40 health a second). A Paladin's sword heals every ally within 4 tiles as much; one shot in ten misses a Pirate entirely ("Lucky!"). Tiered swords now pierce, and the untiered swords whose shots waved, looped round the player or came back (Doctor Swordsworth, the Arcane Rapier, Henchman's Claymore, the Sword of the Colossus and others) fly straight over the same range. Abilities: Warrior helms make you Armored for 1-2 s, Knight shields Armored for 1.5-2.5 s and their shots leave enemies Exposed (−20 defense) for 3 s even when they can't be stunned, Paladin seals heal everyone in range at once (60 at T0 to 180 at T8). Highest stats: Knight Attack 60 (was 50), Paladin Vitality 60 (was 40), Pirate Life 770 (was 700) and Defense 30 (was 25). Damage numbers now show Armored as the server always worked it out (double defense, holding against armor-piercing shots).
- **Classes:** **Ascendant forcefields and Father wards stack.** Before, of several forcefields a player stood in only the strongest counted, and a second ward over one still up only replaced it if stronger. Now the strongest counts in full and each other at half the one before: two 10% forcefields take 15% off, three 17% (at most 90%); two Last Rite wards of 170 give 255, three 297. A new ward never leaves less than it would alone (so a Father recasting on a worn-down ward still refills it), and the ward lasts to the latest of their times.

- **Dungeons:** the untiered item an Empowered Elder boss drops (the Necromantic weapons, armor, rings and the rest) now drops at half the chance: 3% for each player who did enough damage, down from 6%.
- **Dungeons:** Empowered Malus' blood moon ritual heals him far less: each cultist soul still standing when the chant ends gives back 1% of his health, down from 2.5% (at most 3% a ritual instead of 7.5%). Players on their own could not out-damage it.
- **Dungeons:** bosses in the **Elder dungeons and the Black Between** grow half as much with a group: each extra player nearby adds half the health it used to, Empowered bosses there included. Most Elder bosses went from 15% of their health per extra player to 7.5% (the Elder Void Entity 10% to 5%, Elder bosses that shared their regular one's rate, such as the Elder Abyss's or Tomb's, half of it), and the Mysterious One's three forms from 35% to 17.5%. Five players on an Elder Shatters boss now face 1.3 times its solo health instead of 1.6; on the Mysterious One's last form 857,000 instead of 1,210,000. A boss fought alone is unchanged.
- **Items:** the Vial of Cascading Dusk does **twice the damage**: 400 where it lands and 3,000 over 6 seconds (past defense), the four splashes round it 200 + 1,500 and the four beyond 100 + 750. It has a **new look**: the vial tumbles along a high arc, a glowing violet heart with a white glint turning round it, leaving a ribbon of dusk, stars shaken loose and drops falling, its shadow racing along the floor under it; where it lands the glass shatters in a flash, a shockwave and a white ring race out, dusk is flung everywhere and a spiral of twilight and stars climbs out of a dark pool under a ring of stars; streams of dusk run out along the floor to each next splash, which bursts the same way, smaller and darker, and the pools breathe mist and the odd star while they bleed. Its tooltip shows its numbers: Splash 400 + 3,000 over 6 secs, Radius 2.5 sqrs, 4 round it 200 + 1,500 over 6 secs, 4 beyond 100 + 750 over 6 secs, and that the damage over time ignores defense. The Trinity Trap's shows its three stages the same way (Lightning 300 and paralysed 2 secs, Veil 300 and slowed 4 secs, Rift 200 + 450 over 3 secs, radius 3.5, the rift 4). Before, only the description said what they do; the wiki's gear pages list the same rows.
- **Game:** the daily login reward and gifts (this month's free Lil' Oryx) now need **5 stars** on the account. One player made eleven new accounts in a few days just to claim the one-per-account Lil' Oryx on each and trade it away; a fresh account now gets "Reach 5 stars to collect daily rewards and gifts." A class gives stars from its best character's fame (one at 20 fame, five at 2,000), so a few characters played a little get there. Quests and achievements need no stars.
- **Items:** the **Cloak of the Planewalker** made you invisible for 5.5 seconds but came back after 1.5, so a Rogue could chain it and never be seen. It and the Trickster's **Prism of the Bloody Surprise** (3 seconds of invisibility, back after half a second) now need you to have been **visible for half a second** before they work again: in practice the Planewalker is ready about 6 seconds after you used it, not 1.5. Pressing it too soon plays the error sound and costs no mana. Their tooltips and the wiki say so. Other cloaks were already slower than their invisibility and are unchanged, and the Elder **Cloak of the Planar Rift** is kept as it is.
- **Items:** the **Necklace of Light** drops half as often from Elder Limon the Sprite God: 0.25% for each player who did enough damage, down from 0.5%.
- **Servers:** fixed the crash that took Europe 2 down on 18 November (23:58 to 00:02): a rare timing problem when a player disconnected. If a world server ever stops unexpectedly again, it now comes back by itself within seconds instead of staying down until someone restarts it.

## Details

### The sword classes: Frontline

- **Why:** the Warrior, Knight, Paladin and Pirate fight at 3.5-4 tiles, where radial patterns put about twice the bullets on them that an 8-tile class sees, while their heavier armor only let them survive about 1.3 times the hits (at T14, against a 160-damage bullet: Warrior 7.6, Wizard 5.9, Pirate 6.0). Swords were also the only close-range weapon that didn't pierce, the Knight's stun did nothing to the third of bosses that can't be stunned (240 of 758), the Paladin's Vitality topped out at 40, and the Pirate wore leather with 700 health.
- **Server:** new `Player.Frontline.cs`. `Frontline` is a class whose first slot type is 1 (swords). `FrontlineDamage` takes 15% off (rounded to the nearest point) after `ShieldedDamage` in `PlayerHit`, `Player.Damage` and both ground damage paths. `FrontlineHit` (from `EnemyHit`, weapon shots only) feeds a `FrontlineLeech`: 1.5% of the damage dealt, at most 40 health a second, paid at most once a second with a green "+n" (not while Sick, not for ghosts), with `HealedBy` for assist credit. A Paladin's payout also heals every other player within 4 tiles. `PirateDodged`: 10% of a Pirate's projectile hits that would deal damage do nothing; conditions the client already put on are resent, and a "Lucky!" notification is shown. `source/Tests/FrontlineTests.cs` covers the rounding, the leech's pay and cap, and which classes are Frontline.
- **Data** (server and client copies of `EmbeddedData_EquipCXML.xml`/`Equip.xml`, `CustomItems.xml`, `EmbeddedData_PlayersCXML.xml`/`Players.xml`, `DoomedRealms.xml`): `<MultiHit/>` on the tiered swords (T0-T14), the Sword of Ascension and the Enthroned Sword (its Rift's description no longer says it alone pierces). Straight shots: Doctor Swordsworth (was `Parametric` at speed 10 over 4 s: now speed 100 over 400 ms, three shots 11.25° apart), the Arcane Rapier (was `Boomerang`: now 200 ms, the same 4-tile reach; its description changed), and `Wavy`/`Amplitude`/`Frequency` dropped from Henchman's Claymore, the Sword of the Colossus, the Keychain Cutlass, the Apocalypse Feather, the Sword of the Rainbow's End, the Swashbuckler's Sickle (`2WarriorST0`) and the Unstable Anomaly (still a ring of ten); the admin-only Silex's Hammer and Admin Sword are left. Tiered helms add `Armored` on self (1 s T0-T2, 1.5 s T3-T5, 2 s T6-T8, and the Enthroned Helm 2 s); tiered shields add `Armored` on self (1.5/2/2.5 s by the same tiers) and `Exposed` 3 s beside each `Stunned` (on the shot and the item's line); tiered seals add a `HealNova` at the seal's range, 60 + 15 per tier, raised by wisdom. Caps: Knight Attack 50 to 60, Paladin HpRegen 40 to 60, Pirate MaxHitPoints 700 to 770 and Defense 25 to 30.
- **Client:** `combat.rs` `frontline` and `frontline_class` (the numbers shown for hits and ground damage take the cut, `game.rs`); `damage_with_defense` now does Armored as the server does (×2 defense, ahead of armor piercing and Armor Broken; it was AS3's ×1.5 after them, so Armored numbers were shown too high); the new-character screen shows the passive under a sword class's description (`screens.rs`); a "Lucky!" notification is translated. Four new lines in every `app/lang/*.json`. Test tools: `bsk-bot` logs every `Notification`, and the client's `--screen class:0x<type>` opens a class's page (client-rs/AGENTS.md).
- **Docs:** CHANGELOG (Classes), `website/wiki/classes.md` (the Frontline section), `website/wiki/gear-statistics.md` (a note that the Knight's and the sword abilities' rows are out of date), SETUP-LOCAL. The gear and class pages follow from the XML.
- **Deploys:** client and world server (and the website for the wiki). Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes. Existing characters keep their stats: maxed Knights, Paladins and Pirates are no longer maxed in the raised stats until they drink more potions. An older client works with the new server, but shows hits on a sword class 15% higher than taken and Armored hits as before, until it updates.
- **Not done:** the Blademaster (dual blades) is left as it is. The Pirate's cannons are unchanged. Not tuned with real players yet: the 15%, 1.5% and 40 a second are constants in `Player.Frontline.cs` (and the cut in `combat.rs`).

### World server crash on disconnect, and coming back by itself

- **What happened:** on 2026-11-18 at 23:58 world server a (Europe 2) ended with "Collection was modified" from `UpdatedHashSet.RemoveWhere`, called from `ConnectionListener.Disconnect` on a socket thread: `Client.Reset` ran `Player.CleanupPlayerUpdate` there while the world thread was still ticking that player and adding to the same set. The exception escaped a socket callback, which ends the process; players online lost what they'd done since their last save. The proxy never replaces a dead world server, so nobody could connect until `bsk-world` was restarted by hand at 00:02.
- **What changed:** `Client.Reset` posts the cleanup to the player's world thread (`World.Post`, inline only when the world is gone). Both socket callbacks in `NetworkHandler.cs` catch anything left over, log it and disconnect only that client (`SocketCallbacks.Failed`). `ConnectionListener.Disconnect` still returns the client to the pool if its reset throws. `tools/world_proxy.py` starts a new world server (`rolling_restart.py up --no-build`, in the same slot from its last build) when the active one's process has been gone for 5 s and no restart or deploy runs, at most once per 5 minutes. SETUP-LOCAL and source/AGENTS.md describe it.
- **Deploys:** world server and the world proxy (`tools/`). The proxy is the `bsk-world` service itself: the new `world_proxy.py` only runs after `bsk-world` restarts, which kicks everyone, so it waits for the owner's next restart of it (or a quiet moment). The world server part rolls as usual. Nobody is disconnected by the deploy; no Redis changes.

### Ascendant forcefields and Father wards stack

- **What changed:** `Player.Forcefield.cs` keeps every field a player stands in by the field's id (`Shield(fieldId, ...)`, called by `Forcefield.Tick`) and takes `StackedShield` of them off each hit: sorted strongest first, each value shifted right by its place (full, half, quarter..., rounded down), capped at 90. The flare's colour is the strongest field's. `Player.Ward.cs` `GiveWard` keeps the wards raised while one is up (`_wardLayers`, cleared once it breaks or fades, each dropped when its time is up) and sets the ward to `Ward + Stacked(after) - Stacked(before)`, at least the new amount and at most the stack's total; `WardMax` follows, the colour, burst and giver are the strongest's, and its time runs to the latest. The client's damage numbers stack the same way (`combat::stacked_shield`, `game.rs`). New `source/Tests/AbilityStackingTests.cs`. Docs: CHANGELOG (Classes), `website/wiki/classes.md`, SETUP-LOCAL.
- **Why the half-per-layer is geometric:** "each extra at half" read as a flat 50% would let one Father with an 8 s ward and a 4 s cooldown, or a crowd of Ascendants, pile up without end; halving each further layer keeps a stack under twice its strongest, as `StatBoostSelf` already does.
- **Deploys:** world server and client (the shown numbers), website. Nobody is disconnected; no Redis changes. An older client shows hits in stacked forcefields a little higher than taken, until it updates.
- **Not done:** not tried in game; a single Ascendant's own overlapping halos stack too, as do a single Father's recasts while his last ward lasts (a T8 censer's 8 s against its 4 s cooldown keeps him at up to 1.5 times his ward).

### The Gold Store

- **What:** buying gold with PayPal (SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Gold store"). Account server: `App/Controllers/GoldController.cs` (`gold/store`, `signin`, `me`, `signout`, `order`, `capture`, `paypal-webhook`; JSON), `App/PayPal.cs` (Orders API v2 with a cached token and a `PayPal-Request-Id` on every create and capture), `CoreService.PayPal`. Shared: `PayPalSettings.cs` (`serverSettings.paypal`, or `BSK_PAYPAL_*`), `database/GoldStore.cs` (orders, store sessions, 12 orders an hour, `Credit` and `TakeBack` as Lua scripts so an order pays once), `isc/data/GoldMsg.cs` and `Channel.Gold`. Prices: `GoldStore.DefaultPacks` from `GoldStore.Pack(id, dollars, bonus %)` at `GoldPerUnit` 100 gold a dollar, the currency `USD` by default. World server: `core/GoldNews.cs` refreshes an online player's gold, floats "+N Gold" and says thank you. The price is the server's: the page only names a pack; a capture is paid in only when PayPal's own answer matches the order's amount, currency and `custom_id` (account and pack). The webhook's body is never trusted: it names an order or capture, which is looked up at PayPal. Refunds and chargebacks take back what's left of the gold. Website: `gold.html`, `gold.js`, the styles at the end of `style.css`, `coin` and `coins` sprites in `site.js` (`window.ddSprite`), Gold in the menus of `index.html`, `changelog.html` and the wiki (`tools/build_wiki.py`); `wiki/items.md` "The shops". Deploy: `deploy.py` puts `deploy/paypal.local.json` into the account server's `server.json` (logging only the mode: the Actions logs are public), `install.sh` keeps the installed keys when a deploy has none and makes `server.json` mode 600, `ci_setup.py` and `deploy.yml` take a `PAYPAL_CONFIG` secret; `deploy/paypal.example.json`. Testing: `tools/testclient/paypal_mock.py` (a PayPal stand-in), `gold_store_test.py` (the flow end to end), `iso_stack.py start --paypal URL`, `source/Tests/GoldStoreTests.cs`.
- **Deploys:** account server, world server (the `Gold` channel; without it the gold still arrives, shown at the next sign-in or purchase) and website. Nobody is disconnected; the account server restart pauses sign-ins for seconds. No client change: the gold arrives as the existing gold stat.
- **Redis:** new keys only (`gold.*`); nothing migrated.
- **The owner must:** make a PayPal Business account for the address to be paid and a REST app on developer.paypal.com; fill `deploy/paypal.local.json` in the main checkout (and the `PAYPAL_CONFIG` secret for deploys from GitHub), `sandbox` first; add the webhook (`https://play.dodmg.com/api/gold/paypal-webhook`, five events, in SETUP-LOCAL); deploy, buy a pack in sandbox with a sandbox buyer, then switch to `live`. Without keys the page says the store is closed, so this release can ship before PayPal is set up, but then hold back the Gold Store line in the changelog.
- **Not done:** a Buy Gold button in the game client (it would link to the page); partial refunds are only logged; the packs are the defaults ($1 = 100 gold, bonuses 5-25%) until the owner sets their own in `paypal.local.json`.
