Release 2.4.6: Elder bosses rise again, half as many pets, faster browser play and steady sound (deploys client, servers, gateway and website; nobody is disconnected)

## For players

- **Elder dungeons:** every Elder boss (19: Dreadstump to the Elder Ivory Wyvern, the Tomb's Bes, Nut and Geb and Draconis' four dragons) leaves its remains for 75 seconds, circled by souls that call out by name anyone nearby carrying a Necromantic Relic (new Ethereal drop of those bosses, 3% each player past 2% of the damage, tradeable). Used within 3 tiles, the relic raises the boss again, Empowered: a fifth bigger, violet, 2.5x health, +10 defense, shots 30% harder, a quarter faster (more below 30% health), with soul novas, Wailing Souls and a soul storm, twice the loot chances, its Elder loot rolled again and one of 19 new untiered items, each from its slot's top tier at 0.9-1.5x and with a power of its own.
- **Browser:** playing at play.dodmg.com picks up the settings and saved sign-in a browser kept at dodmg.com/play before the move (2.4.4), once, by itself. The browser game downloads less and starts sooner: a first visit goes from about 7.8 MB to 6.2 MB (brotli), an update downloads only the files that changed instead of the whole game again, sounds and music stay in the browser instead of downloading on every visit, and returning visits reuse the browser's compiled copy of the game.
- **Pets:** every pet drops half as often as before, wherever it drops (realm, godlands, events, dungeons, Doomed monsters; the Arena's pets are halved again on top of its own halving). A Lil' Oryx from Oryx goes from 1% to 0.5%, a realm critter from 0.0375-0.125% to 0.019-0.0625%. The wiki shows the new chances.
- **Game:** sound stays steady in big fights: a volley hitting many monsters is one hit sound, at most 3 copies of a sound and 16 effects play at once, and the mix is limited instead of clipping, so it no longer swings between too loud and too quiet.

## Details

### Boss resurrection: remains, the Necromantic Relic and the Empowered bosses

- The design doc "BRM Boss Resurrection Mechanic", first pass on the Elder dungeons' bosses.
- `tools/build_resurrection.py` (new) generates everything: `Resurrection.xml` (server and client copies: the relic, soul bolt, the ritual's soul, the Wailing Soul, each boss's `Remains` (its art, `<Corpse/>`, a `Souls` effect, the ritual sounds) and `Empowered` form (a copy of the boss, 1.2x size, soul bolts as projectiles 90/91), the 19 UTs), `resources/data/resurrection.json` (per boss: names, soul damage, the Empowered loot from `wiki_data.drops`), `Resurrection8x8.png` and `16x16.png` (the relic after the doc's art, the UTs recoloured from their bases), `sfx/monster/necromantic_chant.mp3` and `necromantic_blast.mp3` (synthesised, `--sounds`, ffmpeg). Type codes 0x8C00-0x8CFF, kept in `tools/resurrection/types.json`. `tools/gen_rs_manifest.py` registers the sheets and XML; manifest regenerated.
- Server: `core/resurrection/BossResurrection.cs` (reads the JSON, gives each Empowered form its Elder boss's behaviour and loot plus its own, the relic drop in `Loot.ExtraLootTables`), `BossRemains.cs` (the remains: call-outs to relic holders every 7 s, ambient lines, 75 s then they scatter; the ritual: souls gathering, soul-fire lightning, chant, then a blast and the boss), `Empowered.cs` (health x2.5 reapplied when a behaviour like BossStats sets it, +10 def, violet glow, 2.5 s rise untouchable and still, then Soul Nova, Wailing Souls (6 at most, health and damage by the boss), Soul Storm below 60%, enraged below 30%; on its death its souls go). `Entity.BehaviourTime`/`BehaviourPaused` (its behaviour runs 25%/45% faster, paused while it rises), `Enemy.DamageScale` now multiplies by the Empowered bonus and `DoomedRuntime.Fire` applies it (Doomed-engine bosses ignored DamageScale before), `Enemy.LootBoost` adds to every player's boost. `ActivateEffects.Resurrect`: refused (not spent) away from remains. `BehaviorDb.Resurrection.cs`: the Wailing Soul.
- UT powers: `Item.RelicPower` (`<RelicPower>`), `Player.RelicPowers.cs`: weapons on their shots' hits (Broadside, Venom, Stone Gaze, Hellbrand, Bloodthirst, Ignition), abilities on use (Serpent's Wind, Soul Harvest, Phase, Stormcall, Undertow, Guardian's Oath, Starlight, Earthshaker, Wyvern's Fury), armours when shot (Masquerade, Toxic Scales, Obsidian Shell), the ring on its own (Davy's Bargain). Their extra hits go through `Enemy.Damage` and never set off another power. Tooltips and wiki cards tell each power (`ExtraTooltipData`, `wiki_gear.py`).
- Client: `particles.rs` `Souls` effect (wisps circling, drifting up and out, fading), `view.rs` draws `<Corpse/>` objects lying on their side, grey with a pulsing soul glow (`ObjectDesc.corpse`). No new text to translate (what the souls and bosses say is server speech).
- Wiki: `wiki_data.py` reads `resurrection.json` (the relic on each Elder boss, the Empowered bosses' drops, so the UTs show who drops them), a page of its own, `website/wiki/necromantic-relic.md` ("Raising an Elder boss: the Necromantic Relic", a sub-page of Dungeons, which now heads a `Dungeons` group; Dungeons keeps a short part pointing to it): getting a relic, the remains, the souls' call, the ritual, the Empowered form, the loot, then a part per boss from `{{gen:resurrection}}` (`build_wiki.py`: its health against the Elder boss's, soul attack damage, Wailing Souls' health, notes for the dragons, the Tomb and the Wyvern, its item with the base, strength, stats and power, and its second-roll drops). The numbers come from `resurrection.json` (which now names each boss's item, base and strength) and `Empowered.cs`'s constants, so they follow the game.
- Redeploy: servers (world and account server: new XML and item data), the client (new sheets, XML, effect, corpse drawing) and the website. Nobody is disconnected. No Redis changes; new items only.
- Not done or off: other bosses than the Elder dungeons' (the design asks for all main dungeon and end-game bosses later); the dragons and the Wyvern turn into their loot balloons as before, whose own loot isn't boosted (the Empowered form's own drops are); an Empowered Tomb boss fights without the artifacts that orbit the regular ones. Old clients see the remains standing and no soul wisps.

### Retiring the old machine's game services

- `deploy/remote/install-site.sh` `--retire-game`: `systemctl mask` refuses while a unit's own file is in `/etc/systemd/system`, where install.sh put bsk-redis, bsk-account and the backup units, so on 2026-11-07 those were only disabled (the gateway instances were masked), and the backup timer, disabled without `--now`, was still running: it could have backed up the old machine's stale Redis beside the game machine's backups (it hadn't run since the move). The units' files now move to `/opt/bsk/retired-units` first, then everything is masked, `bsk-backup.service` included. Each deploy says so when one of them is still installed and not masked.
- Done by hand on dodmg.com (169.58.244.146) the same way: all seven units masked, the backup timer stopped; caddy, bsk-discord, bsk-redis-tunnel and Testing's bsk-world untouched.
- SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Two machines": says where the files go.
- Redeploy: nothing needed (the website machine already has it by hand; the next deploy brings the script). Players notice nothing.

### Browser settings carried over from dodmg.com

- Since 2.4.4 browser play is at play.dodmg.com, and browsers keep saved data per address, so players found their settings (and remembered login) gone.
- `website/storage-handover.html` (new, on dodmg.com): in a frame, it posts this site's `betterSkillys-rs.` keys to `https://play.<its host>` only (postMessage's target origin: no other page gets them). `noindex`, and `tools/build_site.py` leaves such pages out of the sitemap.
- `client-rs/web/index.html`: before the game starts (the wasm download runs meanwhile), the first load on an https address with a parent domain loads `https://<parent>/storage-handover.html` in a hidden frame, waits up to 3 s for its message from that origin and frame, and stores what it sent: the settings replace any made here since the move (what players asked for), the login and guest keys only where there's none here. `betterSkillys-rs.handover` marks it done (not set when no answer came, so it tries again next time). Same site (dodmg.com), so the frame sees the first-party storage in Chrome, Firefox and Safari.
- Redeploy: the gateway (the web client) and the website. Nobody is disconnected.

### Browser downloads: per-file versions, brotli, cached sounds

- Every file the page and the game loaded carried the same `?v=<version>-<commit>`, so each release made every browser player download everything again (wasm 2.67 MB gzipped plus about 5.1 MB of art and data); sounds and music came through the gateway's `/api` with no caching headers at all (the gateway dropped the account server's `ETag`/`Last-Modified`), so they were downloaded again on every visit.
- `tools/build_client_rs.py`: each asset's sha-256 (16 hex digits) goes into the built `assets/manifest.json` as `versions`; the page gets the module's, the wasm's and the manifest's (`BSK_JS_VERSION`, `BSK_WASM_VERSION`, `BSK_MANIFEST_VERSION`, replacing `BSK_BUILD`). `.obj` is compressed now. Brotli copies (`.br`, quality 11, window 24) are written by a new `bsk-publish brotli --dir --ext` (the `brotli` crate, threaded; Python has no brotli built in), so the CI deploy needs nothing new.
- `bsk-assets` (`source.rs`, `manifest.rs`): `Manifest.versions` (default empty); in the browser the manifest is asked for with the page's `window.bskManifestVersion` and each file with its own hash, falling back to the build's `?v=` when there's none (desktop and older layouts unchanged).
- `client-rs/web/index.html`: `modulepreload` for the module and `preload` for the wasm, so both start at once; the wasm's `Response` goes to the game as fetched, and the loading bar counts a `clone()` of it, so Chrome's wasm code cache (which needs the browser's own response) works and a returning visit skips compiling.
- Gateway: `ServeDir` with `precompressed_br()` before gzip; `/api/sfx/` and `/api/music/` answers get `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400`, the account server's `ETag` and `Last-Modified` come back, and `If-None-Match`/`If-Modified-Since` go to it (a 304 when unchanged).
- Redeploy: the gateway and its web client (one deploy; nobody is disconnected). A new page with an old gateway still works (gzip, no sound caching), and so does an old page with a new gateway. The first load after the deploy downloads everything once (new URLs); after that only what changes.
- Not done: the wasm changes every release anyway (the commit is built into it); `wasm-opt` isn't installed here or on CI, so the module isn't run through it; the sounds aren't hashed (the account server's files, not the build's), hence the day's caching.

### Steady sound in big fights

- Every hit, death and shot sound started its own voice with nothing to stop them stacking: with many monsters, dozens overlapped, summed past full scale and clipped (the "loud, then quiet" players heard), and each one decoded its MP3 on the audio thread, which could also make it stutter.
- `client-rs/app/src/audio.rs`: effects are decoded once when they arrive and kept as samples; they play through one mixer of their own behind a limiter (`LimitSettings::gaming()`, -3 dBFS); `Voices` drops the same effect again within 45 ms, a 4th copy of one effect still playing, and anything past 16 effects at once. Music is unchanged (its own path, decoded as it plays).
- Redeploy: the client (desktop and the gateway's web client). Nobody is disconnected.

### Pets drop half as often

- `source/WorldServer/logic/loot/Loots.cs`: `Loot.PetDropRate` (0.5) multiplies the chance of any item with a PermaPet activate (a pet's generator) in `Loot.Handle`, both the per-player roll and the OnePlayerLoot roll, everywhere; in the arenas `ArenaPools.PetRate` (0.5) still applies on top, as before. Every monster's drops go through `Loot.Handle` (Doomed-ported ones too, via `DoomedSetup.Drops`), so no behaviour file or generated file changed. Mystery boxes, the death spin, shops and the Pen Keeper are not drops and are unchanged.
- `tools/wiki_data.py`: `drops()` applies `PetDropRate` (read from Loots.cs) to pets' chances, so the Pets guide and gear pages show what really drops.
- SETUP-LOCAL.md, "Pets: single use, drops and attacks": notes the rate.
- Redeploy: the world server (rolling, nobody is disconnected) and the website (wiki). No Redis data changes.
